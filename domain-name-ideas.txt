HCP Domain Name Ideas
======================

Trail/Hare themed
1. HareTrail       - haretrail.com
2. ChalkTalk       - chalktalk.app
3. OnOnHQ          - ononhq.com (from the classic "On-On!" hasher call)
4. HashTrail       - hashtrail.com
5. TrueTrail       - truetrail.app
6. FalseTrail      - falsetrail.com (playful, self-aware)
7. ShaggyPack      - shaggypack.com

Kennel/Community themed
8. KennelKeep      - kennelkeep.com
9. PackCircle      - packcircle.app
10. MismanagementHQ - mismgmt.com (in-joke name for hash leadership)
11. HashKennel     - hashkennel.com
12. CircleUp       - circleup.app (check availability/trademark first)

Beer/Fun themed
13. BeerCheck      - beercheck.app
14. DownDownApp    - downdown.app (the post-run tradition)
15. HashFlash      - hashflash.com
16. ShiggyLog      - shiggylog.com ("shiggy" = hasher slang for messy trail)
17. RedDressHQ     - playful nod to Red Dress Runs, though maybe too event-specific

Passport/Journey themed
18. HashPassport   - hashpassport.com
19. HarrierLog     - harrierlog.com
20. RunCapsule     - runcapsule.app (matches the platform's "Run Capsule" feature name directly)

Notes:
- HashPassport, RunCapsule, and OnOnHQ lean most directly into actual feature names and Hash vocabulary.
- Check trademark/availability before committing.
- Avoid names too close to existing hashing org sites (hashhouseharriers.com, world hash sites) to sidestep confusion or legal friction.
