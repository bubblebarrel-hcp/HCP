# Purpose

Predictive Community Intelligence assists leaders by identifying emerging patterns, potential risks, and future opportunities based on historical evidence and current community activity.

Predictions support planning and preparedness while remaining transparent, explainable, and advisory.

---

# Vision

Notice early.

Prepare thoughtfully.

Strengthen communities.

---

# Philosophy

Predictions inform decisions.

They do not make decisions.

Human judgment remains essential.

---

# Experience Principles

- Explain every prediction.
- Respect uncertainty.
- Prioritize prevention.
- Surface opportunities.
- Preserve trust.
- Encourage action.
- Learn continuously.

---

# Predictive Insights

AI may identify trends relating to:

- Volunteer participation
- Membership growth
- Event attendance
- Leadership succession
- Committee activity
- Recognition frequency
- Historical preservation
- Organizational resilience

---

# Community Health Indicators

Indicators may include:

- Participation consistency
- Volunteer sustainability
- Leadership continuity
- Member retention
- Onboarding effectiveness
- Knowledge preservation
- Community engagement
- Event readiness

Indicators are directional rather than absolute.

---

# Opportunity Detection

AI may recommend:

- Mentor recruitment
- Volunteer appreciation campaigns
- Historical digitization projects
- Leadership development
- Cross-chapter collaboration
- Community outreach initiatives

Recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Risk Awareness

Potential risks include:

- Leadership burnout
- Volunteer shortages
- Declining participation
- Documentation gaps
- Organizational dependency on key individuals
- Event planning bottlenecks

Alerts provide supporting evidence.

---

# Explainability

Every prediction explains:

- Supporting observations
- Historical comparisons
- Confidence level
- Contributing factors
- Alternative explanations

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-021

Predictions shall remain advisory.

---

## BR-AI-022

Prediction confidence shall be visible.

---

## BR-AI-023

Historical evidence shall accompany predictive insights.

---

## BR-AI-024

Predictions shall never replace governance processes.

---

## BR-AI-025

Community health shall emphasize resilience rather than competition.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Predictive Intelligence succeeds when leaders can:

- Recognize trends early.
- Understand supporting evidence.
- Prepare appropriate responses.
- Strengthen organizational resilience.
- Improve long-term planning.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Predictive model is documented.
- Community health philosophy is defined.
- Risk-awareness framework is established.
