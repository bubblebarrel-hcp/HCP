# Purpose

This document defines the architectural principles that enable HCP's AI capabilities to evolve independently of underlying models, vendors, and technologies while preserving community knowledge, governance, and trust.

---

# Vision

Future-ready intelligence.

Stable foundations.

Community knowledge first.

---

# Philosophy

Community knowledge is permanent.

AI implementations are temporary.

Architecture should separate the two.

---

# Architectural Principles

- Model independence.
- Vendor neutrality.
- Knowledge portability.
- Explainability by design.
- Observable intelligence.
- Modular capabilities.
- Graceful evolution.

---

# AI Platform Layers

The AI Platform consists of:

## Community Knowledge Layer

- Documents
- Run Capsules
- Governance records
- Historical archives
- Evidence
- Community graph
- Organizational memory

---

## Intelligence Layer

- Retrieval
- Semantic search
- Recommendation engine
- Narrative generation
- Prediction engine
- Planning assistance
- Translation
- Classification

---

## Model Layer

Supports multiple AI providers and models.

Capabilities may include:

- Large Language Models
- Small Language Models
- Vision Models
- Speech Models
- Embedding Models
- Future AI paradigms

Models remain interchangeable.

---

## Governance Layer

Responsible for:

- Explainability
- Audit logging
- Permissions
- Privacy
- Trust scoring
- Human review
- Policy enforcement

---

## Experience Layer

Delivers AI assistance through:

- Member Assistant
- Leadership Assistant
- Search
- Dashboards
- Mobile
- APIs
- Future interfaces

---

# Multi-Model Strategy

Different AI models may specialize in:

- Search
- Translation
- Summarization
- Image understanding
- Historical analysis
- Planning assistance

The platform routes tasks to the most appropriate capability.

---

# Vendor Independence

No single AI provider becomes a permanent dependency.

The platform shall support:

- Provider replacement
- Model comparison
- Controlled experimentation
- Incremental upgrades
- Hybrid deployments

---

# Continuous Evaluation

The platform continuously measures:

- Recommendation quality
- Hallucination rate
- Evidence alignment
- User satisfaction
- Fairness
- Latency
- Cost efficiency

Evaluation informs platform evolution.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-031

Community knowledge remains independent of AI providers.

---

## BR-AI-032

AI capabilities shall evolve without disrupting community workflows.

---

## BR-AI-033

Platform architecture shall support multiple intelligence providers.

---

## BR-AI-034

Trust metrics shall remain observable.

---

## BR-AI-035

Future AI capabilities shall inherit existing governance controls.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The AI Platform succeeds when:

- Models can evolve independently.
- Community knowledge remains portable.
- Governance remains consistent.
- New capabilities integrate safely.
- Users experience continuous improvement.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- AI architecture is documented.
- Future evolution strategy is defined.
- Vendor independence is established.
