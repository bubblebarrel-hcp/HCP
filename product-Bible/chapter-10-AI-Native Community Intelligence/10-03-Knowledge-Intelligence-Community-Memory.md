# Purpose

The Knowledge Intelligence Platform transforms community knowledge into a searchable, explainable, and continuously growing institutional memory.

Its purpose is to help members rediscover information, connect historical knowledge, and preserve organizational wisdom across generations.

---

# Vision

Never lose knowledge.

Never repeat avoidable mistakes.

Always learn from history.

---

# Philosophy

Knowledge grows through contribution.

Wisdom grows through rediscovery.

---

# Experience Principles

- Search by meaning.
- Preserve historical context.
- Connect related knowledge.
- Explain discoveries.
- Respect evidence.
- Encourage contribution.
- Keep knowledge alive.

---

# Knowledge Sources

Knowledge may originate from:

- Run Capsules
- Event reports
- Meeting minutes
- Constitutions
- Governance decisions
- Volunteer documentation
- Community discussions
- Historical archives
- Photos
- Videos
- Oral histories
- Recognition records
- Training materials

---

# Semantic Search

Members may search using natural language.

Examples:

- "When did our chapter first host Interhash?"
- "Show flood relief events."
- "Find Trail Master training."
- "Who organized the 2015 anniversary?"
- "How have we welcomed first-time Hashers?"

Search understands concepts, not just keywords.

---

# Knowledge Graph

The platform connects:

- People
- Events
- Organizations
- Places
- Documents
- Awards
- Committees
- Historical milestones

Relationships become discoverable.

---

# AI Knowledge Assistance

AI may:

- Summarize long documents
- Explain historical context
- Connect related materials
- Identify missing knowledge
- Recommend further reading
- Suggest archival improvements

---

# Knowledge Confidence

Results indicate:

- Source documents
- Evidence quality
- Historical confidence
- Verification status
- Last review date

Confidence remains transparent.

# Business Rules

Knowledge discoveries shall remain traceable to original sources.

## BR-AI-012

Historical uncertainty shall be clearly communicated.

## BR-AI-013

---

## BR-AI-014

---

## BR-AI-015

Historical preservation shall remain a continuous process.

# Acceptance Criteria

Knowledge Intelligence succeeds when members can:

- Find information naturally.
- Understand historical context.
- Discover related knowledge.
- Trust search results.
- Build upon previous generations.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Knowledge architecture is documented.
- Semantic search philosophy is defined.
- Community memory model is established.
