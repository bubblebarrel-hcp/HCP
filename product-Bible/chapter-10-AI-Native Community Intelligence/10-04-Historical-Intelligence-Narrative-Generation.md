# Purpose

The Historical Intelligence Platform transforms verified community history into meaningful narratives that educate, inspire, and preserve institutional identity.

AI assists in organizing, connecting, and presenting history while respecting evidence, uncertainty, and human editorial oversight.

---

# Vision

Preserve every generation.

Connect every story.

Keep community history alive.

---

# Philosophy

History is more than dates.

History is people, places, traditions, challenges, and shared experiences.

AI helps communities rediscover their own story.

---

# Experience Principles

- Preserve authenticity.
- Respect evidence.
- Explain uncertainty.
- Connect generations.
- Celebrate community identity.
- Encourage historical contribution.
- Keep humans in editorial control.

---

# Historical Narratives

AI may generate:

- Chapter histories
- Anniversary retrospectives
- Leadership timelines
- Event stories
- Volunteer histories
- Tradition histories
- Organization biographies
- Community milestone summaries

Generated narratives remain editable.

---

# Historical Connections

The platform identifies relationships between:

- Events
- Leaders
- Organizations
- Places
- Traditions
- Photos
- Awards
- Run Capsules
- Oral histories

Connections help reveal historical patterns.

---

# Narrative Evidence

Each narrative links to:

- Source documents
- Photos
- Videos
- Passport records
- Governance documents
- Community contributions
- Confidence indicators

Narratives remain traceable.

---

# Historical Uncertainty

Where evidence conflicts, the platform:

- Explains differing accounts
- Identifies missing evidence
- Highlights confidence levels
- Invites community review

Historical honesty strengthens trust.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- Drafting anniversary books
- Summarizing decades of activity
- Creating exhibit timelines
- Suggesting preservation priorities
- Connecting forgotten stories
- Identifying historical gaps

Editorial approval remains human.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-016

Historical narratives shall remain evidence-backed.

---

## BR-AI-017

AI-generated history shall remain distinguishable from verified historical documents.

---

## BR-AI-018

Conflicting evidence shall remain visible rather than hidden.

---

## BR-AI-019

Communities retain editorial ownership of their history.

---

## BR-AI-020

Narrative generation shall encourage additional preservation efforts.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Historical Intelligence succeeds when members can:

- Understand community history.
- Explore historical relationships.
- Verify narrative sources.
- Discover forgotten stories.
- Contribute to preservation.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Historical narrative philosophy is documented.
- Narrative generation model is defined.
- Editorial governance is established.
