# Purpose

The Member AI Assistant provides personalized, context-aware assistance that helps members navigate community life, discover opportunities, preserve memories, and contribute more effectively.

The assistant enhances participation while respecting member autonomy, privacy, and organizational governance.

---

# Vision

Know the member.

Understand the context.

Support meaningful participation.

---

# Philosophy

Assist without distracting.

Recommend without manipulating.

Remember with permission.

---

# Experience Principles

- Context-aware assistance.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Respect for privacy.
- Minimal interruption.
- Encourage real-world participation.
- Preserve member agency.
- Learn responsibly.

---

# Core Capabilities

The assistant may:

- Answer community questions
- Explain governance processes
- Recommend events
- Suggest volunteer opportunities
- Find historical records
- Draft announcements
- Summarize discussions
- Translate community content
- Assist with onboarding

Recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Personal Intelligence

The assistant may understand:

- Membership history
- Current organization
- Upcoming commitments
- Passport milestones
- Volunteer interests
- Preferred communication style
- Accessibility preferences
- Language preferences

Personalization occurs only within approved privacy settings.

---

# Community Guidance

The assistant may suggest:

- Members to welcome
- Events to attend
- Committees seeking volunteers
- Historical content of interest
- Mentorship opportunities
- Recognition candidates

Suggestions prioritize community benefit.

---

# Memory Assistance

With member permission, the assistant may:

- Surface past memories
- Organize albums
- Draft yearly reflections
- Suggest timeline entries
- Identify missing historical information
- Prepare annual summaries

Members remain in control of personal memories.

---

# Explainability

Recommendations should explain:

- Why this suggestion was made
- Which information influenced it
- Whether AI confidence is high, medium, or low
- Available supporting evidence

---

# Privacy

Members control:

- Personalization level
- Memory usage
- AI learning preferences
- Recommendation categories
- Data retention for AI features

Controls are available at any time.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-001

The assistant shall prioritize community participation over prolonged AI interaction.

---

## BR-AI-002

Personal recommendations shall remain explainable.

---

## BR-AI-003

Members may disable personalization without losing core platform functionality.

---

## BR-AI-004

AI shall not impersonate community leaders or members.

---

## BR-AI-005

AI-generated content shall be identifiable where appropriate.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Member AI Assistant succeeds when members can:

- Find information quickly.
- Participate more confidently.
- Discover meaningful opportunities.
- Preserve personal memories.
- Understand community processes.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Assistant capabilities are defined.
- Personal intelligence model is documented.
- Privacy controls are established.
