# Purpose

This document establishes the ethical framework, governance model, accountability requirements, and operational safeguards governing all AI capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its purpose is to ensure AI remains transparent, trustworthy, accountable, and aligned with the community's values.

---

# Vision

Build trust.

Protect people.

Strengthen communities.

---

# Philosophy

AI serves the community.

The community does not serve the AI.

Trust is earned continuously through transparency, accountability, and responsible behavior.

---

# Core Principles

- Human authority.
- Explainability.
- Transparency.
- Privacy.
- Fairness.
- Accountability.
- Safety.
- Continuous oversight.

---

# Human Authority

AI shall not independently:

- Elect leaders
- Grant awards
- Suspend members
- Amend constitutions
- Approve finances
- Modify governance records
- Override human decisions

AI recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Transparency

Members shall know:

- When AI was involved
- What AI generated
- Why recommendations were made
- Which evidence informed responses
- What confidence level applies

AI participation remains visible.

---

# Accountability

Every significant AI interaction records:

- Timestamp
- Model version
- Source evidence
- Confidence
- Human reviewer (where applicable)
- Final outcome

AI actions remain auditable.

---

# Fairness

The platform continuously evaluates:

- Recommendation bias
- Geographic bias
- Language bias
- Participation bias
- Historical bias
- Data quality limitations

Potential bias is documented and reviewed.

---

# Privacy

AI respects:

- Member consent
- Data minimization
- Access controls
- Organizational boundaries
- Applicable legal requirements

Privacy remains foundational.

---

# Safety

The platform shall include safeguards for:

- Harmful recommendations
- Hallucination detection
- Evidence verification
- Abuse prevention
- Prompt injection resistance
- Unauthorized automation

Safety evolves continuously.

---

# Appeals & Review

Members may:

- Question AI recommendations
- Report incorrect information
- Request human review
- Correct historical inaccuracies
- Appeal AI-assisted decisions

Human review remains available.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The platform regularly evaluates:

- Recommendation quality
- User trust
- False positives
- False negatives
- Community feedback
- Governance compliance

Improvement remains evidence-driven.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-026

AI shall remain subordinate to community governance.

---

## BR-AI-027

Every significant recommendation shall be explainable.

---

## BR-AI-028

AI-generated content shall be identifiable.

---

## BR-AI-029

Human review shall remain available for consequential outcomes.

---

## BR-AI-030

Community trust shall take precedence over automation.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Responsible Intelligence succeeds when members:

- Trust AI assistance.
- Understand recommendations.
- Can challenge outcomes.
- Retain meaningful control.
- Benefit from transparent automation.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Ethical framework is documented.
- Governance model is defined.
- Accountability requirements are established.
