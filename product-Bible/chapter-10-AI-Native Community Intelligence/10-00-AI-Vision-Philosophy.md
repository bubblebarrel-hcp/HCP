# Purpose

The AI-Native Community Intelligence Platform provides intelligent assistance across the Hash Community Platform (HCP) while preserving trust, transparency, human oversight, and institutional knowledge.

Its purpose is to reduce administrative burden, improve discoverability, strengthen historical preservation, and help communities make better-informed decisions.

AI is an assistant—not an authority.

---

# Vision

Help every member.

Support every leader.

Preserve every generation of knowledge.

---

# Philosophy

AI should amplify people.

AI should preserve context.

AI should remain accountable.

Humans remain responsible for important decisions.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Human-in-the-loop.
- Explainable AI.
- Evidence-aware reasoning.
- Privacy-respecting intelligence.
- Trust before automation.
- Bias awareness.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# Scope

The AI Platform supports:

- Member assistance
- Leadership assistance
- Knowledge discovery
- Search enhancement
- Historical storytelling
- Event planning assistance
- Operational recommendations
- Governance assistance
- Volunteer recommendations
- Community analytics

---

# AI Capabilities

The platform may:

- Summarize
- Explain
- Recommend
- Organize
- Translate
- Draft
- Classify
- Discover patterns
- Surface historical connections

AI shall not independently exercise governance authority.

---

# Human Oversight

Human review is required for:

- Awards
- Governance decisions
- Constitutional changes
- Financial approvals
- Member sanctions
- Historical corrections
- Organizational policy

AI recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Explainability

Every significant AI recommendation should answer:

- Why was this suggested?
- Which information was considered?
- How confident is the recommendation?
- What evidence supports it?
- What assumptions were made?

---

# Trust

AI interactions integrate with:

- Evidence Records
- Audit Platform
- Operations
- Security
- Privacy
- Knowledge Platform

Trust remains measurable.

---

# Success Criteria

The AI Platform succeeds when:

- Members save time.
- Leaders make better decisions.
- Community knowledge becomes easier to discover.
- Institutional memory grows stronger.
- Human judgment remains central.

---

# Business Principles

Assist responsibly.

Explain clearly.

Respect human judgment.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- AI philosophy is documented.
- Human oversight is defined.
- Explainability requirements are established.
