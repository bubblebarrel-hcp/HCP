# Purpose

The Leadership AI platform assists community leaders by reducing administrative workload, surfacing organizational insights, improving continuity, and supporting informed decision-making.

AI augments leadership without replacing human responsibility or governance.

---

# Vision

Support leaders.

Strengthen organizations.

Preserve institutional wisdom.

---

# Philosophy

Leaders should spend more time with people than paperwork.

AI handles preparation.

Humans make decisions.

---

# Experience Principles

- Decision support.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Operational clarity.
- Respect volunteer time.
- Institutional continuity.
- Evidence-first reasoning.
- Human accountability.

---

# Leadership Assistance

The platform may assist with:

- Meeting preparation
- Agenda generation
- Action item tracking
- Volunteer coordination
- Event planning
- Membership summaries
- Governance reminders
- Organization health reports

---

# Organizational Intelligence

AI may analyze:

- Participation trends
- Volunteer engagement
- Event attendance
- Leadership continuity
- Committee activity
- Recognition patterns
- Historical participation
- Knowledge gaps

Analysis supports discussion rather than automatic action.

---

# Decision Support

AI may recommend:

- Volunteers for open roles
- Potential mentors
- Recognition candidates
- Event scheduling improvements
- Committee staffing
- Training opportunities

Recommendations remain advisory and explainable.

---

# Leadership Continuity

AI assists by:

- Summarizing outgoing leadership activities
- Preparing transition briefings
- Highlighting unfinished initiatives
- Preserving institutional knowledge
- Identifying historical precedents

---

# Risk Awareness

The platform may identify:

- Declining volunteer participation
- Event planning risks
- Governance deadlines
- Documentation gaps
- Historical preservation needs
- Organizational bottlenecks

Alerts remain informational.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation explains:

- Why it was generated
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence level
- Alternative interpretations
- Relevant historical examples

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-006

Leadership recommendations remain advisory.

---

## BR-AI-007

AI shall not replace constitutional governance processes.

---

## BR-AI-008

Recommendations shall remain evidence-backed.

---

## BR-AI-009

Leaders remain accountable for organizational decisions.

---

## BR-AI-010

Organizational intelligence shall prioritize long-term health over short-term optimization.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Leadership AI succeeds when leaders can:

- Prepare faster.
- Coordinate volunteers more effectively.
- Understand organizational trends.
- Preserve continuity.
- Make more informed decisions.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Leadership assistance is defined.
- Organizational intelligence is documented.
- Decision-support philosophy is established.
