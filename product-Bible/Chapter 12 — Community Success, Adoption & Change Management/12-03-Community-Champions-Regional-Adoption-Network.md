# Purpose

This document defines the Community Champion program and Regional Adoption Network that help communities learn from one another, accelerate adoption, and build long-term confidence in HCP.

The program recognizes that sustainable change is driven by trusted people within communities.

---

# Vision

Communities helping communities.

Leaders empowering leaders.

Success shared globally.

---

# Philosophy

The strongest advocates for HCP are community members who have experienced its value firsthand.

The platform should amplify their knowledge and encourage peer-to-peer support.

---

# Community Champion Principles

- Lead by example.
- Share practical experience.
- Encourage participation.
- Mentor with humility.
- Celebrate community success.
- Build trust through service.

---

# Community Champion Roles

Champions may:

- Welcome new communities.
- Share implementation experiences.
- Mentor chapter leaders.
- Host learning sessions.
- Recommend best practices.
- Provide peer guidance.
- Surface community feedback.
- Encourage continuous improvement.

---

# Regional Adoption Network

The Regional Adoption Network supports:

- Regional coordinators.
- Community champions.
- Experienced administrators.
- Volunteer trainers.
- Preservation advocates.
- Accessibility ambassadors.
- Technical mentors.

Regional collaboration is encouraged.

---

# Recognition

Champions may receive recognition for:

- Mentoring communities.
- Training leaders.
- Preserving knowledge.
- Supporting successful adoption.
- Contributing educational resources.
- Strengthening regional collaboration.

Recognition emphasizes service.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CS-016

Community Champions shall remain volunteers unless otherwise designated.

---

## BR-CS-017

Participation shall remain open and inclusive.

---

## BR-CS-018

Regional collaboration shall respect organizational governance.

---

## BR-CS-019

Recognition shall emphasize contribution over status.

---

## BR-CS-020

Champion feedback shall inform platform evolution.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Champion Program succeeds when:

- New communities receive peer support.
- Regional collaboration increases.
- Adoption accelerates through shared experience.
- Leadership confidence grows.
- Communities actively help one another.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Champion framework is documented.
- Regional network is defined.
- Recognition philosophy is established.
