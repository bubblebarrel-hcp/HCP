# Purpose

This document defines the framework for developing capable leaders, confident volunteers, and knowledgeable community members through structured learning, certifications, mentorship, and continuous development within HCP.

Training is viewed as an ongoing journey rather than a one-time event.

---

# Vision

Empowered members.

Confident leaders.

Stronger communities.

---

# Philosophy

Communities thrive when knowledge is shared, leadership is developed, and experience is passed from one generation to the next.

The platform supports lifelong learning.

---

# Learning Principles

- Learn by participating.
- Teach through experience.
- Encourage mentorship.
- Recognize achievement.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Make learning accessible.

---

# Learning Paths

Learning journeys may include:

- New Member Orientation
- Volunteer Essentials
- Event Coordination
- Chapter Administration
- Community History & Preservation
- Leadership Development
- Governance Fundamentals
- AI Literacy
- Platform Administration
- Trainer Certification

---

# Certification Framework

Certifications may include:

- Participant
- Volunteer
- Coordinator
- Historian
- Chapter Administrator
- Leadership
- Trainer
- Community Steward

Certifications remain verifiable.

---

# Learning Resources

The platform supports:

- Interactive lessons
- Video learning
- Practical exercises
- Knowledge assessments
- Community discussions
- AI coaching
- Resource libraries
- Live workshops

Learning remains flexible.

---

# Mentorship

Members may:

- Request mentors.
- Offer mentorship.
- Track mentorship goals.
- Record mentoring milestones.
- Celebrate mentoring achievements.

Mentorship strengthens the community.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CS-011

Learning opportunities shall remain accessible.

---

## BR-CS-012

Certifications shall reflect demonstrated capability.

---

## BR-CS-013

Mentorship shall remain voluntary.

---

## BR-CS-014

Learning progress shall remain visible to the learner.

---

## BR-CS-015

Training content shall evolve with community needs.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Training succeeds when:

- Members grow confidently.
- Leaders feel prepared.
- Knowledge transfers effectively.
- Mentorship flourishes.
- Communities develop future leaders.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Learning framework is documented.
- Certification model is defined.
- Leadership development philosophy is established.
