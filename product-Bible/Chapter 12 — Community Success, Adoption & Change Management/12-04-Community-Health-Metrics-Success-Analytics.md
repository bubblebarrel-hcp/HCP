# Purpose

This document defines the principles, metrics, dashboards, and analytical frameworks used to evaluate community health, organizational resilience, adoption success, and long-term impact.

The objective is to provide meaningful insights that help communities improve rather than simply report activity.

---

# Vision

Meaningful insights.

Healthier communities.

Better decisions.

---

# Philosophy

Analytics exist to support community growth.

Measurements should encourage learning, reflection, and improvement rather than competition or surveillance.

---

# Measurement Principles

- Measure outcomes over activity.
- Respect privacy.
- Explain every metric.
- Encourage improvement.
- Avoid vanity metrics.
- Preserve historical context.
- Support evidence-based decisions.

---

# Community Health Domains

The platform evaluates:

- Participation
- Volunteer engagement
- Leadership continuity
- Historical preservation
- Governance effectiveness
- Member retention
- Knowledge sharing
- Community satisfaction
- Event quality
- Inclusion & accessibility

No single metric defines community health.

---

# Success Dashboards

Dashboards may include:

- Leadership health
- Volunteer trends
- Event outcomes
- Historical archive growth
- Passport participation
- Recognition activity
- AI adoption
- Learning progress
- Governance performance
- Community resilience

Insights remain contextual.

---

# Recommendations

Analytics may recommend:

- Leadership mentoring
- Volunteer recognition
- Historical preservation opportunities
- Governance reviews
- Training resources
- Accessibility improvements
- Community engagement initiatives

Recommendations support decision-making.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Community Analytics succeeds when leaders can:

- Understand community trends.
- Identify opportunities.
- Make informed decisions.
- Preserve historical context.
- Strengthen community resilience.

# Completion Criteria

- Dashboard philosophy is established.
