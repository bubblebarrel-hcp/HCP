# Purpose

This document defines the principles, processes, and support mechanisms for migrating communities from legacy systems to HCP while preserving institutional knowledge, minimizing disruption, and supporting organizational change.

Migration is treated as both a technical and human process.

---

# Vision

Smooth transitions.

Preserved history.

Confident communities.

---

# Philosophy

Communities should never feel that adopting HCP requires abandoning their history or disrupting the relationships and traditions that define them.

Migration should preserve continuity while enabling future growth.

---

# Migration Principles

- Preserve before transforming.
- Validate before replacing.
- Progress incrementally.
- Minimize disruption.
- Respect community traditions.
- Support human change.
- Maintain transparency.

---

# Legacy Sources

Migration may support:

- Spreadsheet imports
- Legacy databases
- Membership software
- Document repositories
- Email archives
- Photo collections
- Physical records (digitized)
- Cloud storage platforms

Historical provenance should be retained where possible.

---

# Migration Workflow

Typical migration includes:

1. Community assessment
2. Data inventory
3. Backup creation
4. Data mapping
5. Validation
6. Pilot migration
7. Community review
8. Production migration
9. Post-migration verification
10. Continuous refinement

---

# Organizational Change

Successful adoption includes:

- Leadership alignment
- Communication plans
- Volunteer engagement
- Role clarification
- Training
- Feedback collection
- Celebration of milestones

Technology alone is insufficient.

---

# Migration Support

The platform provides:

- Import tools
- Validation reports
- AI-assisted mapping
- Duplicate detection
- Migration checklists
- Progress tracking
- Rollback guidance
- Community Success support

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CS-026

Original data shall remain recoverable until migration is confirmed.

---

## BR-CS-027

Migration reports shall remain transparent.

---

## BR-CS-028

Historical attribution shall be preserved where available.

---

## BR-CS-029

Migration shall include organizational readiness activities.

---

## BR-CS-030

Communities determine migration pace.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Migration succeeds when:

- Historical information is preserved.
- Community confidence increases.
- Legacy systems are retired responsibly.
- Leaders understand the process.
- Operational continuity is maintained.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Migration methodology is documented.
- Change management approach is defined.
- Legacy preservation strategy is established.
