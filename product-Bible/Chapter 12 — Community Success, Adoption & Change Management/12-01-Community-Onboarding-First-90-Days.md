# Purpose

This document defines the onboarding framework that guides new communities from initial setup to confident, sustainable use of the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is not merely software deployment, but successful organizational adoption.

---

# Vision

Confident beginnings.

Supported journeys.

Thriving communities.

---

# Philosophy

Communities should never feel overwhelmed.

The platform introduces capabilities progressively, helping leaders build confidence through meaningful accomplishments rather than exhaustive training.

---

# Onboarding Principles

- Start simple.
- Deliver quick wins.
- Learn by doing.
- Respect community culture.
- Celebrate progress.
- Encourage collaboration.
- Build long-term confidence.

---

# Success Outcomes

At the end of onboarding, communities should:

- Understand core workflows.
- Successfully organize events.
- Preserve historical content.
- Empower volunteers.
- Establish governance.
- Feel confident using AI assistance.
- Identify future opportunities.

---

# The First 90 Days

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)

Objectives:

- Configure organization
- Invite leadership
- Define governance
- Import members
- Publish first event
- Complete administrator orientation

---

## Phase 2 — Participation (Days 31–60)

Objectives:

- Member profile completion
- Community Passport adoption
- Volunteer management
- First Run Capsule
- Recognition workflow
- AI introduction

---

## Phase 3 — Momentum (Days 61–90)

Objectives:

- Historical preservation
- Leadership reporting
- Community health review
- Workflow refinement
- Celebration of milestones
- Long-term planning

---

# Onboarding Support

Communities receive:

- Interactive setup guides
- Checklists
- Sample data
- Video tutorials
- AI assistance
- Community Success resources
- Bubble Barrel guidance (where applicable)

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CS-006

Onboarding shall prioritize achievable milestones over feature exposure.

---

## BR-CS-007

Communities may progress at their own pace.

---

## BR-CS-008

Training shall remain contextual.

---

## BR-CS-009

Onboarding progress shall remain visible.

---

## BR-CS-010

Completion shall celebrate community achievement.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Onboarding succeeds when:

- Leaders gain confidence.
- Members participate.
- Governance is operational.
- Historical preservation begins.
- Communities feel prepared for long-term success.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- 90-day framework is documented.
- Success milestones are defined.
- Support model is established.
