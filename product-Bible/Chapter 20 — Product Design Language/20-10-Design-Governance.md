# Purpose

This document defines how design decisions are proposed, reviewed, approved, documented, implemented, and maintained across the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure the product evolves consistently while preserving usability, accessibility, and brand integrity.

---

# Vision

Consistency is created through governance, not coincidence.

---

# Philosophy

A design system is a living product.

It requires ownership, documentation, review, and continuous improvement.

Every design decision should become easier—not harder—to understand over time.

---

# Governance Objectives

The Design Governance process shall:

- Maintain consistency.
- Reduce duplication.
- Protect accessibility.
- Improve collaboration.
- Accelerate development.
- Preserve product quality.
- Support future products.

---

# Design Authority

Every design artifact shall have a designated owner.

Examples include:

- Design tokens.
- Components.
- Icons.
- Typography.
- Illustrations.
- Motion patterns.
- Interaction standards.
- Content guidelines.

Ownership ensures accountability.

---

# Change Management

All proposed design changes should include:

- Business rationale.
- User impact.
- Accessibility review.
- Engineering impact.
- Backward compatibility.
- Migration strategy (if required).

Changes should be reviewed before adoption.

---

# Review Process

Design reviews should evaluate:

- User experience.
- Accessibility.
- Consistency.
- Performance implications.
- Brand alignment.
- Reusability.

Reviews focus on solving user problems rather than personal preferences.

---

# Component Lifecycle

Every shared component shall move through defined stages:

1. Proposal
2. Prototype
3. Review
4. Approved
5. Production
6. Deprecated
7. Archived

Lifecycle management prevents uncontrolled growth.

---

# Documentation

Every shared design asset should include:

- Purpose.
- Usage guidelines.
- Do and Don't examples.
- Accessibility considerations.
- Responsive behavior.
- Interaction notes.
- Related components.

Documentation is part of the deliverable.

---

# Versioning

The Product Design Language, Design System, and Component Library shall be versioned independently.

Breaking changes should be documented with migration guidance.

---

# Contribution Model

Anyone may propose improvements.

Approval should be based on:

- User value.
- Evidence.
- Consistency.
- Accessibility.
- Maintainability.

Authority should not replace evidence.

---

# Quality Metrics

Representative governance metrics include:

- Component reuse rate.
- Accessibility compliance.
- Design review completion rate.
- Design debt.
- UI inconsistency reports.
- Time to adopt new components.

Metrics help evaluate system health.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-046

Shared design assets shall have designated owners.

---

## BR-UX-047

Breaking design changes shall include migration guidance.

---

## BR-UX-048

Reusable components shall be documented before production adoption.

---

## BR-UX-049

Accessibility review shall be part of every shared component review.

---

## BR-UX-050

Design decisions shall be evidence-based where practical.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Design Governance process succeeds when:

- Product consistency improves over time.
- Duplicate components decrease.
- Accessibility remains stable.
- Design decisions become easier to understand.
- New contributors onboard quickly.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Governance process is documented.
- Component lifecycle is defined.
- Review model is established.
