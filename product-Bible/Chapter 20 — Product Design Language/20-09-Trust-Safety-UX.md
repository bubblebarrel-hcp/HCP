# Purpose

This document defines how trust, safety, and transparency are expressed through the user experience of the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its purpose is to ensure users feel informed, protected, respected, and empowered while participating in communities, events, and platform activities.

---

# Vision

Safety should feel supportive, not restrictive.

---

# Philosophy

Trust is built through consistent actions rather than isolated features.

The platform should communicate clearly, explain important decisions, and provide users with appropriate control over their information and interactions.

---

# Trust Principles

## Transparency

Users should understand:

- What information is collected.
- Why it is collected.
- Who can access it.
- How it is used.

Important decisions should be explainable.

---

## User Control

Users should have meaningful control over:

- Privacy settings.
- Notification preferences.
- Profile visibility.
- Shared content.
- Community participation.

Control increases trust.

---

## Predictability

Security-related interactions should be consistent.

Examples include:

- Login verification.
- Permission requests.
- Content reporting.
- Account recovery.

Unexpected behavior reduces confidence.

---

## Respect

Safety features should protect users without treating them as potential offenders.

Language should remain respectful throughout moderation and enforcement processes.

---

# Privacy Experience

Privacy settings should be:

- Easy to find.
- Easy to understand.
- Easy to change.

Avoid hidden or confusing controls.

---

# Community Safety

Users should be able to:

- Report inappropriate content.
- Block unwanted interactions.
- Leave communities.
- Appeal moderation decisions where applicable.

Safety tools should be accessible without becoming intrusive.

---

# Moderation Experience

Moderation actions should:

- Explain the reason when appropriate.
- Describe available next steps.
- Avoid unnecessarily exposing private information.

Moderation should reinforce fairness.

---

# Verification

Where verification is supported, the platform should explain:

- Why verification is requested.
- How verification works.
- What benefits verification provides.

Verification should build confidence rather than create barriers.

---

# Permission Requests

Permission requests should occur only when necessary.

Each request should explain:

- Why access is needed.
- How it improves the experience.
- Whether the feature remains usable without it.

---

# Sensitive Actions

High-impact actions should include appropriate safeguards.

Examples:

- Account deletion.
- Community ownership transfer.
- Role changes.
- Data exports.

Users should understand the consequences before proceeding.

---

# AI Transparency

When AI contributes to content or recommendations, the platform should indicate this clearly where it materially affects user decisions.

Users should know when they are interacting with AI-assisted features.

---

# Recovery Experience

If something goes wrong:

- Preserve user work whenever possible.
- Explain what happened.
- Offer recovery options.
- Reduce frustration.

Recovery builds confidence.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-041

Privacy controls shall remain understandable to non-technical users.

---

## BR-UX-042

Users shall receive clear explanations before granting sensitive permissions.

---

## BR-UX-043

Safety and moderation actions shall provide understandable reasons where appropriate.

---

## BR-UX-044

High-impact operations shall communicate their consequences before execution.

---

## BR-UX-045

AI-assisted experiences shall provide appropriate transparency.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Trust & Safety UX succeeds when:

- Users report confidence in privacy controls.
- Moderation processes are perceived as fair.
- Sensitive actions are completed with fewer accidental errors.
- AI-assisted features remain understandable.
- Trust in the platform increases over time.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Trust principles are documented.
- Privacy and moderation experiences are defined.
- Safety interactions are standardized.
