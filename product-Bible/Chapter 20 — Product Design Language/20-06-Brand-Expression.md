# Purpose

This document defines how the Hash Community Platform (HCP) expresses its identity visually and verbally while remaining aligned with the broader Bubble Barrel brand ecosystem.

It establishes a consistent approach to branding across interfaces, communications, marketing materials, and future platform extensions.

---

# Vision

Every interaction should reinforce trust in both HCP and Bubble Barrel.

---

# Brand Architecture

HCP is a product of Bubble Barrel.

The relationship should be visible without overwhelming HCP's individual identity.

Brand hierarchy:

Bubble Barrel

↓

Hash Community Platform

↓

Community (Kennel)

This hierarchy communicates ownership while allowing communities to retain their own identities.

---

# Brand Personality

HCP should consistently feel:

- Mischievous
- Rugged
- International
- Human
- Energetic
- Welcoming
- Dependable
- Community-driven
- Inclusive
- Respectful

Avoid personalities that feel:

- Corporate
- Sterile
- Exclusive
- Aggressive
- Overly playful
- Overly formal

The brand should say "this platform understands hashing", never "LOOK, THIS IS A HASH APP". Literal Hash clichés in the interface — beer imagery, skulls, muddy footprints — are out. The personality comes through in microcopy, illustration, animation, icons, trail visualisation and the occasional easter egg. See Chapter 20-05 for the color system (D24).

---

# Tone of Voice

Communication should be:

- Friendly.
- Clear.
- Direct.
- Respectful.
- Encouraging.

Examples:

Instead of:

"Authentication failed."

Prefer:

"We couldn't sign you in. Please check your email and password and try again."

---

Instead of:

"Submission rejected."

Prefer:

"We couldn't approve this yet. Here's what needs attention."

---

# Messaging Principles

Messages should:

- Explain.
- Encourage.
- Guide.
- Celebrate.

Avoid:

- Blame.
- Fear.
- Artificial urgency.
- Unnecessary technical language.

---

# Logo Usage

The HCP logo should:

- Remain legible at all sizes.
- Maintain sufficient clear space.
- Never be distorted.
- Never be recolored outside approved brand variants.
- Always appear on appropriate contrast backgrounds.

---

# Product Identity

HCP's identity should emphasize:

- Community.
- Shared experiences.
- Exploration.
- Stories.
- Belonging.

Visual elements should consistently reinforce these themes.

---

# Photography Direction

Preferred imagery:

- Real members.
- Authentic events.
- Outdoor environments.
- Genuine interactions.
- Diverse communities.

Avoid:

- Staged corporate photography.
- Unrealistic stock imagery.
- Overly polished scenes that reduce authenticity.

---

# Illustration Direction

Illustrations should:

- Support understanding.
- Welcome newcomers.
- Explain features.
- Celebrate participation.

Illustrations should reflect diversity and inclusion.

---

# Iconography

Icons should:

- Match the shared Bubble Barrel icon style.
- Be simple.
- Be recognizable.
- Maintain consistent stroke weight and proportions.

---

# Brand Across Platforms

Brand consistency should extend to:

- Mobile applications.
- Web applications.
- Administrative portals.
- Emails.
- Documentation.
- Social media.
- Presentations.
- Marketing assets.

---

# Community Identity

While HCP provides the platform identity, individual kennels should retain flexibility to express:

- Logos.
- Banners.
- Colors (within accessibility constraints).
- Descriptions.
- Traditions.

The platform should celebrate local identity while preserving overall consistency.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-026

Product branding shall remain consistent across all supported platforms.

---

## BR-UX-027

User-facing communication shall follow the defined tone of voice.

---

## BR-UX-028

Community branding shall not compromise usability or accessibility.

---

## BR-UX-029

Marketing assets shall align with the Bubble Barrel brand architecture.

---

## BR-UX-030

Brand expression shall reinforce trust, inclusion, and community.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The brand expression succeeds when:

- Users recognize HCP across platforms.
- Communication feels consistent.
- Community identity is respected.
- Bubble Barrel ownership is clear without overshadowing HCP.
- Marketing and product experiences feel unified.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Brand architecture is documented.
- Voice and tone are defined.
- Visual identity guidelines are established.
