# Purpose

This document defines the writing style, tone of voice, and content standards used throughout the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its purpose is to ensure every word—from onboarding to notifications, error messages to AI responses—feels consistent, clear, respectful, and aligned with the platform's mission of strengthening communities.

---

# Vision

Every word should help someone move forward.

---

# Philosophy

Content is part of the interface.

Good writing reduces confusion, builds trust, and helps people accomplish their goals with confidence.

Users should never need to translate system language into human language.

---

# Voice Attributes

The HCP voice should be:

- Clear
- Friendly
- Respectful
- Helpful
- Honest
- Encouraging
- Inclusive
- Calm

Avoid sounding:

- Robotic
- Condescending
- Overly casual
- Overly formal
- Sales-focused
- Alarmist

---

# Writing Principles

## Clarity First

Prefer familiar words over technical jargon.

Example:

Instead of:

"Authentication credentials are invalid."

Use:

"Your email or password is incorrect."

---

## Action-Oriented

Help users understand what they can do next.

Example:

Instead of:

"Upload failed."

Use:

"We couldn't upload your photo. Please check your connection and try again."

---

## Honest Communication

Never hide problems.

If something goes wrong:

- Explain what happened.
- Explain what users can do.
- Explain what the platform is doing.

Avoid vague messages.

---

## Encourage, Don't Blame

Users should never feel at fault.

Instead of:

"You entered invalid data."

Use:

"We couldn't understand that date. Try using DD/MM/YYYY."

---

## Celebrate Meaningful Moments

Recognize achievements that strengthen the community.

Examples:

- First event joined.
- First trail report published.
- Volunteer recognition.
- Community milestones.

Celebration should feel sincere, not manipulative.

---

## Respect Attention

Keep messages concise.

Users should be able to understand essential information quickly.

Long explanations belong in help documentation—not dialogs.

---

# Microcopy Standards

Buttons should use verbs.

Examples:

- Join Event
- Save Draft
- Invite Member
- Publish Report

Avoid vague labels like:

- OK
- Submit
- Continue

unless context makes them obvious.

---

# Error Messages

Every error message should answer:

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen? (if known)
3. What can I do next?

---

# Success Messages

Success messages should:

- Confirm completion.
- Reinforce confidence.
- Avoid unnecessary celebration.

Example:

"Your event has been published."

not

"🎉 Amazing! Fantastic! You're awesome!"

---

# Empty States

Empty states should:

- Explain the situation.
- Offer a meaningful next step.
- Encourage exploration.

Example:

"No upcoming events yet.

Why not create one or join a nearby community?"

---

# Notifications

Notifications should:

- Deliver value.
- Explain why they matter.
- Respect user preferences.
- Avoid clickbait language.

---

# AI Responses

AI-generated content should:

- Clearly distinguish facts from suggestions.
- Cite sources where appropriate.
- Admit uncertainty when necessary.
- Match the HCP voice.

---

# Localization

Content should:

- Avoid idioms that do not translate well.
- Use culturally neutral language where practical.
- Support localization without changing intent.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-036

User-facing content shall prioritize clarity over technical precision.

---

## BR-UX-037

Every actionable message shall guide users toward an appropriate next step.

---

## BR-UX-038

System messaging shall remain respectful and non-judgmental.

---

## BR-UX-039

AI-generated content shall follow the same voice guidelines as human-written content.

---

## BR-UX-040

Microcopy shall be reviewed as part of the product design process.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The content voice succeeds when:

- Users understand system messages quickly.
- Support requests caused by unclear wording decrease.
- Product copy feels consistent across features.
- AI responses align with platform voice.
- Community interactions remain welcoming and respectful.

- Writing principles are established.
