# Purpose

This document defines the emotional qualities the Hash Community Platform (HCP) should evoke throughout the user journey.

While functionality determines what users can do, emotional experience influences how they feel while doing it. The goal is to foster confidence, belonging, and enjoyment without relying on excessive visual effects or unnecessary complexity.

---

# Vision

Every interaction should strengthen trust, belonging, and confidence.

---

# Philosophy

People rarely remember every feature they used.

They remember how a product made them feel.

For HCP, the desired feeling is that of joining a welcoming community where participation is simple, safe, and rewarding.

---

# Emotional Pillars

## Belonging

Users should feel they are part of a community rather than using a piece of software.

Design should highlight:

- Groups.
- Shared experiences.
- Member contributions.
- Community achievements.
- Welcoming language.

---

## Confidence

Users should feel in control.

The interface should communicate:

- What is happening.
- What happens next.
- Whether actions succeeded.
- How to recover from mistakes.

Confidence reduces hesitation.

---

## Warmth

The platform should feel approachable.

Use:

- Friendly language.
- Encouraging microcopy.
- Human-centered messaging.
- Positive reinforcement.

Avoid robotic or overly formal communication.

---

## Trust

Trust should be earned through consistency and transparency.

The platform should:

- Explain important actions.
- Respect privacy.
- Keep promises.
- Avoid surprises.
- Make policies understandable.

---

## Celebration

Community achievements deserve recognition.

Celebrate:

- First event joined.
- Volunteer milestones.
- Community anniversaries.
- Completed trails.
- Positive contributions.

Recognition should feel genuine rather than gamified.

---

## Calm

Avoid overwhelming users.

Reduce:

- Visual clutter.
- Notification overload.
- Excessive animations.
- Competing calls to action.

Calm interfaces help people focus.

---

## Inclusion

Every member should feel welcome regardless of:

- Experience level.
- Device.
- Language.
- Accessibility needs.
- Community role.

Inclusive design expands participation.

---

# Emotional Journey

## First Visit

Desired emotions:

- Curiosity.
- Safety.
- Excitement.

Users should quickly understand what HCP offers and how to get involved.

---

## Joining a Community

Desired emotions:

- Welcome.
- Confidence.
- Belonging.

The joining process should feel like entering a group, not completing paperwork.

---

## Participating in an Event

Desired emotions:

- Anticipation.
- Connection.
- Enjoyment.

The interface should stay out of the way so users can focus on the experience itself.

---

## Sharing Memories

Desired emotions:

- Pride.
- Gratitude.
- Nostalgia.

Uploading photos, writing trail reports, or thanking volunteers should feel meaningful.

---

## Returning Later

Desired emotions:

- Familiarity.
- Comfort.
- Motivation.

Users should feel invited back, not pressured to return.

---

# Microcopy Guidelines

Language should be:

- Friendly.
- Clear.
- Respectful.
- Inclusive.
- Encouraging.

Avoid:

- Blame.
- Sarcasm.
- Technical jargon.
- Guilt-driven messaging.
- Artificial urgency.

---

# Notification Philosophy

Notifications should:

- Inform.
- Encourage.
- Celebrate.

They should not manipulate users into opening the app.

Every notification should have a clear purpose.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-011

User messaging shall prioritize encouragement over urgency.

---

## BR-UX-012

Recognition shall celebrate meaningful community participation rather than arbitrary engagement metrics.

---

## BR-UX-013

Notifications shall provide value before requesting attention.

---

## BR-UX-014

Important system actions shall reinforce user confidence through clear feedback.

---

## BR-UX-015

The interface shall foster belonging throughout the user journey.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The emotional experience succeeds when:

- Users feel welcome during onboarding.
- Community participation increases.
- Trust in the platform grows.
- Notifications are perceived as helpful.
- Returning users feel comfortable and familiar with the experience.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Emotional pillars are documented.
- User journey emotions are defined.
- Messaging philosophy is established.
