# Purpose

This document defines the privacy philosophy, consent framework, and privacy-by-design principles that govern the collection, processing, storage, sharing, and retention of personal information within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Privacy is treated as a core product capability rather than a legal afterthought.

---

# Vision

Transparent privacy.

Meaningful consent.

Respectful technology.

---

# Philosophy

Members should always understand:

- what information is collected,
- why it is collected,
- who can access it,
- how long it is retained,
- and how they can control it.

Privacy supports trust through clarity and choice.

---

# Privacy by Design Principles

The platform shall:

- Minimize unnecessary data collection.
- Use the least privileged access.
- Protect information by default.
- Explain data usage clearly.
- Respect user preferences.
- Support secure deletion where appropriate.
- Preserve legitimate historical records responsibly.

Privacy is considered during design, not after implementation.

---

# Consent Principles

Consent should be:

- Explicit where required.
- Easy to understand.
- Granular where practical.
- Easy to review.
- Easy to withdraw.
- Recorded transparently.

Consent should never rely on ambiguity.

---

# Data Categories

Examples include:

- Identity information
- Membership records
- Community participation
- Historical contributions
- Media
- AI interactions
- Learning records
- Governance activities
- Audit information

Each category follows documented handling rules.

---

# Privacy Controls

Members may control:

- Public profile visibility
- Search discoverability
- Contact preferences
- Media permissions
- AI personalization
- Notification preferences
- Linked accounts
- Data exports

Controls remain understandable.

---

# Organizational Responsibilities

Communities are responsible for:

- Respecting member privacy.
- Managing authorized access.
- Honoring consent decisions.
- Following applicable legal obligations.
- Protecting historical information responsibly.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-TE-006

Privacy shall be enabled by default wherever practical.

---

## BR-TE-007

Consent records shall remain auditable.

---

## BR-TE-008

Members shall be able to review their privacy settings.

---

## BR-TE-009

Data collection shall remain proportionate to community needs.

---

## BR-TE-010

Privacy controls shall remain understandable by non-technical users.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Privacy succeeds when:

- Members understand how information is used.
- Communities manage data responsibly.
- Consent remains transparent.
- Privacy settings are easy to control.
- Trust increases through predictable behavior.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Privacy principles are documented.
- Consent framework is defined.
- User control model is established.
