# Purpose

This document defines the principles, responsibilities, and governance practices for preserving community history, institutional knowledge, and digital legacies within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Historical preservation is treated as an ethical responsibility that extends across generations.

---

# Vision

Preserved memories.

Living history.

Enduring communities.

---

# Philosophy

Technology should help communities remember.

Historical records are not simply archives; they are expressions of identity, culture, service, and shared experience.

Preservation should protect authenticity while ensuring future generations can discover, understand, and learn from the past.

---

# Stewardship Principles

Historical stewardship shall:

- Preserve authenticity.
- Respect provenance.
- Document context.
- Protect integrity.
- Encourage contribution.
- Enable discovery.
- Support future generations.

Preservation extends beyond technology.

---

# Community Memory

The platform supports preservation of:

- Historical photographs.
- Oral histories.
- Community publications.
- Leadership histories.
- Event narratives.
- Meeting records.
- Milestone celebrations.
- Recognition histories.
- Volunteer stories.
- Cultural traditions.

Community memory is broader than structured data.

---

# Digital Legacy

Communities may establish policies governing:

- Historical archives.
- Long-term preservation.
- Successor stewardship.
- Public exhibitions.
- Anniversary collections.
- Memorial tributes.
- Preservation priorities.

Policies remain community-controlled.

---

# Preservation Responsibilities

The platform shall:

- Preserve provenance.
- Record attribution.
- Protect historical integrity.
- Maintain version history.
- Support archival exports.
- Encourage metadata quality.

Stewardship remains collaborative.

# Acceptance Criteria

Digital Legacy succeeds when:

- Community history remains discoverable.
- Future leaders understand past decisions.
- Historical authenticity is preserved.
- Members contribute confidently.
- Communities strengthen their collective identity.
