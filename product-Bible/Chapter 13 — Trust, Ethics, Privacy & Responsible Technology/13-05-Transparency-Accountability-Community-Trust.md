# Purpose

This document defines the transparency, accountability, and governance practices that help communities understand how the Hash Community Platform (HCP) operates, how decisions are made, and how responsibilities are assigned.

Transparency strengthens confidence by making significant actions understandable and reviewable.

---

# Vision

Transparent systems.

Accountable stewardship.

Trusted communities.

---

# Philosophy

Communities should never have to guess:

- why something happened,
- who approved it,
- what changed,
- or how decisions can be reviewed.

Responsible technology communicates openly.

---

# Transparency Principles

The platform shall:

- Explain important actions.
- Record meaningful decisions.
- Distinguish automation from human judgment.
- Preserve audit history.
- Encourage review.
- Support responsible governance.
- Promote organizational learning.

Transparency exists to increase understanding, not complexity.

---

# Accountability Model

Responsibilities remain identifiable for:

- Governance decisions
- Administrative actions
- AI recommendations
- Data changes
- Plugin activity
- Security events
- Historical preservation

Authority remains traceable.

---

# Explainability

Where appropriate, the platform explains:

- Why a recommendation was made.
- Why access was granted or denied.
- Why a workflow changed.
- Why notifications were sent.
- Why policies apply.

Explanations remain understandable.

---

# Review Mechanisms

Communities may review:

- Governance history
- AI-assisted actions
- Audit trails
- Policy changes
- Historical revisions
- Administrative activities

Review supports trust.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-TE-026

Significant platform actions shall remain explainable.

---

## BR-TE-027

Human decisions and AI recommendations shall remain distinguishable.

---

## BR-TE-028

Audit records shall preserve accountability.

---

## BR-TE-029

Communities retain authority over governance decisions.

---

## BR-TE-030

Transparency shall balance openness with privacy and security.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Transparency succeeds when:

- Communities understand important decisions.
- Accountability remains visible.
- Audit history supports learning.
- Trust increases through clarity.
- Governance becomes more understandable.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Transparency principles are documented.
- Accountability framework is defined.
- Explainability model is established.
