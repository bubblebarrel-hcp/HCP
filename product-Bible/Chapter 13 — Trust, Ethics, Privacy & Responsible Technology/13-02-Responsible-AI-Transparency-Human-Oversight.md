# Purpose

This document defines the ethical principles, governance framework, transparency requirements, and human oversight mechanisms for all artificial intelligence capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

AI is positioned as an assistive technology that strengthens communities while respecting human autonomy, governance, and accountability.

---

# Vision

Responsible intelligence.

Transparent assistance.

Human-centered decisions.

---

# Philosophy

Artificial intelligence supports community members and leaders by providing insights, recommendations, automation, and knowledge assistance.

Final authority remains with appropriately authorized people.

---

# AI Principles

AI within HCP shall:

- Explain recommendations.
- Respect community governance.
- Preserve human autonomy.
- Minimize bias.
- Support accessibility.
- Protect privacy.
- Encourage learning.
- Remain accountable.

---

# Human Oversight

AI shall not independently make governance decisions such as:

- Leadership appointments.
- Member disciplinary actions.
- Voting outcomes.
- Membership termination.
- Policy approval.
- Recognition awards requiring human judgment.

AI may assist but not decide.

---

# Transparency

The platform clearly indicates:

- When AI is involved.
- Why recommendations were made.
- Confidence where appropriate.
- Available alternatives.
- Supporting evidence.
- Human review requirements.

AI behavior remains understandable.

---

# AI Roles

Examples include:

- Knowledge assistant
- Historical research
- Meeting summaries
- Event planning
- Volunteer recommendations
- Learning assistance
- Preservation support
- Community analytics

AI remains advisory.

---

# Continuous Evaluation

AI systems are periodically evaluated for:

- Accuracy
- Fairness
- Bias
- Explainability
- Reliability
- User satisfaction
- Governance alignment

Continuous improvement is expected.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-TE-011

AI recommendations shall remain distinguishable from human decisions.

---

## BR-TE-012

Human review shall remain available for significant actions.

---

## BR-TE-013

AI explanations shall accompany meaningful recommendations.

---

## BR-TE-014

AI shall respect documented governance policies.

---

## BR-TE-015

Responsible AI practices shall evolve over time.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Responsible AI succeeds when:

- Members understand AI involvement.
- Leaders retain authority.
- Recommendations remain explainable.
- Trust in AI increases responsibly.
- Communities benefit without losing autonomy.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Responsible AI framework is documented.
- Human oversight model is defined.
- Transparency requirements are established.
