# Purpose

This document establishes the principles and safeguards that promote fairness, reduce bias, and encourage inclusive decision support throughout the Hash Community Platform (HCP), particularly where artificial intelligence and analytics are involved.

The objective is to help communities make better-informed decisions while preserving dignity, equity, and organizational trust.

---

# Vision

Fair recommendations.

Inclusive participation.

Trusted outcomes.

---

# Philosophy

Communities are strengthened when every member has a fair opportunity to contribute, participate, and be recognized.

Technology should reduce barriers rather than reinforce them.

---

# Fairness Principles

The platform shall:

- Encourage equitable participation.
- Surface diverse perspectives.
- Avoid unnecessary exclusion.
- Explain recommendations.
- Continuously evaluate outcomes.
- Respect community governance.
- Support accessibility.

Fairness is an ongoing practice.

---

# Bias Mitigation

The platform evaluates potential sources of bias including:

- Incomplete historical data.
- Uneven participation records.
- Over-representation of highly active members.
- Historical leadership patterns.
- Language limitations.
- Data quality issues.

Bias reviews support continuous improvement.

---

# Inclusive Decision Support

Recommendations should:

- Present supporting evidence.
- Offer alternative viewpoints where appropriate.
- Encourage human review.
- Highlight uncertainty.
- Avoid presenting probabilistic outcomes as facts.

Decision support remains advisory.

---

# Accessibility & Inclusion

Fairness also includes:

- Accessible interfaces.
- Multilingual support.
- Flexible learning resources.
- Respect for varying digital literacy.
- Inclusive communication.

Participation should remain attainable.

---

# Continuous Evaluation

Fairness reviews consider:

- Recommendation outcomes.
- Community feedback.
- Reported concerns.
- Emerging risks.
- Changes in community demographics.
- AI performance.

Evaluation remains continuous.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-TE-016

AI recommendations shall support equitable participation.

---

## BR-TE-017

Potentially sensitive recommendations shall include human review.

---

## BR-TE-018

Bias mitigation efforts shall remain documented.

---

## BR-TE-019

Communities shall retain final decision-making authority.

---

## BR-TE-020

Fairness evaluations shall inform future platform improvements.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Fairness succeeds when:

- Recommendations remain understandable.
- Communities perceive processes as fair.
- Participation opportunities broaden.
- Bias is actively monitored.
- Trust continues to grow.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Fairness principles are documented.
- Bias mitigation strategy is defined.
- Inclusive decision support framework is established.
