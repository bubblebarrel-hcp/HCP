# Purpose

This document establishes the ethical foundations that guide the design, development, deployment, operation, and evolution of the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

It defines the values that shape every technical and organizational decision.

Trust is considered a strategic asset that must be earned continuously.

---

# Vision

Trusted communities.

Responsible technology.

Human-centered stewardship.

---

# Philosophy

Technology should strengthen communities without compromising dignity, autonomy, transparency, or historical integrity.

Every capability should reinforce trust rather than merely increase functionality.

---

# Core Ethical Principles

The platform is guided by:

- Respect for people.
- Respect for communities.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Fairness.
- Privacy.
- Accessibility.
- Inclusion.
- Stewardship.
- Long-term responsibility.

---

# Human-Centered Design

Technology exists to assist people.

It shall not:

- Replace community relationships.
- Undermine leadership.
- Manipulate participation.
- Encourage unhealthy behaviors.
- Prioritize engagement over wellbeing.

The platform supports people rather than directing them.

---

# Trust Principles

Trust is built through:

- Honest communication.
- Predictable behavior.
- Explainable systems.
- Reliable operation.
- Evidence-based decisions.
- Respect for community governance.

Trust requires consistency over time.

---

# Ethical Decision Framework

When evaluating new capabilities, teams should ask:

1. Does this strengthen communities?
2. Does it respect individual dignity?
3. Is it transparent?
4. Is it fair?
5. Can it be explained?
6. Can communities control it?
7. Will future stewards understand why it exists?

---

# Business Rules

## BR-TE-001

Trust shall take precedence over short-term optimization.

---

## BR-TE-002

Platform capabilities shall respect community autonomy.

---

## BR-TE-003

Ethical considerations shall be documented for significant capabilities.

---

## BR-TE-004

Technology shall augment rather than replace human judgment.

---

## BR-TE-005

Responsible stewardship shall remain a continuous obligation.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The ethical framework succeeds when:

- Teams understand guiding values.
- Product decisions remain consistent.
- Communities experience trustworthy behavior.
- Ethical trade-offs become visible.
- Long-term trust increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Ethical philosophy is documented.
- Decision framework is established.
- Trust principles are defined.
