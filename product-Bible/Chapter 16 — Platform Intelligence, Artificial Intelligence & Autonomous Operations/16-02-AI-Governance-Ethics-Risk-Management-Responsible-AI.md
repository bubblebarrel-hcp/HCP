# Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework, ethical principles, risk management strategy, and responsible AI requirements for all Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure AI remains trustworthy, transparent, accountable, secure, and aligned with the mission of strengthening communities.

---

# Vision

Responsible intelligence.

Transparent decisions.

Trusted communities.

---

# Philosophy

AI should earn trust through predictable behavior, transparency, accountability, and respect for human authority.

Every AI capability should improve the community without compromising privacy, fairness, or safety.

---

# Governance Objectives

The AI Governance framework shall:

- Define accountability.
- Protect community data.
- Ensure ethical operation.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Enable human oversight.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Maintain public trust.

Governance enables responsible innovation.

---

# Core Ethical Principles

Artificial Intelligence within HCP shall:

- Respect human dignity.
- Preserve community autonomy.
- Avoid unnecessary bias.
- Explain important recommendations.
- Protect confidential information.
- Minimize harm.
- Support accessibility.
- Operate within defined authority.

Ethics remain operational requirements.

---

# AI Risk Categories

AI risks include:

- Hallucinations
- Incorrect recommendations
- Bias
- Privacy leakage
- Security misuse
- Automation errors
- Model drift
- Prompt injection
- Data poisoning
- Unauthorized access

Each risk shall have documented mitigation strategies.

---

# Human Oversight

AI actions shall be classified according to required oversight.

### Informational

AI provides information only.

No approval required.

Examples:

- Summaries
- Search
- Explanations

---

### Advisory

AI provides recommendations.

Humans approve decisions.

Examples:

- Volunteer recommendations
- Event planning
- Policy suggestions

---

### Assisted Execution

AI performs actions after explicit authorization.

Examples:

- Drafting announcements
- Scheduling meetings
- Sending reminders

---

### Restricted

AI shall never independently perform:

- Governance voting
- Financial approvals
- Membership termination
- Legal decisions
- Compliance certification
- Security authorization

Human authority remains mandatory.

---

# Transparency

Communities should know:

- When AI generated content.
- Which assistant responded.
- What information sources were used (where appropriate).
- Confidence level (when meaningful).
- Whether human review occurred.

Transparency strengthens trust.

---

# Accountability

Every AI capability shall define:

- Owner
- Purpose
- Approved datasets
- Risk level
- Review frequency
- Escalation path

No AI capability operates without ownership.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-006

AI-generated content shall be distinguishable from human-authored content where appropriate.

---

## BR-AI-007

Restricted actions require human authorization.

---

## BR-AI-008

High-risk AI capabilities require periodic governance review.

---

## BR-AI-009

AI decisions shall be auditable.

---

## BR-AI-010

Communities shall retain control over AI configuration.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Responsible AI succeeds when:

- Communities trust AI.
- Risks remain manageable.
- Human oversight is effective.
- Transparency is maintained.
- Governance evolves with technology.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- AI governance framework is documented.
- Ethical principles are established.
- Risk management strategy is defined.
