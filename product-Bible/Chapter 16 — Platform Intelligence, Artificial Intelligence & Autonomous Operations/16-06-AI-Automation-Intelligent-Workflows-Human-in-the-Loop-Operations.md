# Purpose

This document defines the automation framework, intelligent workflow architecture, approval mechanisms, and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) operating model for Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to automate routine work responsibly while preserving human oversight for decisions requiring judgment, accountability, or governance.

---

# Vision

Automate routine work.

Support human judgment.

Increase organizational capacity.

---

# Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence should automate repetitive, predictable, and low-risk tasks while enabling humans to make informed decisions for activities with greater impact.

Automation should reduce administrative burden without diminishing human responsibility.

---

# Automation Principles

AI automation shall:

- Be transparent.
- Be reversible where practical.
- Respect permissions.
- Operate within approved policies.
- Produce audit records.
- Escalate uncertainty.
- Require approval for high-impact actions.

Automation serves people.

---

# Workflow Categories

## Informational

Provides information only.

Examples:

- Search
- Summaries
- Status reports

No approval required.

---

## Assistive

Produces drafts and recommendations.

Examples:

- Meeting agendas
- Newsletters
- Event plans
- Volunteer schedules

User review before execution.

---

## Guided Automation

Executes approved workflows after explicit authorization.

Examples:

- Send approved announcements.
- Schedule approved meetings.
- Generate recurring reports.
- Update approved calendars.

Authorization required.

---

## Supervised Automation

Performs repetitive operational tasks within predefined rules.

Examples:

- Reminder notifications.
- Routine document classification.
- Metadata enrichment.
- Knowledge indexing.

Subject to governance policies.

---

## Restricted Automation

AI shall never independently:

- Approve governance resolutions.
- Conduct elections.
- Remove community members.
- Override security controls.
- Commit financial transactions.
- Modify governing documents.

Human authority remains mandatory.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

The HITL framework defines:

- Approval checkpoints.
- Escalation rules.
- Confidence thresholds.
- Override mechanisms.
- Audit logging.
- Exception handling.

Human oversight remains integral.

---

# Workflow Engine

The automation engine coordinates:

- AI agents
- Business workflows
- Approval requests
- Notifications
- External integrations
- Task execution
- Rollback procedures

Automation follows defined workflows.

---

# Automation Metrics

Representative metrics include:

- Automation success rate
- Approval rate
- Human override frequency
- Time saved
- Error reduction
- User satisfaction
- Workflow completion rate

Metrics drive improvement.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-026

High-impact workflows require human approval.

---

## BR-AI-027

Automated actions shall be auditable.

---

## BR-AI-028

Automation shall operate within approved policy boundaries.

---

## BR-AI-029

Users shall retain authority to interrupt or cancel eligible automated workflows.

---

## BR-AI-030

Automation effectiveness shall be measured continuously.

# Acceptance Criteria

AI Automation succeeds when:

- Administrative effort decreases.
- Human oversight remains effective.
- Workflow completion accelerates.
- Errors decline.
- Communities retain confidence.
