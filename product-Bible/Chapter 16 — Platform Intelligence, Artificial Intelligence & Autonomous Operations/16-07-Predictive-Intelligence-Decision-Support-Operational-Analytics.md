# Purpose

This document defines the predictive analytics, decision support framework, operational intelligence capabilities, and forecasting strategy for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to transform historical and real-time organizational data into actionable insights that help communities anticipate risks, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.

---

# Vision

Understand today.

Anticipate tomorrow.

Decide with confidence.

---

# Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence should provide evidence-based predictions and recommendations while leaving strategic decisions under human control.

Predictions support leadership—they do not replace it.

---

# Predictive Intelligence Objectives

The platform shall:

- Detect emerging trends.
- Forecast operational outcomes.
- Identify organizational risks.
- Recommend preventive actions.
- Improve planning.
- Increase decision confidence.
- Surface hidden patterns.

Insights should be timely and actionable.

---

# Prediction Domains

Representative prediction domains include:

- Member growth
- Member retention
- Volunteer participation
- Event attendance
- Budget variance
- Committee activity
- Document usage
- Community engagement
- Training completion
- Operational workload

Each domain may evolve independently.

---

# Decision Support

AI may assist leaders by:

- Highlighting trends.
- Explaining contributing factors.
- Comparing historical outcomes.
- Simulating scenarios.
- Recommending options.
- Estimating confidence.
- Identifying uncertainties.

Recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Scenario Analysis

The platform should support:

- Best-case projections.
- Expected outcomes.
- Worst-case projections.
- What-if simulations.
- Resource planning.
- Capacity forecasting.

Planning improves resilience.

---

# Explainability

Predictive recommendations should include:

- Confidence estimates.
- Supporting evidence.
- Relevant historical trends.
- Influencing factors.
- Applicable assumptions.

Leaders should understand why recommendations are made.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-031

Predictions shall include confidence indicators where appropriate.

---

## BR-AI-032

Decision support shall distinguish observations from recommendations.

---

## BR-AI-033

Predictions shall not override community governance.

---

## BR-AI-034

Forecast models shall be monitored for performance.

---

## BR-AI-035

Communities may configure predictive capabilities according to governance policies.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Predictive Intelligence succeeds when:

- Decision quality improves.
- Risks are identified earlier.
- Planning becomes more effective.
- Community leaders trust recommendations.
- Forecast accuracy improves over time.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Predictive framework is documented.
- Decision support model is established.
- Forecasting principles are defined.
