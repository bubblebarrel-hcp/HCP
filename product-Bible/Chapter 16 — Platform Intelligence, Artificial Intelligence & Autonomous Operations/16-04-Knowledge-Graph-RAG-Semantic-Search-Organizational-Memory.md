# Purpose

This document defines the knowledge architecture, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework, semantic search capabilities, knowledge graph strategy, and organizational memory model for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to transform community information into trusted, searchable, explainable, and continuously evolving organizational intelligence.

---

# Vision

Every document.

Every decision.

Every policy.

Every event.

Instantly understandable.

---

# Philosophy

Knowledge should never become inaccessible because of time, personnel changes, or document volume.

Artificial Intelligence should help communities discover knowledge—not invent it.

---

# Knowledge Sources

The knowledge platform may index:

- Constitutions
- Bylaws
- Policies
- Meeting minutes
- Financial reports
- Newsletters
- Historical archives
- Events
- Committees
- Member roles
- Training materials
- Help documentation
- Uploaded documents
- Knowledge articles

Knowledge grows continuously.

---

# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

AI responses shall prioritize retrieved organizational knowledge before relying on model knowledge.

The retrieval pipeline includes:

- Query understanding
- Semantic retrieval
- Permission filtering
- Context assembly
- Citation generation
- Response generation

Retrieval precedes generation.

---

# Semantic Search

Search shall support:

- Natural language
- Similar concepts
- Synonyms
- Related topics
- Historical references
- Cross-document relationships
- Multilingual retrieval

Search should understand meaning rather than keywords alone.

---

# Knowledge Graph

The knowledge graph represents relationships between:

- Members
- Communities
- Events
- Committees
- Documents
- Policies
- Decisions
- Projects
- Historical milestones

Relationships improve discovery.

---

# Organizational Memory

Organizational memory preserves:

- Historical decisions
- Institutional practices
- Leadership transitions
- Community traditions
- Lessons learned
- Strategic initiatives

Knowledge survives leadership change.

---

# Knowledge Quality

Knowledge shall include:

- Source attribution
- Version history
- Confidence indicators
- Freshness metadata
- Approval status
- Access permissions

Trust requires provenance.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-016

AI responses shall prioritize authorized organizational knowledge where available.

---

## BR-AI-017

Knowledge retrieval shall respect existing permissions.

---

## BR-AI-018

Retrieved sources shall be attributable.

---

## BR-AI-019

Knowledge freshness shall be monitored.

---

## BR-AI-020

Organizational memory shall remain versioned and auditable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Knowledge Intelligence succeeds when:

- Members locate information quickly.
- AI responses reference trusted sources.
- Institutional knowledge is preserved.
- Search quality improves.
- Community confidence increases.

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Knowledge architecture is documented.
- RAG strategy is established.
