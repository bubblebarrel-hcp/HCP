# Purpose

This document defines the operational framework for monitoring, evaluating, maintaining, and continuously improving Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure AI systems remain reliable, observable, measurable, and trustworthy throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Vision

Reliable intelligence.

Measured performance.

Continuous improvement.

---

# Philosophy

AI capabilities require the same operational discipline as any other critical platform service.

Observability, evaluation, and governance are continuous responsibilities.

---

# AI Observability

The platform shall monitor:

- Request volume
- Response latency
- Model availability
- Token consumption
- Tool execution
- Retrieval quality
- Error rates
- User feedback
- Human overrides
- Policy violations

Operational visibility supports trust.

---

# Evaluation Framework

Evaluation shall include:

- Answer quality
- Citation accuracy
- Hallucination rate
- Task completion
- Recommendation usefulness
- Workflow success
- User satisfaction
- Accessibility compliance

Evaluation is continuous.

---

# Model Lifecycle

The platform shall support:

- Candidate evaluation
- Controlled rollout
- A/B testing
- Canary deployment
- Rollback
- Retirement
- Replacement

Model evolution remains controlled.

---

# Continuous Learning

Continuous improvement may originate from:

- User feedback
- Human corrections
- Governance reviews
- Operational metrics
- Knowledge updates
- Workflow outcomes
- Incident analysis

Learning improves future performance.

---

# Prompt Management

Prompt assets shall define:

- Owner
- Purpose
- Version
- Test coverage
- Approval history
- Supported models

Prompts are managed assets.

---

# AI Operations

Operational responsibilities include:

- Monitoring
- Capacity planning
- Cost optimization
- Incident response
- Vendor management
- Evaluation scheduling
- Governance reporting

AI operations remain measurable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

AI Operations succeeds when:

- Quality remains stable.
- Hallucinations decrease.
- Retrieval quality improves.
- Costs remain predictable.
- Community confidence increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- AI operational framework is documented.
- Evaluation strategy is established.
- Continuous improvement process is defined.
