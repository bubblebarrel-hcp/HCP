# Hash Community Platform Product Bible

# Chapter 5 --- User Personas

## Purpose

Defines the primary users, their goals, permissions, motivations and
success criteria.

## Personas

### Hasher

Goals: Join runs, follow trails, upload media, interact with community.
Permissions: Join kennels, RSVP, upload media, comment, react.

### Hare

Goals: Design and publish trails. Permissions: Create/edit trails,
manage release time, configure waypoints.

### Hash Scribe

Goals: Publish official trail reports. AI assists with draft generation
while the Scribe retains editorial control.

### Grand Master

Manages kennel operations, officers and settings.

### Committee Member

Supports operations according to assigned role.

### Visitor

Discovers and participates in runs hosted by other kennels.

### Virgin

First-time participant with onboarding guidance.

### Photographer

Captures and geo-tags photos and videos.

### Platform Administrator

Maintains the global platform, moderation and verification.

## Cross-Persona Collaboration

Hares create trails, Hashers experience them, Scribes preserve them, GMs
manage communities, Visitors expand them, Photographers document them.

## Accessibility

Offline-first, large touch targets, multilingual readiness,
screen-reader support.

## Success Metrics

Each persona has measurable success criteria focused on participation,
administration efficiency and historical preservation.
