# Purpose

This document defines the evaluation and observability framework for AI capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including quality measurement, operational monitoring, experimentation, benchmarking, and continuous improvement.

The objective is to ensure AI systems remain reliable, measurable, cost-effective, and aligned with organizational goals.

---

# Vision

Measure continuously.

Improve continuously.

Trust through evidence.

---

# Philosophy

AI quality should be demonstrated through measurable evidence rather than assumptions.

Every model, prompt, agent, and workflow should produce operational metrics that support informed decision-making.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The Evaluation Platform shall:

- Measure AI quality.
- Monitor operational health.
- Detect regressions.
- Benchmark providers.
- Support experimentation.
- Improve cost efficiency.
- Drive continuous improvement.

---

# Evaluation Scope

The platform shall evaluate:

- AI models.
- Prompt templates.
- Agents.
- Knowledge retrieval.
- Memory retrieval.
- Workflow execution.
- Tool usage.
- Human feedback.

Evaluation spans the entire Intelligence Platform.

---

# Evaluation Metrics

Representative metrics include:

- Accuracy.
- Precision.
- Recall.
- Hallucination rate.
- Citation quality.
- Task completion rate.
- Latency.
- Cost per request.
- User satisfaction.
- Human override rate.

Metrics should be standardized where possible.

---

# Operational Monitoring

The platform shall monitor:

- API availability.
- Token usage.
- Provider latency.
- Error rates.
- Workflow duration.
- Agent execution.
- Tool failures.
- Retrieval latency.

Monitoring supports operational excellence.

---

# Benchmarking

Benchmarking shall support:

- Model comparison.
- Prompt comparison.
- Agent comparison.
- Provider comparison.
- Version comparison.
- Cost comparison.

Comparisons should be repeatable and evidence-based.

---

# Experimentation

The platform shall support:

- A/B testing.
- Shadow testing.
- Canary deployments.
- Prompt experiments.
- Model experiments.
- Retrieval experiments.

Experiments enable safe improvement.

---

# Governance Integration

Evaluation results shall inform:

- Governance reviews.
- Autonomy levels.
- Workflow optimization.
- Prompt revisions.
- Model selection.
- Cost optimization.

Measurement drives governance decisions.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-061

Every production AI capability shall define measurable success metrics.

---

## BR-AI-062

Regression testing shall occur before production deployment.

---

## BR-AI-063

Evaluation datasets shall remain version controlled.

---

## BR-AI-064

Operational metrics shall be retained for trend analysis.

---

## BR-AI-065

Evaluation results shall support auditability.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Evaluation Platform succeeds when:

- AI quality becomes measurable.
- Performance regressions are detected quickly.
- Business outcomes improve.
- Costs remain observable.
- Optimization becomes data-driven.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Evaluation framework is documented.
- Monitoring strategy is defined.
- Benchmarking process is established.
