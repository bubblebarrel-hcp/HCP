# Purpose

This document defines the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) architecture for the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including review workflows, approval models, intervention mechanisms, escalation paths, and governance for AI-assisted decision-making.

The objective is to ensure intelligent automation remains accountable, transparent, and adaptable to organizational risk.

---

# Vision

Humans guide.

AI assists.

Together they achieve better outcomes.

---

# Philosophy

Automation should match the level of business risk.

AI should not replace human judgment where legal, financial, ethical, or organizational accountability requires human oversight.

The platform shall support configurable levels of autonomy.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The Human-in-the-Loop Platform shall:

- Support configurable approvals.
- Improve trust.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Increase transparency.
- Enable safe automation.
- Preserve accountability.
- Capture human feedback.

---

# Autonomy Levels

The platform shall support:

Level 0 — Human Only

Level 1 — AI Suggests

Level 2 — AI Drafts

Level 3 — AI Executes with Approval

Level 4 — AI Executes with Audit

Level 5 — Fully Autonomous

Organizations determine acceptable autonomy by workflow.

---

# Human Actions

Human participants may:

- Review.
- Approve.
- Reject.
- Modify.
- Delegate.
- Pause.
- Escalate.
- Cancel.

All interventions remain traceable.

---

# Approval Models

Representative models include:

- Single approver.
- Multiple approvers.
- Sequential approval.
- Parallel approval.
- Consensus.
- Risk-based approval.

Approval strategies remain configurable.

---

# Escalation

Escalation may occur due to:

- Timeout.
- High confidence uncertainty.
- Policy violation.
- High business impact.
- Human request.
- Workflow rules.

Escalations improve resilience.

---

# Feedback Loop

Human feedback shall support:

- AI evaluation.
- Prompt improvement.
- Workflow optimization.
- Model benchmarking.
- Knowledge refinement.

Human expertise continuously improves the platform.

---

# Governance

Every approval workflow shall define:

- Owner.
- Required approvers.
- Approval SLA.
- Escalation policy.
- Audit policy.
- Retention.

Governance supports accountability.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-051

High-risk workflows shall support configurable human approval.

---

## BR-AI-052

Human interventions shall remain auditable.

---

## BR-AI-053

Organizations shall define autonomy levels for workflows.

---

## BR-AI-054

Approval workflows shall support escalation.

---

## BR-AI-055

Human feedback shall be retained for continuous improvement.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Human-in-the-Loop Platform succeeds when:

- AI adoption increases.
- Organizational trust improves.
- Automation remains appropriate to risk.
- Human oversight remains effective.
- Feedback improves platform quality.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- HITL architecture is documented.
- Approval model is defined.
- Governance is established.
