# Purpose

This document defines the Workflow Intelligence Engine for the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including workflow modeling, orchestration, automation, event handling, human participation, and AI integration.

The objective is to enable reliable, observable, and adaptable business processes that coordinate humans, AI agents, and software systems.

---

# Vision

Intelligent workflows.

Adaptive automation.

Human-aware orchestration.

---

# Philosophy

Business processes should be modeled independently of AI.

AI may assist, optimize, or automate workflow steps, but workflow definitions remain explicit, versioned, and governed.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The Workflow Intelligence Engine shall:

- Orchestrate business processes.
- Coordinate AI agents.
- Coordinate human tasks.
- Integrate enterprise systems.
- Support long-running processes.
- Enable automation.
- Provide operational visibility.

---

# Workflow Components

Every workflow shall define:

- Trigger.
- Goal.
- Participants.
- Tasks.
- Decisions.
- Conditions.
- Approvals.
- Timeouts.
- Escalations.
- Completion criteria.

Workflow behavior remains explicit.

---

# Supported Triggers

Representative triggers include:

- User actions.
- Scheduled events.
- API calls.
- Business events.
- Agent requests.
- Webhooks.
- External integrations.

Workflows remain event-driven.

---

# Workflow Participants

Participants may include:

- Human users.
- AI agents.
- Internal services.
- External systems.
- Third-party integrations.

Participation depends on workflow requirements.

---

# Workflow States

Representative states include:

- Draft.
- Pending.
- Running.
- Waiting.
- Approved.
- Rejected.
- Completed.
- Failed.
- Cancelled.

State transitions remain traceable.

---

# Human Participation

Human tasks shall support:

- Review.
- Approval.
- Rejection.
- Delegation.
- Comments.
- Escalation.

Human involvement remains configurable.

---

# Workflow Governance

Workflows shall define:

- Owner.
- Version.
- Permissions.
- Audit requirements.
- SLA.
- Retention policy.

Governance supports operational consistency.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Workflow Intelligence Engine succeeds when:

- Business processes become reusable.
- Automation increases.
- Human oversight remains available.
- Operational visibility improves.
- Workflow reliability strengthens.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Workflow architecture is documented.
- Governance is established.
- Execution model is defined.
