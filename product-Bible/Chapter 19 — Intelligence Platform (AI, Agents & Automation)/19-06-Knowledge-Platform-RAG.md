# Purpose

This document defines the Knowledge Platform for the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including knowledge ingestion, indexing, retrieval, governance, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

The objective is to enable AI capabilities to retrieve accurate, authorized, and current organizational knowledge while reducing hallucinations and improving explainability.

---

# Vision

Knowledge that is searchable.

Knowledge that is trustworthy.

Knowledge that powers intelligence.

---

# Philosophy

Knowledge should be treated as a managed organizational asset.

AI should retrieve relevant information from trusted sources before generating responses whenever domain knowledge is required.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The Knowledge Platform shall:

- Centralize organizational knowledge.
- Improve AI accuracy.
- Reduce hallucinations.
- Support explainable responses.
- Enable semantic search.
- Respect authorization.
- Maintain knowledge freshness.

---

# Knowledge Sources

Representative sources include:

- Policies.
- Manuals.
- Documentation.
- Wikis.
- Standard Operating Procedures.
- Meeting notes.
- Product catalogs.
- Structured databases.
- Approved external sources.

Knowledge sources remain governed.

---

# Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge assets shall support:

- Registration.
- Ingestion.
- Classification.
- Indexing.
- Retrieval.
- Versioning.
- Archival.
- Deletion.

Knowledge evolves over time.

---

# Retrieval

Retrieval shall support:

- Keyword search.
- Semantic search.
- Hybrid search.
- Metadata filtering.
- Authorization-aware retrieval.
- Relevance ranking.

Retrieval should remain explainable.

---

# Retrieval-Augmented Generation

The platform shall support:

1. User query.
2. Query understanding.
3. Knowledge retrieval.
4. Context assembly.
5. Prompt construction.
6. AI generation.
7. Citation generation.
8. Response delivery.

Generation should remain grounded in retrieved knowledge.

---

# Knowledge Governance

Knowledge governance includes:

- Ownership.
- Approval.
- Version control.
- Retention.
- Access control.
- Auditability.

Governance improves trust.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-026

AI responses using organizational knowledge shall retrieve relevant context before generation where applicable.

---

## BR-AI-027

Knowledge retrieval shall respect authorization policies.

---

## BR-AI-028

Knowledge assets shall remain version controlled.

---

## BR-AI-029

Retrieved knowledge shall support traceability.

---

## BR-AI-030

Knowledge freshness shall remain measurable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Knowledge Platform succeeds when:

- AI accuracy improves.
- Hallucinations decrease.
- Organizational knowledge becomes reusable.
- Responses remain explainable.
- Users trust generated answers.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Knowledge strategy is documented.
- Retrieval model is established.
- Governance is defined.
