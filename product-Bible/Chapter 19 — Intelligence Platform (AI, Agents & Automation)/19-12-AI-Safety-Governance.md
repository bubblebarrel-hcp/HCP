# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for artificial intelligence within the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including safety controls, policy enforcement, risk management, compliance, monitoring, and continuous oversight.

The objective is to ensure AI capabilities are deployed responsibly while protecting users, organizations, and platform integrity.

---

# Vision

Safe intelligence.

Trusted automation.

Responsible innovation.

---

# Philosophy

Every AI capability shall operate within clearly defined governance boundaries.

Safety is not a feature added after deployment; it is a platform capability designed into every layer of the Intelligence Platform.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The AI Governance Platform shall:

- Protect users.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve transparency.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable responsible automation.
- Maintain accountability.
- Continuously improve governance.

---

# Governance Domains

The platform shall govern:

- AI models.
- Prompt templates.
- Agents.
- Workflows.
- Memory.
- Knowledge.
- Semantic search.
- Human oversight.
- External AI providers.
- AI APIs.

Governance applies across the entire platform.

---

# Safety Controls

Representative controls include:

- Prompt injection protection.
- Output validation.
- Data classification.
- Content moderation.
- Policy enforcement.
- Tool permission controls.
- Sensitive data protection.
- Hallucination mitigation.

Safety controls remain configurable.

---

# Risk Management

The platform shall classify AI workloads by risk.

Representative categories:

- Low Risk.
- Moderate Risk.
- High Risk.
- Critical Risk.

Risk levels influence autonomy, approvals, and monitoring.

---

# Compliance

Governance shall support:

- Audit logging.
- Data retention.
- Consent management.
- Access controls.
- Privacy requirements.
- Organizational policies.
- Regional regulations.

Compliance remains measurable.

---

# Incident Management

AI incidents shall support:

- Detection.
- Classification.
- Containment.
- Investigation.
- Resolution.
- Post-incident review.

Governance includes operational response.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

Every AI capability shall support:

- Registration.
- Review.
- Approval.
- Deployment.
- Monitoring.
- Reassessment.
- Retirement.

Lifecycle management improves consistency.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-AI-056

Every AI capability shall have an assigned owner.

---

## BR-AI-057

High-risk AI capabilities shall undergo governance review before production deployment.

---

## BR-AI-058

AI policy violations shall generate auditable events.

---

## BR-AI-059

Organizations shall configure governance policies.

---

## BR-AI-060

Governance metrics shall remain observable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The AI Governance Platform succeeds when:

- AI remains trustworthy.
- Policy violations decrease.
- Compliance improves.
- User confidence increases.
- Governance scales with platform growth.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Governance framework is documented.
- Safety controls are defined.
- Risk model is established.
