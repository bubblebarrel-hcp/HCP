# Purpose

This domain provides the administrative and governance framework for kennels and higher-level Hash organizations operating within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

It enables transparent leadership, structured membership management, institutional continuity, constitutional governance, and historical preservation.

The goal is to ensure that every kennel—from newly formed groups to decades-old institutions—can operate efficiently while preserving its unique traditions and governance.

---

# Vision

Every kennel should have a digital home.

Not just a profile.

A complete operating environment where leaders manage members, preserve institutional knowledge, conduct elections, maintain official records, and pass leadership to future generations.

---

# Philosophy

People come and go.

Kennels endure.

Governance should be transparent.

Leadership should be accountable.

History should never be lost.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Community before hierarchy.
- Transparency before authority.
- Institutional continuity.
- Respect for local traditions.
- Democratic participation where applicable.
- Preservation of governance history.

---

# Governance Scope

This domain supports governance for:

- Local Kennels
- Regional Associations
- National Associations
- Continental Councils
- Interhash Committees
- Temporary Organizing Committees
- Heritage Committees
- Working Groups

The governance model is flexible enough to accommodate regional customs while maintaining a consistent platform architecture.

---

# Core Objects

The Governance domain includes:

- Organizations
- Kennels
- Memberships
- Officers
- Committees
- Constitutions
- Policies
- Elections
- Motions
- Votes
- Meeting Minutes
- Governance Documents
- Audit Records

---

# Governance Lifecycle

Every organization progresses through a governance lifecycle:

1. Formation
2. Recognition
3. Active Governance
4. Leadership Transition
5. Historical Preservation
6. Archival (if dissolved)

Historical governance records remain accessible according to organizational policy.

# Success Criteria

The Governance domain succeeds when:

- Every kennel can manage its administration digitally.
- Leadership transitions occur smoothly.
- Institutional knowledge is preserved.
- Governance actions are transparent and auditable.
- Local autonomy is respected.
