# Purpose

This document establishes the foundational governance principles, business rules, acceptance criteria, and operational standards for Governance & Kennel Administration within HCP.

It ensures that governance remains transparent, consistent, auditable, respectful of organizational autonomy, and aligned with the long-term preservation of institutional knowledge.

---

# Vision

Good governance creates confidence.

Transparent governance creates trust.

Preserved governance creates continuity.

---

# Philosophy

HCP provides governance tools.

Each organization retains authority over its own constitution, customs, bylaws, and leadership processes.

The platform enables governance—it does not govern the community.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Organization autonomy.
- Constitution-first governance.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Historical preservation.
- Privacy by design.
- Evidence-based administration.

# Governance Business Rules

## BR-GOV-001 — Organization Autonomy

Every organization owns and manages its own governance.

Platform defaults may be customized provided they do not compromise platform security or legal obligations.

---

## BR-GOV-002 — Constitution Authority

Where governance workflows conflict with an organization's constitution, the constitution takes precedence.

HCP should allow configurable workflows that reflect local governance practices.

---

## BR-GOV-003 — Transparency

Governance actions should be visible to the appropriate audience.

Visibility must balance:

- Transparency
- Privacy
- Organizational policy
- Legal requirements

---

## BR-GOV-004 — Accountability

Every significant governance action shall have:

- Responsible actor
- Timestamp
- Supporting evidence
- Audit record

No governance action should become anonymous after execution.

---

## BR-GOV-005 — Leadership Stewardship

Officer roles represent stewardship responsibilities rather than permanent authority.

Leadership history remains part of the organization's institutional memory.

---

## BR-GOV-006 — Democratic Participation

Voting systems shall support constitutional flexibility while preserving:

- Fairness
- Eligibility
- Ballot integrity
- Verifiable outcomes

---

## BR-GOV-007 — Committee Governance

Committees exist to distribute responsibility.

Committee membership, mandates, and decisions remain historically preserved.

---

## BR-GOV-008 — Institutional Memory

Governance records should remain understandable decades after their creation.

Future leaders should be able to understand:

- What happened.
- Why it happened.
- Who participated.
- Which documents supported the decision.

---

## BR-GOV-009 — Privacy

Personal information shall only be visible where legitimately required.

Historical records should preserve institutional continuity while respecting applicable privacy requirements.

---

## BR-GOV-010 — AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence may assist governance by:

- Summarizing.
- Searching.
- Explaining.
- Translating.
- Identifying inconsistencies.

AI shall never replace constitutional decision-making or exercise governance authority.

---

## BR-GOV-011 — Historical Preservation

The Governance Archive permanently preserves:

- Constitutions
- Policies
- Elections
- Officer appointments
- Meeting minutes
- Motions
- Decisions
- Annual reports
- Heritage documents

Subject to applicable law, these records should remain accessible for future generations.

---

## BR-GOV-012 — Platform Neutrality

HCP shall remain neutral regarding:

- Political opinions
- Internal organizational disagreements
- Election outcomes
- Constitutional interpretations

The platform records governance—it does not determine it.

---

# Permission Principles

Permissions shall follow:

- Least privilege.
- Separation of duties.
- Delegated authority.
- Time-limited elevation where appropriate.
- Complete auditability.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Governance domain is complete when:

- Organization structures are documented.
- Membership lifecycle is defined.
- Officer management is complete.
- Constitutions are version-controlled.
- Committees are supported.
- Meetings and elections are configurable.
- Voting is transparent and auditable.
- Governance archives are preserved.
- Permissions follow RBAC.
- Evidence Records support governance decisions.

---

# Non-Functional Expectations

The Governance subsystem shall provide:

- High availability.
- Strong consistency.
- Fine-grained authorization.
- Immutable audit logging.
- Secure document storage.
- Disaster recovery.
- Long-term archival durability.
- Explainable AI assistance.
