# Purpose

This document defines how organizations conduct elections, meetings, motions, resolutions, voting, and governance decisions within HCP.

The objective is to ensure governance processes are transparent, configurable, auditable, and historically preserved while respecting each organization's constitution and traditions.

---

# Vision

Every governance decision should be:

- Fair.
- Transparent.
- Documented.
- Auditable.
- Historically preserved.

The platform supports governance—it never replaces the organization's constitutional authority.

---

# Philosophy

Good governance is not simply about voting.

It is about informed participation.

Every proposal has a history.

Every decision has context.

Every vote contributes to the organization's future.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Constitution-first.
- Democratic flexibility.
- Transparent processes.
- Privacy where required.
- Permanent historical record.

---

# Governance Activities

Supported governance activities include:

- Elections
- Appointments
- General Meetings
- Executive Meetings
- Extraordinary Meetings
- Motions
- Resolutions
- Constitutional Amendments
- Committee Decisions
- Advisory Polls

Organizations may define additional governance workflows.

---

# FR-GOV-021 — Meeting Management

Organizations may schedule:

- Annual General Meetings (AGMs)
- Extraordinary General Meetings (EGMs)
- Executive Committee Meetings
- Committee Meetings
- Strategic Planning Sessions
- Officer Handover Meetings

Each meeting includes:

- Agenda
- Supporting documents
- Attendee list
- Quorum requirements
- Minutes
- Action items

---

# FR-GOV-022 — Agenda Builder

Meeting organizers may create structured agendas.

Agenda items support:

- Presenter
- Time allocation
- Background documents
- Discussion notes
- Motions
- Decisions
- Attachments

Agenda changes are versioned until the meeting begins.

---

# FR-GOV-023 — Motion Management

Members may submit governance motions where permitted.

Each motion records:

- Title
- Description
- Sponsor
- Supporting documents
- Related constitutional clauses
- Discussion period
- Voting method
- Final outcome

Approved motions become part of the governance archive.

---

# FR-GOV-024 — Election Management

Organizations may configure elections for:

- Individual officer positions
- Committee appointments
- Multi-seat elections
- Confirmations
- Recalls

Election workflows support constitutional variations.

---

# FR-GOV-025 — Voting Methods

Supported voting methods include:

- Show of hands
- Secret ballot
- Anonymous digital ballot
- Ranked-choice voting
- Approval voting
- Weighted voting (where constitutionally permitted)
- Consensus confirmation

Voting methods are selected according to organizational rules.

---

# FR-GOV-026 — Eligibility Validation

Before voting, HCP automatically validates:

- Membership status
- Voting eligibility
- Officer eligibility
- Conflict-of-interest restrictions
- Constitutional requirements

Ineligible participants receive a clear explanation.

---

# FR-GOV-027 — Meeting Minutes

Minutes may include:

- Attendance
- Quorum confirmation
- Agenda discussions
- Decisions
- Votes
- Action items
- Assigned owners
- Deadlines

Minutes are versioned until formally approved.

---

# FR-GOV-028 — Action Item Tracking

Governance decisions generate action items.

Each action item includes:

- Owner
- Due date
- Priority
- Related motion
- Status
- Completion history

Outstanding governance actions appear on leadership dashboards.

---

# FR-GOV-029 — Decision Archive

Every governance decision becomes part of the permanent archive.

Archived records include:

- Proposal
- Discussion summary
- Vote
- Effective date
- Related policies
- Related constitutional amendments
- Historical impact

Decision archives support long-term institutional learning.

---

# FR-GOV-030 — Governance Analytics

Organizations may review:

- Meeting attendance
- Voting participation
- Motion outcomes
- Committee effectiveness
- Officer turnover
- Governance workload
- Policy amendment trends

Analytics assist leaders without ranking individual members.

---

# Business Principles

Decisions should be informed.

Voting should be trusted.

Governance history should remain accessible.

Leadership should always be accountable.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Meetings are supported.
- Elections are configurable.
- Voting methods are flexible.
- Decision archives are permanent.
- Governance analytics are available.
