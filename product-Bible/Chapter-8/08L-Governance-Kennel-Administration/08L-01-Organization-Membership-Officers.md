# Purpose

This document defines how organizations, kennels, memberships, officers, and leadership structures are represented within HCP.

The goal is to provide a flexible governance model capable of supporting both informal local kennels and highly structured national or international organizations.

---

# Vision

Every organization should have a clearly defined identity.

Every member should understand their role.

Every officer should have transparent responsibilities.

Every leadership transition should preserve institutional continuity.

---

# Philosophy

Organizations are living communities.

Leadership is stewardship.

Membership is participation.

Authority exists to serve the community.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Clear organizational structure.
- Transparent membership lifecycle.
- Accountable leadership.
- Flexible governance models.
- Long-term institutional continuity.

---

# Organization Types

Supported organization types include:

- Local Kennel
- Regional Association
- National Association
- Continental Organization
- International Committee
- Working Group
- Heritage Foundation
- Temporary Event Committee

Organizations may define parent-child relationships where appropriate.

---

# FR-GOV-001 — Organization Profiles

Every organization maintains an official profile containing:

- Name
- Official abbreviation
- Founding date
- Founding members
- Geographic location
- Meeting schedule
- Home trail
- Contact information
- Branding assets
- Website
- Social links
- Constitution
- Status

Profiles may also include historical summaries and notable achievements.

---

# FR-GOV-002 — Membership Lifecycle

Supported membership states include:

- Applicant
- Pending Review
- Active
- Visiting Member
- Honorary Member
- Inactive
- Suspended
- Archived

State transitions follow organization-defined governance rules.

---

# FR-GOV-003 — Membership Applications

Organizations may configure application workflows including:

- Open applications
- Invitation-only membership
- Sponsor requirements
- Approval committees
- Interview process
- Trial participation
- Membership fees (optional)

All application decisions are recorded.

---

# FR-GOV-004 — Officer Roles

Organizations define officer positions such as:

- Grand Master
- Religious Adviser
- On Sec
- Hash Cash
- Trail Master
- Scribe
- Beer Master
- Web Master
- Safety Officer
- Membership Officer

Organizations may create additional custom roles.

---

# FR-GOV-005 — Officer Responsibilities

Each officer role includes:

- Description
- Responsibilities
- Permissions
- Term duration
- Appointment method
- Reporting relationships
- Required qualifications (optional)

Responsibilities are visible to all members.

---

# FR-GOV-006 — Leadership Timeline

Every officer appointment creates a historical record including:

- Officer
- Position
- Start date
- End date
- Appointment method
- Successor
- Major initiatives

The timeline becomes part of the organization's permanent history.

---

# FR-GOV-007 — Organizational Directory

Members may browse:

- Current officers
- Committee members
- Active volunteers
- Honorary members
- Founding members
- Past leaders

Visibility respects privacy settings and organizational policies.

---

# FR-GOV-008 — Membership Directory

Organizations maintain a searchable member directory with support for:

- Hash name
- Home kennel
- Membership status
- Roles
- Join date
- Skills
- Languages
- Certifications (where applicable)

Advanced filters assist organizers in finding members for specific responsibilities.

---

# FR-GOV-009 — Organizational Branding

Organizations may customize:

- Logo
- Banner
- Colors
- Motto
- Founding story
- Welcome message
- Public profile

Branding automatically appears across relevant HCP modules.

---

# FR-GOV-010 — Institutional History

Organizations maintain a living historical record including:

- Founding documents
- Milestones
- Leadership succession
- Landmark events
- Awards
- Historical photographs
- Oral histories
- Run Capsule collections

Institutional history integrates with the Global Hash Atlas and Community Graph.

---

# Business Principles

Organizations should remain understandable.

Leadership should remain accountable.

Institutional history should never depend on a single person's memory.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Organizational hierarchy is defined.
- Membership lifecycle is documented.
- Officer management is complete.
- Leadership history is preserved.
- Institutional profiles are established.
