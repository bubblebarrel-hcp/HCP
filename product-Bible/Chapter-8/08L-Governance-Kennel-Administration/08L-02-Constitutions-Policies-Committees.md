# Purpose

This document defines how constitutions, bylaws, policies, committee structures, governance records, and institutional documents are created, maintained, versioned, and preserved within HCP.

The objective is to provide every organization with a secure, transparent, and historically accurate governance repository.

---

# Vision

Every governance decision should be traceable.

Every policy should have a history.

Every committee should have defined responsibilities.

Every constitutional amendment should remain permanently preserved.

---

# Philosophy

Governance documents are living records.

They evolve through community consensus.

Nothing should be silently overwritten.

Institutional memory should outlive individual leaders.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Version everything.
- Preserve history.
- Make governance transparent.
- Respect organizational autonomy.
- Support collaborative drafting.

---

# Governance Document Types

Supported document categories include:

- Constitution
- Bylaws
- Standing Orders
- Code of Conduct
- Membership Policy
- Financial Policy
- Event Policy
- Safety Policy
- Election Rules
- Committee Charters
- Meeting Minutes
- Annual Reports
- Strategic Plans
- Historical Records

Organizations may define additional document types.

---

# FR-GOV-011 — Constitution Management

Organizations maintain one active constitution while preserving all previous versions.

Each version records:

- Version number
- Publication date
- Effective date
- Authors
- Approvers
- Amendment summary
- Supporting vote
- Superseded version

Historical constitutions remain permanently accessible.

---

# FR-GOV-012 — Article-Based Structure

Constitutions are organized into:

- Articles
- Sections
- Clauses
- Appendices

Individual clauses may be referenced throughout the platform.

Example:

Article IV

Section 2

Clause 4.3

Officer Eligibility

This structure enables precise linking from policies, elections, and governance workflows.

---

# FR-GOV-013 — Version History

Every governance document maintains a complete revision history.

Each revision records:

- Editor
- Timestamp
- Summary of changes
- Review comments
- Approval status

Users may compare any two historical versions.

---

# FR-GOV-014 — Amendment Workflow

Organizations may configure amendment workflows.

Typical process:

1. Proposal Submitted
2. Review Period
3. Community Discussion
4. Committee Recommendation
5. Vote
6. Ratification
7. Publication
8. Effective Date

Every stage is audited.

---

# FR-GOV-015 — Committee Management

Organizations may create committees including:

- Executive Committee
- Finance Committee
- Events Committee
- Membership Committee
- Heritage Committee
- Safety Committee
- Disciplinary Committee
- Election Committee

Each committee records:

- Members
- Chairperson
- Responsibilities
- Term
- Meeting schedule

---

# FR-GOV-016 — Committee Workspace

Committees receive dedicated workspaces containing:

- Tasks
- Documents
- Agendas
- Minutes
- Decisions
- Action items
- Calendar
- Shared files

Committee workspaces remain part of the institutional archive.

---

# FR-GOV-017 — Governance Repository

Organizations maintain a searchable governance repository containing:

- Constitutions
- Policies
- Meeting minutes
- Reports
- Resolutions
- Committee records
- Historical documents

Advanced search supports filtering by document type, author, committee, date, and keyword.

---

# FR-GOV-018 — Linked Governance

Governance documents may reference:

- Officer roles
- Events
- Policies
- Committees
- Membership rules
- Historical decisions

Cross-references improve discoverability and reduce duplication.

---

# FR-GOV-019 — Annual Reporting

Organizations may publish annual reports containing:

- Membership statistics
- Officer reports
- Financial summaries
- Event highlights
- Strategic initiatives
- Community impact
- Heritage projects

Reports become permanent historical records.

---

# FR-GOV-020 — Governance Dashboard

Officers access a dashboard summarizing:

- Pending amendments
- Committee activity
- Upcoming meetings
- Expiring officer terms
- Outstanding actions
- Recently published documents
- Governance health indicators

The dashboard acts as the operational center for organizational leadership.

---
