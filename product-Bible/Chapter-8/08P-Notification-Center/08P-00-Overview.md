# Purpose

The Notification Center provides the unified attention management infrastructure for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Rather than allowing every platform service to deliver notifications independently, all communication requiring member attention is coordinated through a shared Notification Center that intelligently prioritizes, schedules, groups, and delivers notifications.

The Notification Center ensures members remain informed without becoming overwhelmed.

---

# Vision

Every notification deserves attention.

No notification should compete unfairly for it.

---

# Philosophy

Attention is limited.

Trust is earned.

Silence is sometimes the best notification.

---

# Guiding Principles

- User-first attention management.
- Relevance over volume.
- Context-aware delivery.
- Respect quiet time.
- Explainable prioritization.
- Cross-platform consistency.
- Intelligent batching.

# Success Criteria

The Notification Center succeeds when:

- Members receive fewer but more meaningful notifications.
- Critical information is never missed.
- Routine updates are intelligently grouped.
- User preferences are respected.
- Notification fatigue is minimized.
