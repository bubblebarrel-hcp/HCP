# Purpose

This document establishes governance policies, analytics capabilities, federation support, business rules, and operational standards for the Notification Center.

The Notification Center protects member attention while ensuring important information reaches the appropriate audience through transparent, explainable, and configurable delivery.

---

# Vision

Attention is a shared community resource.

Use it wisely.

---

# Philosophy

Notifications should strengthen participation.

They should never become background noise.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Trust through restraint.
- Transparent automation.
- Member control.
- Organizational flexibility.
- Platform consistency.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# FR-NOT-021 — Notification Governance

Organizations define policies governing:

- Delivery channels
- Default priorities
- Reminder schedules
- Escalation rules
- Emergency overrides
- Digest frequency
- Quiet hours

Policies are version-controlled and auditable.

---

# FR-NOT-022 — Notification Analytics

Analytics may include:

- Delivery success
- Read rates
- Action completion
- Reminder effectiveness
- Escalation frequency
- Digest engagement
- Notification fatigue indicators

Analytics are used to improve communication rather than monitor individuals.

---

# FR-NOT-023 — Notification Federation

Trusted organizations may coordinate notification policies for shared initiatives.

Examples include:

- Interhash announcements
- National association updates
- Regional emergency alerts
- Joint event coordination

Federated notifications respect local member preferences unless overridden by approved emergency policies.

---

# FR-NOT-024 — Emergency Notification Framework

Emergency notifications support:

- Immediate delivery
- Multi-channel broadcasting
- Delivery confirmation
- Retry policies
- Escalation chains
- Incident tracking

Emergency events are auditable and time-stamped.

---

# FR-NOT-025 — AI Notification Governance

Organizations control AI capabilities including:

- Draft reminders
- Suggested delivery times
- Digest generation
- Notification grouping
- Escalation recommendations
- Attention optimization

AI recommendations remain explainable and configurable.

---

# FR-NOT-026 — Notification Auditing

Audit records include:

- Notification creation
- Delivery attempts
- Preference changes
- Automation triggers
- Escalation actions
- Policy modifications

Audit records integrate with the Evidence Record Service.

---

# FR-NOT-027 — Business Continuity

The Notification Center supports:

- Queue persistence
- Retry mechanisms
- Geographic redundancy
- Disaster recovery
- Delivery replay
- Failover routing

Critical notifications remain reliable during infrastructure failures.

---

# FR-NOT-028 — Notification Lifecycle

Notifications progress through lifecycle stages:

- Created
- Scheduled
- Queued
- Delivered
- Acknowledged
- Completed
- Expired
- Archived

Lifecycle history remains permanently auditable.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-NOT-001

Every notification must reference a valid platform object or workflow.

---

## BR-NOT-002

Notification priorities must follow platform-wide standards.

---

## BR-NOT-003

Members retain ultimate control over non-emergency notification preferences.

---

## BR-NOT-004

AI-generated notifications require explainable reasoning.

---

## BR-NOT-005

Emergency notifications may override preferences only under approved organizational policies.

---

## BR-NOT-006

Notification grouping should be preferred whenever it reduces interruption without increasing risk.

---

## BR-NOT-007

Attention optimization must never suppress critical safety, governance, or legal notifications.

---

## BR-NOT-008

Notification policies are version-controlled and historically preserved.
