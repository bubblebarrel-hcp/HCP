# Purpose

This document defines the Hash Time Machine, an immersive replay experience that reconstructs a Hash Run from the preserved Run Capsule.

Rather than simply displaying archived information, the Hash Time Machine allows users to relive the sequence of events as they unfolded.

---

# Vision

A Run Capsule preserves history.

The Hash Time Machine brings that history back to life.

Users should feel as though they are following the trail alongside the Hashers, discovering Beer Checks, hearing Circle songs, and reading the Trail Report as the day unfolds.

---

# Philosophy

History should not remain frozen.

It should be experienced.

The Hash Time Machine reconstructs events while preserving historical accuracy.

No events are invented or reordered.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Authenticity before spectacle.
- Replay what happened, not what might have happened.
- Respect privacy settings.
- Celebrate community participation.
- Preserve context.

---

# Replay Modes

Users may choose from several replay experiences:

- Guided Replay
- Free Exploration
- Timeline Scrubbing
- Story Replay
- Map Replay
- Media Replay
- Circle Replay

Each mode uses the same historical data while presenting it differently.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-001 — Guided Replay

## Priority

Critical

The Guided Replay automatically progresses through the Run Session.

Replay includes:

- Trail progression
- Story narration
- Story Cards
- Photos
- Videos
- Beer Checks
- Circle events
- Awards

Users may pause, resume, or jump to any moment.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-002 — Interactive Map Replay

The map animates the trail chronologically.

As playback progresses:

- The route is drawn.
- Waypoints appear.
- Beer Checks become active.
- Story Cards open automatically.
- Media becomes available at the corresponding location.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-003 — Narrative Synchronization

The official Trail Report synchronizes with playback.

As events occur:

- Relevant paragraphs are highlighted.
- Quotes appear.
- Photos expand inline.
- Comments become visible.
- Timeline advances automatically.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-004 — Media Playback

Media appears according to its original timestamp.

Supported media includes:

- Photos
- Videos
- Audio
- Voice notes
- Ambient recordings (future)

Playback never changes the original timestamps.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-005 — Event Overlay

Users may enable overlays such as:

- Weather
- Elevation
- Attendance
- GPS statistics
- Trail difficulty
- Digital Chalk
- Trail DNA

Overlays are optional.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-006 — Replay Speed

Replay supports:

- Real Time
- 2×
- 5×
- 10×
- Story Mode (event-driven rather than time-driven)

Users may also scrub directly to any point in the timeline.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-007 — Historical Context

At any point during replay, users may explore:

- Related Story Cards
- Historical reports
- Similar runs
- Anniversary events
- Participant profiles

Replay pauses while historical references are explored.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-008 — Shared Memories

Participants may attach additional memories after publication, including:

- Personal reflections
- Additional photographs
- Scanned memorabilia
- Anniversary recollections

These appear as supplemental layers and never modify the original historical record.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-009 — Privacy-Aware Replay

Replay respects:

- Media permissions
- Participant privacy
- Kennel visibility
- Regional legal requirements

Restricted content is omitted gracefully without disrupting playback.

---

# FR-TIMEMACHINE-010 — Educational Mode

Kennels may create curated replay experiences for:

- New member orientation
- Historic anniversary runs
- Memorial events
- Charity runs
- Interhash highlights

Educational Mode allows additional narration while preserving the underlying historical record.

---

# Business Principles

- Replay should educate and entertain.
- Authenticity outweighs visual effects.
- History should remain accessible.
- Every participant should recognize the day they experienced.
- Future generations should understand the traditions they inherit.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Guided Replay exists.
- Timeline synchronization functions correctly.
- Maps, stories, and media remain synchronized.
- Privacy is respected.
- Educational replay is supported.
