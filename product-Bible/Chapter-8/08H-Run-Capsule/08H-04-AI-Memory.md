# Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence assists in preserving, summarizing, organizing and discovering memories contained within a Run Capsule.

AI exists to enhance historical preservation—not replace human storytelling.

The Hash Scribe remains the official author of history.

---

# Philosophy

People create memories.

Technology remembers them.

AI helps people rediscover them.

---

# Vision

Years after a Run has finished, a Hasher should be able to open a Run Capsule and instantly understand:

- what happened
- who attended
- what made it memorable
- why the run became significant

without reading hundreds of comments or browsing thousands of photographs.

---

# Guiding Principles

- AI never rewrites history.
- AI never invents facts.
- Human stories remain authoritative.
- Transparency is mandatory.
- Every generated insight links back to evidence.

---

# Memory Layers

Each Run Capsule contains multiple layers of memory.

## Layer 1

Raw Events

Examples:

- GPS
- Photos
- Videos
- Comments
- Beer Checks
- Attendance
- Story Cards

---

## Layer 2

Structured Story

Created by:

- Trail Report
- Story Timeline
- Story Graph

---

## Layer 3

AI Understanding

Generated insights include:

- Event summaries
- Key moments
- Themes
- Milestones
- Traditions

---

## Layer 4

Historical Connections

AI discovers links between:

- Previous runs
- Similar trails
- Anniversary events
- Repeat visitors
- Shared traditions

---

# FR-MEMORY-001

Automatic Run Summary

Priority:

Critical

After publication AI generates a concise summary including:

- Run overview
- Trail highlights
- Major events
- Attendance
- Awards
- Beer Checks
- Community moments

The summary always references the official Trail Report.

---

# FR-MEMORY-002

One Minute Summary

Users may request:

> Tell me about Run #842 in one minute.

The generated summary contains only verified historical information.

---

# FR-MEMORY-003

Long-form Narrative

AI may produce:

- Magazine-style articles
- Newsletter summaries
- Anniversary retrospectives
- Historical essays

These are clearly marked as AI-generated derivative works.

---

# FR-MEMORY-004

Memory Highlights

The platform identifies notable moments such as:

- funniest comment
- highest attendance
- longest trail
- first Beer Check
- final Circle
- memorable awards

Highlights link directly to supporting evidence.

---

# FR-MEMORY-005

Photo Story

AI may automatically arrange photographs into a visual story.

Each section includes:

- captions
- locations
- timestamps
- related Story Cards

---

# FR-MEMORY-006

Video Story

Future versions may assemble short highlight reels using:

- approved videos
- captions
- map animation
- music licensed by the kennel

Original media remains untouched.

---

# FR-MEMORY-007

Anniversary Reflection

On future anniversaries AI may prepare:

"Five years ago today..."

including:

- original Trail Report
- photos
- participants
- route
- weather
- related memories

Publication requires kennel approval if shared publicly.

---

# FR-MEMORY-008

Memory Search

Users may ask natural-language questions:

Show every run that crossed this river.

When was the first Red Dress Run?

Which runs did this Hare lay?

Find every run mentioning this pub.

Results always cite original records.

---

# FR-MEMORY-009

Historical Themes

AI may identify recurring themes including:

- Halloween
- Christmas
- Charity
- Memorial
- Red Dress
- Full Moon
- Beach Run

Themes enrich historical discovery.

---

# FR-MEMORY-010

Community Memory

Participants may contribute:

- reflections
- stories
- interviews
- anniversary comments

These become supplemental memories attached to the Run Capsule.

The official Trail Report remains unchanged.

---

# Business Principles

AI preserves.

People remember.

Scribes tell stories.

History belongs to the community.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- AI summaries exist.
- Memory search works.
- Anniversary reflections are supported.
- Community memories are preserved.
- Historical integrity is maintained.
