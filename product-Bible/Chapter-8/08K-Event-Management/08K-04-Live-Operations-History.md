# Purpose

This document defines how HCP supports live event communications, operational awareness, participant engagement, and the transition from an active event into a permanent historical record.

The objective is to ensure that every event is well coordinated while preserving its legacy for future generations.

---

# Vision

An event should not disappear when the closing circle ends.

Its stories, photos, trails, achievements, and community moments should become part of the living history of the Hash.

---

# Philosophy

Communicate clearly.

Respond quickly.

Preserve automatically.

Celebrate together.

---

# Guiding Principles

- One trusted communication channel.
- Context-aware notifications.
- Operational transparency.
- Respectful communication.
- Automatic historical preservation.

---

# Communication Channels

Supported channels include:

- In-app announcements
- Push notifications
- Email
- SMS (optional)
- Digital noticeboards
- Public event pages
- Organizer broadcasts

Each event controls its own communication preferences.

---

# FR-EVENT-031 — Event Announcements

Organizers may publish announcements targeted by:

- All attendees
- Volunteers
- Committee members
- Specific ticket types
- Activity participants
- Language
- Geographic location (where appropriate)

Announcements support scheduled publishing.

---

# FR-EVENT-032 — Live Schedule Updates

Participants receive real-time updates when:

- Activities change location
- Start times shift
- Transportation changes
- Weather impacts plans
- Safety notices are issued
- Activities are cancelled or added

Updates include clear explanations and revised instructions.

---

# FR-EVENT-033 — Context-Aware Notifications

The platform may notify participants about:

- Upcoming activities
- Boarding times
- Check-in reminders
- Merchandise collection
- Passport stamp opportunities
- Nearby information desks
- Closing deadlines

Notifications respect user preferences and quiet hours where appropriate.

---

# FR-EVENT-034 — Live Operations Feed

Authorized organizers view a live operational feed containing:

- Incident updates
- Volunteer reports
- Venue status
- Transportation updates
- Medical notifications
- Weather advisories

Operational messages are prioritized by severity.

---

# FR-EVENT-035 — Participant Activity Feed

Participants may view a chronological event feed including:

- Official announcements
- Schedule changes
- Trail openings
- Photo highlights
- Community milestones
- Award ceremonies
- Closing reminders

The feed focuses on useful event information rather than social engagement.

---

# FR-EVENT-036 — Automatic Run Capsule Creation

When an event concludes, HCP initiates a Run Capsule workflow.

Suggested content includes:

- Official programme
- Trail maps
- Attendance summaries
- Photos
- Videos
- Trail Reports
- Award recipients
- Volunteer acknowledgements

Organizers review and publish the final Run Capsule collection.

---

# FR-EVENT-037 — Event Timeline

Every event maintains a permanent timeline including:

- Planning milestones
- Registration phases
- Live updates
- Activities completed
- Incidents resolved
- Closing ceremony
- Publication of Run Capsules

The timeline becomes part of the historical archive.

---

# FR-EVENT-038 — Community Contributions

Participants may contribute:

- Photos
- Videos
- Trail Reports
- Personal stories
- Oral history recordings
- Reflections

Contributions are moderated according to event governance.

---

# FR-EVENT-039 — Event Analytics

After the event, organizers may review:

- Attendance
- Registration trends
- Check-in performance
- Volunteer participation
- Transportation performance
- Incident statistics
- Communication effectiveness
- Participant feedback

Analytics support continuous improvement.

---

# FR-EVENT-040 — Event Archive

Every completed event becomes part of the HCP archive.

The archive includes:

- Official programme
- Run Capsule collection
- Trails
- Media
- Awards
- Historical summaries
- Passport integration
- Atlas references
- Community Graph updates

Archives remain searchable for future generations.

---

# Business Principles

Communicate with purpose.

Operate with confidence.

Preserve with care.

Every event deserves to become part of community history.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Live communications are documented.
- Operational awareness is supported.
- Run Capsule creation is automated.
- Historical archives are defined.
- Event analytics are available.
