# Purpose

This document defines the operational capabilities required to coordinate transportation, accommodation, volunteers, equipment, medical support, and logistics for Hash events of any scale.

The goal is to provide organizers with a unified operational command system while giving participants a seamless event experience.

---

# Vision

Every attendee should know where they need to be.

Every volunteer should know what they need to do.

Every organizer should know what still needs attention.

---

# Philosophy

Successful events are built on invisible logistics.

The best logistics are those participants never notice because everything works.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Operational clarity.
- Real-time coordination.
- Self-service where appropriate.
- Offline resilience.
- Continuous visibility.

---

# Logistics Domains

The platform supports:

- Transportation
- Accommodation
- Volunteer Operations
- Equipment
- Medical Services
- Hospitality
- Incident Management
- Lost & Found
- Vendor Operations

---

# FR-EVENT-021 — Transportation Management

Organizers may manage:

- Airport pickups
- Shuttle services
- Bus fleets
- Trail transport
- Driver assignments
- Vehicle capacities
- Departure schedules
- Return journeys

Vehicles support live occupancy tracking during the event.

---

# FR-EVENT-022 — Participant Transportation

Participants may view:

- Pickup location
- Pickup time
- Assigned vehicle
- Driver contact (where appropriate)
- Route updates
- Return schedule

Changes trigger notifications automatically.

---

# FR-EVENT-023 — Accommodation Management

Support accommodation assignments including:

- Hotels
- Guest houses
- Campgrounds
- Host families
- Shared rooms
- Premium upgrades

Accommodation records include:

- Check-in dates
- Check-out dates
- Room allocations
- Accessibility requirements
- Special requests

---

# FR-EVENT-024 — Volunteer Management

Volunteers may:

- Apply for roles
- Select availability
- Receive assignments
- View schedules
- Check in for shifts
- Report task completion

Volunteer coordinators may monitor staffing levels in real time.

---

# FR-EVENT-025 — Equipment Tracking

Manage event assets including:

- PA systems
- Generators
- Tents
- Water stations
- Medical kits
- Radios
- Tables
- Signage
- Merchandise stock

Equipment supports assignment, status tracking, and return verification.

---

# FR-EVENT-026 — Medical Operations

Medical teams may manage:

- First aid stations
- Ambulance availability
- Medical volunteers
- Incident reports
- Emergency contacts
- Evacuation plans

Sensitive medical information is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

---

# FR-EVENT-027 — Hospitality Operations

Manage services such as:

- Welcome desks
- Information booths
- VIP reception
- Guest relations
- International delegations
- Lost visitor assistance

Hospitality teams have access only to the information required for their duties.

---

# FR-EVENT-028 — Incident Management

Authorized users may record operational incidents.

Examples include:

- Transport delays
- Lost property
- Medical events
- Weather disruptions
- Venue issues
- Safety concerns

Incidents include:

- Timestamp
- Location
- Severity
- Status
- Assigned responders
- Resolution notes

---

# FR-EVENT-029 — Operations Dashboard

Organizers view a live dashboard showing:

- Volunteer coverage
- Vehicle status
- Hotel occupancy
- Incident queue
- Equipment availability
- Medical readiness
- Activity readiness

The dashboard highlights critical issues requiring attention.

---

# FR-EVENT-030 — Offline Operations

Core operational functions continue working when internet connectivity is unavailable.

Supported offline capabilities include:

- Participant lookup
- Check-in
- Volunteer schedules
- Incident logging
- Equipment tracking

Data synchronizes automatically when connectivity returns.

---

# Business Principles

Operations should be predictable.

Volunteers should be empowered.

Participants should experience confidence rather than confusion.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Transportation is managed.
- Accommodation workflows exist.
- Volunteer operations are documented.
- Medical coordination is supported.
- Offline resilience is defined.
