# Purpose

This document defines how HCP analyzes, preserves, and presents trail-related intelligence while respecting the traditions and values of the Hash House Harriers.

The purpose is not competition.

The purpose is understanding, storytelling, planning, and preserving the unique identity of each Hare and each kennel.

---

# Philosophy

Every Hare leaves a signature.

Some trails are remembered for impossible checks.

Some for beautiful scenery.

Some for relentless hills.

Some for unforgettable Beer Checks.

Trail DNA exists to preserve those signatures—not to score them.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Celebrate creativity over competition.
- Preserve historical context.
- Avoid gamification that changes Hash culture.
- Surface meaningful insights.
- Make analytics useful for future planning.

---

# Core Concepts

## Trail DNA

A long-term profile describing the characteristics of a Hare's trails.

Trail DNA evolves naturally as more trails are completed.

It is descriptive, not prescriptive.

---

## Kennel DNA

A profile that reflects the traditions and planning patterns of a kennel over time.

Examples include:

- Average trail length
- Typical terrain
- Common themes
- Beer Check culture
- Visitor participation
- Seasonal preferences

---

## Historical Intelligence

Historical Intelligence helps Hares and officers learn from previous runs without encouraging repetition.

---

# FR-DNA-001 — Generate Trail DNA

## Priority

High

The platform shall automatically generate a Trail DNA profile for each Hare based on completed trails.

Example characteristics include:

- Average distance
- Preferred terrain
- Elevation profile
- Typical duration
- Beer Check frequency
- False trail frequency
- Split trail usage
- Scenic waypoint usage

Trail DNA shall update after each completed Run Capsule.

---

# FR-DNA-002 — Generate Kennel DNA

The platform shall generate an evolving profile for each kennel.

Insights may include:

- Annual activity
- Attendance trends
- Trail diversity
- Geographic coverage
- Visitor participation
- Historic milestones

---

# FR-DNA-003 — Trail Comparison

Authorized Hares may compare trails using metrics such as:

- Distance
- Elevation
- Terrain
- Duration
- Waypoints
- Beer Stops
- Hazard density

Comparison is intended for planning and learning rather than ranking.

---

# FR-DNA-004 — Historical Explorer

The platform shall provide tools to explore historical trails.

Filters include:

- Year
- Hare
- Kennel
- Country
- Theme
- Terrain
- Difficulty
- Trail type

Historical exploration supports inspiration and education.

---

# FR-DNA-005 — AI Planning Insights

Where enabled, AI may analyze previous trails to provide suggestions such as:

- Areas rarely explored
- Repeated route segments
- Typical Beer Check spacing
- Seasonal route suitability
- Potential safety concerns

Recommendations remain optional.

---

# FR-DNA-006 — Signature Trail Recognition

The platform may identify trails that become historically significant within a kennel.

Examples include:

- Anniversary Runs
- Charity Runs
- Interhash Trails
- Landmark Trails

Recognition is informational and not competitive.

---

# FR-DNA-007 — Trail Evolution

The platform may visualize how a Hare's trail style changes over time.

Visualizations may include:

- Distance trends
- Terrain diversity
- Elevation changes
- Planning complexity
- Beer Check patterns

---

# FR-DNA-008 — Planning Intelligence

Trail Studio may recommend planning considerations based on historical data.

Examples:

- Frequently congested start areas
- Popular regroup locations
- Typical completion times
- Weather patterns
- Accessibility considerations

Recommendations assist planning without dictating it.

---

# FR-DNA-009 — Community Insights

Kennels may view aggregate insights including:

- Total trails laid
- Historic attendance
- Geographic expansion
- Volunteer participation
- Trail variety

Individual privacy settings shall always be respected.

---

# FR-DNA-010 — Preservation

Trail intelligence becomes part of the long-term historical archive.

Analytics may evolve over time, but original trail data remains unchanged.

---

# Business Principles

- Analytics should celebrate stories, not create competition.
- Historical insight should improve future planning.
- Every Hare has a unique style worth preserving.
- Data belongs to the community that created it.
- Context matters more than numbers.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Trail DNA profiles are generated.
- Kennel DNA is maintained.
- Historical exploration is supported.
- AI insights assist planning.
- Analytics preserve culture rather than distort it.
