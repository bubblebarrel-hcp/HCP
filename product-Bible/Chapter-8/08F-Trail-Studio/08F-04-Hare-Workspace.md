# Purpose

This document defines the Hare Workspace, a dedicated planning environment where Hares collaborate to design, review, and prepare memorable trails while preserving secrecy and respecting Hash traditions.

The Hare Workspace is not an administration screen.

It is a creative studio designed specifically for trail planning.

---

# Vision

The Hare Workspace should feel like sitting around a table with fellow Hares planning the next great Hash.

It combines maps, planning tools, checklists, collaboration, AI assistance, and historical insight into one cohesive experience.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Planning should be enjoyable.
- Collaboration should be effortless.
- Secrecy should be preserved.
- History should inform, not constrain.
- The Hare remains in complete control.

---

# Workspace Overview

The Hare Workspace includes:

- Interactive map
- Digital Chalk palette
- Trail timeline
- Beer Check planner
- Waypoint manager
- Safety planner
- Equipment checklist
- Collaboration panel
- AI Assistant
- Run preview
- Trail release controls

---

# FR-HARE-001 — Workspace Dashboard

The dashboard shall display:

- Current Run Session
- Assigned Hares
- Planning progress
- Countdown to run day
- Weather forecast
- Outstanding tasks
- Recent activity

---

# FR-HARE-002 — Collaborative Editing

Multiple Hares may work simultaneously.

The platform shall:

- Display active collaborators.
- Prevent conflicting edits.
- Synchronize changes in real time.
- Record planning history.

---

# FR-HARE-003 — Planning Notes

Hares may create private planning notes.

Notes may include:

- Trail ideas
- Logistics
- Supplies
- Parking reminders
- Landowner permissions
- Contingency plans

Notes remain private to authorized planners.

---

# FR-HARE-004 — Equipment Checklist

The workspace shall provide configurable checklists.

Example items:

- Chalk
- Flour
- Whistle
- Beer
- Water
- First Aid Kit
- Ice
- Cups
- Headlamps
- GPS Device
- Phone Charger

Templates may be reused across runs.

---

# FR-HARE-005 — AI Planning Assistant

Where enabled, AI may assist by:

- Estimating trail duration.
- Calculating elevation gain.
- Identifying steep sections.
- Suggesting Beer Check spacing.
- Highlighting potential hazards.
- Detecting overlaps with recent trails.

AI recommendations are advisory only.

---

# FR-HARE-006 — Trail Preview

Hares may preview the participant experience before publication.

Preview modes include:

- Planner View
- Participant View
- Living Trail View
- Historic Trail View

---

# FR-HARE-007 — Historical Reference

The workspace may display previous trails from the same area.

Hares may compare:

- Distance
- Terrain
- Elevation
- Beer Check locations
- Difficulty
- Themes

Historical data is intended to inspire, not encourage duplication.

---

# FR-HARE-008 — Trail Secrecy Controls

Hares control:

- Release timing
- Visibility rules
- Progressive reveal
- Officer access
- Emergency override

Default behavior keeps trails hidden until release.

---

# FR-HARE-009 — Risk Assessment

The workspace shall support recording:

- Known hazards
- Escape routes
- Emergency contacts
- Medical facilities
- Mobile coverage
- Water availability

Risk assessments may be reviewed by officers before publication.

---

# FR-HARE-010 — Publish Trail

Publishing validates the trail and prepares it for release.

Validation checks include:

- Missing finish
- Unconnected segments
- Duplicate Beer Checks
- Incomplete metadata

Warnings are presented before publication.

---

# Business Principles

- The Hare owns the trail.
- AI supports, never replaces, creativity.
- Collaboration should reduce effort.
- Historical knowledge should improve future planning.
- Secrecy remains essential until release.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Hares can plan collaboratively.
- AI assistance is available.
- Equipment and safety planning are integrated.
- Trail preview works.
- Secrecy controls are enforced.
