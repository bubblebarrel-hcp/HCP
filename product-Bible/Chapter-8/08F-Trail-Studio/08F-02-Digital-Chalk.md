# Purpose

This document defines the Digital Chalk system used within Trail Studio.

Digital Chalk is HCP's visual language for trail design and navigation. Rather than replacing traditional Hash markings, it translates them into a digital experience that remains familiar to Hashers across the world.

The objective is to preserve the traditions of trail laying while enhancing planning, navigation, historical preservation, and accessibility.

---

# Philosophy

Traditional chalk marks are the language of the trail.

Digital Chalk is that same language expressed through modern technology.

The platform shall never force GPS-style navigation where symbolic trail interpretation better reflects the culture of the Hash.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Respect traditional trail markings.
- Support regional kennel variations.
- Allow customization.
- Preserve mystery.
- Prioritize readability.
- Support offline use.

# Digital Chalk Library

Trail Studio shall provide a configurable library of Digital Chalk symbols.

Symbols are grouped into categories.

---

# Category — Direction

Examples include:

- ON ON
- Left Turn
- Right Turn
- Straight Ahead
- Sharp Left
- Sharp Right
- U-Turn

Each symbol may include:

- Icon
- Meaning
- Localized description
- Optional animation
- Custom kennel variant

---

# Category — Checks

Supported symbols include:

- Check
- False Trail
- True Trail
- Three-Way Check
- Circle Check
- Back Check

Kennels may extend the library with their own symbols.

---

# Category — Stops

Supported stop types include:

- Beer Check
- Water Stop
- Food Stop
- Regroup
- Rest Area

Stops may contain:

- GPS coordinates
- Photos
- Videos
- Notes
- Opening conditions
- Visibility rules

---

# Category — Hazards

Examples include:

- River Crossing
- Busy Road
- Steep Hill
- Mud
- Slippery Surface
- Wildlife
- Construction

Hazards may be:

- Public
- Hare Only
- Officer Only

---

# Category — Finish

Supported finish types include:

- Finish
- Circle Location
- Shuttle Pickup
- Return Route

---

# FR-CHALK-001 — Place Digital Chalk

## Priority

Critical

Authorized Hares shall be able to place Digital Chalk symbols directly onto the map.

Symbols snap to the trail unless free placement is selected.

---

# FR-CHALK-002 — Edit Chalk Marks

Hares may:

- Move
- Rotate
- Duplicate
- Delete
- Group
- Annotate

All edits are versioned.

---

# FR-CHALK-003 — Progressive Reveal

Each Digital Chalk mark may define a visibility strategy.

Supported strategies include:

- Immediately visible
- At scheduled time
- When participant reaches previous marker
- Geofence activation
- Manual reveal

This allows the trail to unfold naturally.

---

# FR-CHALK-004 — Symbol Metadata

Each symbol may contain:

- Title
- Description
- Timestamp
- Creator
- Associated waypoint
- Photos
- Audio
- Notes

Metadata becomes part of the Run Capsule.

---

# FR-CHALK-005 — Custom Kennel Symbols

Kennels may define their own Digital Chalk symbols.

Custom symbols may include:

- Icon
- Name
- Meaning
- Color
- Usage guidance

These remain private to the kennel unless shared publicly.

---

# FR-CHALK-006 — Symbol Templates

Trail Studio shall support reusable symbol templates.

Example templates:

- Standard Beer Check
- Scenic Viewpoint
- Safety Warning
- Typical Regroup

Templates accelerate trail planning.

---

# FR-CHALK-007 — Symbol Validation

The system shall detect common planning issues such as:

- Isolated symbols
- Missing continuation
- Overlapping symbols
- Duplicate finish markers
- Conflicting directions

Validation warnings assist Hares but do not automatically block publication.

---

# FR-CHALK-008 — Offline Rendering

Digital Chalk symbols shall be available without network connectivity.

Participants who download the trail beforehand should continue to see symbols even if mobile coverage is lost.

---

# FR-CHALK-009 — Historical Preservation

Every Digital Chalk symbol shall become part of the Historic Trail.

Future viewers may explore the original trail exactly as it was laid.

---

# FR-CHALK-010 — Accessibility

Digital Chalk shall support:

- High-contrast mode
- Scalable icons
- Screen reader descriptions
- Color-independent recognition

Accessibility enhancements shall preserve the traditional meaning of the symbols.

---

# Business Principles

- Chalk remains the heart of trail communication.
- Digital representation should reinforce—not replace—traditional knowledge.
- Every symbol contributes to the story of the run.
- Regional diversity should be celebrated.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- A standardized Digital Chalk library exists.
- Kennels can customize symbols.
- Progressive reveal is supported.
- Offline rendering works.
- Historical preservation is guaranteed.
