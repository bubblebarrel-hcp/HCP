# Purpose

This document defines how the Community Graph preserves long-term community history while providing ethical analytics and AI-assisted insights.

The objective is to help the Hash community understand its evolution, celebrate its contributors, and preserve relationships across generations.

---

# Vision

Every Hasher leaves a mark.

Every friendship strengthens the community.

Every generation should be able to understand the one that came before it.

The Community Graph is the living historical network of the Hash.

---

# Philosophy

History is built through people.

Communities become stronger when they remember their contributors.

Analytics should illuminate patterns, not judge individuals.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Preserve relationships.
- Celebrate contribution.
- Respect privacy.
- Explain every AI insight.
- Favor stewardship over competition.

---

# Analytics Categories

The Community Graph supports:

- Community Growth
- Mentorship
- Collaboration
- Leadership
- Visitor Exchange
- Heritage
- Legacy
- Preservation

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-031 — Community Growth

Visualize:

- Membership growth
- New member retention
- Mentorship participation
- Officer succession
- Visitor exchanges
- Cross-kennel collaboration

Analytics are available at:

- Global
- Country
- Kennel
- Event

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-032 — Mentorship Network

Display anonymized insights such as:

- Number of active mentors
- Average mentorship duration
- Mentor-to-member ratios
- Mentorship completion trends

Individual mentorship details remain subject to privacy controls.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-033 — Leadership History

Maintain a permanent record of leadership across kennels.

Examples:

- Grand Masters
- Religious Advisers
- Hash Cash
- Trail Masters
- On Sec

Each role includes verified service periods and links to related events.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-034 — Collaboration Network

Visualize long-term collaboration patterns, including:

- Co-Hares
- Co-Scribes
- Event organizers
- Heritage projects
- Charity initiatives

Users can explore how collaborations evolved over time.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-035 — Legacy Profiles

Members may designate trusted contacts to manage their profile if they become permanently inactive or pass away.

Legacy Profiles preserve:

- Trail history
- Run Capsules
- Stories
- Contributions
- Officer service
- Mentorship
- Community tributes

Legacy status is governed by documented policies.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-036 — Community Tributes

Kennels may publish tributes recognizing significant contributors.

Tributes may include:

- Written reflections
- Photos
- Audio recordings
- Video messages
- Historical achievements

Tributes become part of the permanent historical archive.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-037 — AI Heritage Assistant

AI may assist with:

- Summarizing decades of contributions
- Identifying preservation gaps
- Connecting related historical records
- Suggesting oral history interviews

Every AI-generated summary references supporting evidence.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-038 — Historical Network Explorer

Users may explore the Community Graph across time.

Examples:

- Officer succession by decade
- Growth of a kennel
- Expansion into new regions
- Mentorship generations
- Long-standing collaborations

The explorer integrates with the Global Hash Atlas.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-039 — Preservation Health

Each kennel receives a Preservation Health summary based on:

- Historical completeness
- Media preservation
- Run Capsule coverage
- Leadership history
- Oral history collection
- Community participation

This summary is intended to encourage preservation efforts rather than comparison.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-040 — Community Knowledge Base

The Community Graph supports a curated knowledge base of:

- Historic members
- Founding stories
- Traditions
- Landmark events
- Community terminology
- Heritage projects

Content is linked to primary historical sources whenever possible.

---

# Business Principles

Communities thrive when they remember.

Recognition should preserve history.

Analytics should guide stewardship.

Legacy belongs to the entire community.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Community analytics are documented.
- Legacy Profiles are supported.
- Historical exploration is defined.
- AI heritage tools are transparent.
- Preservation metrics are established.
