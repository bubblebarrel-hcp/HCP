# Purpose

This document defines how the Hash Community Platform recognizes meaningful contributions to the community.

Recognition exists to celebrate participation, stewardship, mentorship, and service—not popularity.

The platform intentionally avoids follower counts, engagement rankings, and influencer mechanics.

---

# Vision

Every Hasher contributes differently.

Some lay unforgettable trails.

Some preserve history.

Some mentor newcomers.

Some organize events.

Some travel the world connecting communities.

The platform should celebrate all of these equally.

---

# Philosophy

Recognition should encourage contribution.

Contribution strengthens the community.

Recognition should never become competition.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Contribution over popularity.
- Stewardship over status.
- Diversity of contribution.
- Transparency in recognition.
- Community-first design.

---

# Recognition Categories

HCP recognizes contributions across multiple dimensions:

- Trail Building
- Storytelling
- Mentorship
- Hospitality
- Leadership
- Exploration
- Preservation
- Photography
- Event Organization
- Community Service

No single category is considered more valuable than another.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-011 — Contribution Profile

Each member profile includes a contribution summary.

Examples:

- Trails Laid
- Trail Reports Written
- Run Capsules Contributed
- Photos Shared
- Visitors Hosted
- Mentorships
- Officer Terms
- Community Projects
- Events Organized

These metrics are descriptive, not competitive.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-012 — Community Roles

Members may earn long-term recognition roles such as:

- 🐇 Trail Builder
- ✍️ Storyteller
- 📸 Chronicler
- 🤝 Mentor
- 🌍 Explorer
- 🍺 Hospitality Ambassador
- 🧭 Organizer
- ❤️ Community Builder
- 🏛 Heritage Keeper

Roles reflect sustained contribution rather than isolated achievements.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-013 — Recognition Timeline

Members may browse a timeline of meaningful milestones.

Examples:

- First Trail Laid
- 100th Run
- First Interhash
- First Mentorship
- 10 Years of Service
- 500th Trail Report
- Heritage Contribution

Every milestone links to supporting historical records.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-014 — Heritage Recognition

Exceptional long-term contributions may be acknowledged through Heritage Recognition.

Examples include:

- Founding members.
- Long-serving officers.
- Historic Trail Blazers.
- Community Historians.
- Distinguished Mentors.

Recognition is granted through transparent governance and is intended to preserve history rather than create exclusivity.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-015 — Community Appreciation

Members may express appreciation for specific contributions.

Examples:

- "Thanks for laying today's trail."
- "Great Trail Report."
- "Wonderful hospitality."

Appreciation is attached to the contribution itself rather than increasing a user's popularity score.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-016 — Contribution Collections

Members automatically build curated collections such as:

- My Trails
- My Stories
- My Photos
- My Events
- My Mentorships
- My Hosted Visitors
- My Heritage Contributions

Collections become part of the member's long-term legacy.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-017 — Officer Service History

The platform maintains a verified history of official service.

Examples:

- Grand Master
- Religious Adviser
- Hash Cash
- On Sec
- Joint Master
- Trail Master

Each role includes:

- Kennel
- Start date
- End date
- Related events

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-018 — Community Contributions Dashboard

Users may privately review summaries of their contributions over time.

Examples:

- Years active
- Communities served
- Visitors welcomed
- Events supported
- Historical content preserved

This dashboard is reflective, not comparative.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-019 — AI Legacy Summary

With user approval, AI may generate a narrative summary of a member's contributions.

Example:

> "Over 22 years, Sarah helped shape three kennels, mentored 27 new Hashers, laid 94 trails, documented hundreds of stories, and welcomed visitors from five continents."

Every statement links back to verifiable records.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-020 — Recognition Governance

Recognition programs are governed by published criteria.

The platform records:

- Recognition type
- Date awarded
- Awarding authority
- Supporting evidence

Recognition may not be granted solely by automated systems.

---

# Business Principles

Celebrate service.

Encourage stewardship.

Honor history.

Recognize people for what they give back.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Recognition categories are defined.
- Contribution profiles exist.
- Heritage recognition is documented.
- AI summaries are transparent.
- Governance ensures fairness.
