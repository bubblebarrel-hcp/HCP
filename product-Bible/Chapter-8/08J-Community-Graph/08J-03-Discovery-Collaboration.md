# Purpose

This document defines how the Community Graph enables meaningful discovery, collaboration, and introductions across the global Hash community.

Recommendations are based on shared experiences, participation, and verified community relationships—not engagement metrics or popularity.

---

# Vision

The Community Graph should help Hashers discover people they are likely to enjoy meeting, working with, or learning from.

Every recommendation should strengthen the community.

---

# Philosophy

Communities grow through introductions.

Technology should facilitate authentic encounters rather than replace them.

Discovery should always be explainable.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Explain every recommendation.
- Prioritize real-world context.
- Never recommend based on popularity alone.
- Respect privacy and consent.
- Encourage cross-kennel friendships.

---

# Discovery Categories

The platform supports:

- People Discovery
- Mentor Discovery
- Visitor Discovery
- Collaboration Discovery
- Event Discovery
- Twin Kennel Discovery
- Community Projects
- Reunion Discovery

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-021 — People You May Know

Recommendations may be based on:

- Shared Run Sessions
- Mutual collaborators
- Shared kennels
- Similar travel history
- Officer service
- Community projects
- Shared mentors

Every recommendation includes an explanation.

Example:

> "You both attended Interhash 2032 and have hashed together in Cape Town and Nairobi."

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-022 — Mentor Discovery

New Hashers may browse mentors by:

- Kennel
- Experience
- Languages spoken
- Trail expertise
- Leadership roles
- Availability

Mentorship participation is entirely optional.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-023 — Collaboration Opportunities

The platform may suggest collaborators for:

- Trail laying
- Event organization
- Charity initiatives
- Heritage preservation
- Photography
- Trail Reports
- Oral history interviews

Suggestions are based on complementary skills and previous collaboration.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-024 — Visitor Connections

Traveling Hashers may discover:

- Local hosts
- Visitor-friendly kennels
- Nearby public runs
- Language-compatible members
- Twin kennel contacts

All introductions require user consent.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-025 — Reunion Discovery

The platform highlights opportunities to reconnect.

Examples:

- "You last hashed with Alex eight years ago."
- "Five members from your first Hash are attending this anniversary run."
- "Your former RA is visiting your city next month."

These prompts celebrate community continuity.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-026 — Community Projects

Members may discover and join projects including:

- Archive digitization
- Anniversary planning
- Charity events
- Trail restoration
- Oral history collections
- Historical research

Projects become collaborative spaces linked to the Community Graph.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-027 — Twin Kennel Discovery

AI may identify kennels with strong historical or cultural similarities based on:

- Shared visitor history
- Similar traditions
- Comparable trail styles
- Collaborative events

Suggested partnerships require approval by both kennels.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-028 — Community Intelligence

AI may identify opportunities such as:

- Under-documented history
- Emerging visitor patterns
- Potential mentorship gaps
- Preservation priorities

Insights are advisory and always explainable.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-029 — Explainable Recommendations

Every recommendation includes:

- Supporting evidence
- Data sources
- User-controlled preferences
- Ability to dismiss or hide future suggestions

Recommendations remain transparent.

---

# FR-COMMUNITY-030 — Consent & Privacy

Users control whether they may be:

- Suggested to others
- Listed as mentors
- Contacted by visitors
- Included in collaboration recommendations

Consent may be updated at any time.

---

# Business Principles

Discovery should create friendships.

Collaboration should preserve culture.

Recommendations should always respect autonomy.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Discovery features are defined.
- Collaboration workflows exist.
- AI recommendations are explainable.
- Consent controls are comprehensive.
- Community projects integrate with the graph.
