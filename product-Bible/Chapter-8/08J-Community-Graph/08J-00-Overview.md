# Purpose

The Community Graph models the relationships that naturally emerge through participation in the Hash.

Unlike traditional social networks, relationships are earned through shared experiences rather than created through follows or friend requests.

The Community Graph becomes the social memory of HCP.

---

# Vision

The Hash is built on people.

The Community Graph should help preserve those human connections across decades, continents and generations.

Instead of asking:

"Who follows whom?"

It asks:

"Who has shared adventures together?"

---

# Philosophy

Shared experiences create stronger communities than digital interactions.

Relationships should emerge from participation.

Technology should reflect real-world friendships rather than replace them.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Community before popularity.
- Experiences before engagement.
- Privacy before visibility.
- Authenticity before algorithms.
- Connections through participation.

---

# What is the Community Graph?

The Community Graph is a network of people, kennels, events and shared experiences.

Relationships are formed by real-world participation, such as:

- Running together
- Laying trails together
- Serving as officers
- Visiting kennels
- Attending Interhash
- Writing Trail Reports
- Contributing media
- Organizing events

The graph continuously evolves as members participate in the community.

---

# Core Nodes

The graph contains interconnected nodes representing:

- Hashers
- Kennels
- Run Sessions
- Run Capsules
- Trails
- Events
- Countries
- Cities
- Passport Stamps
- Story Cards

---

# Relationship Types

Examples include:

- Hashed With
- Hare For
- Scribed
- Hosted
- Visited
- Mentored
- Served Together
- Organized
- Traveled Together
- Contributed To
- Attended

Every relationship has supporting evidence.

---

# Why It Exists

The Community Graph powers:

- Friend discovery
- Historical research
- Visitor introductions
- Travel recommendations
- Shared memories
- AI-assisted storytelling
- Cultural preservation

---

# Example Journey

A Hasher opens their profile.

Instead of seeing:

Followers: 328

They see:

- Hashed with 1,847 people
- Visited 39 kennels
- Shared 212 Run Capsules
- Mentored 8 new Hashers
- Laid 24 trails
- Served as RA for 5 years

The profile tells a life story rather than a popularity story.

---

# Success Criteria

The Community Graph succeeds when it reflects the real relationships created through Hashing.

It should strengthen the community without encouraging vanity metrics.

---

# Business Principles

People create the Hash.

The Graph preserves those connections.

Relationships matter more than numbers.
