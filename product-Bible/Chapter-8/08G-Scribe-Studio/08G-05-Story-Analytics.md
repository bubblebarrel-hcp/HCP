# Purpose

This document defines how HCP transforms Trail Reports and Story Graphs into a living knowledge base that preserves the collective memory of the global Hash community.

The goal is not to measure writing performance.

The goal is to preserve knowledge, improve discoverability, and strengthen historical continuity.

---

# Philosophy

Every Trail Report is more than a story.

It is a piece of community knowledge.

When connected with thousands of other reports, it forms an evolving picture of Hash traditions across years, kennels, countries, and generations.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Preserve meaning over metrics.
- Celebrate diversity of storytelling.
- Respect privacy and local customs.
- Make history discoverable.
- AI explains; humans interpret.

---

# Core Concepts

## Story Knowledge Graph

Every published report contributes structured relationships between:

- Runs
- Kennels
- Hares
- Scribes
- Participants
- Visitors
- Beer Checks
- Songs
- Awards
- Trails
- Locations
- Events

Together these form the Story Knowledge Graph.

---

## Story Intelligence

AI may analyze historical reports to identify recurring themes, traditions, anniversaries, and connections.

Insights are descriptive rather than evaluative.

---

## Cultural Memory

Cultural Memory represents the accumulated traditions and stories preserved by the community.

It becomes richer with every published report.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-001 — Build Story Knowledge Graph

## Priority

High

The platform shall continuously build a structured Story Knowledge Graph from published reports and associated Story Cards.

Relationships include:

- Person ↔ Run
- Hare ↔ Trail
- Kennel ↔ Event
- Beer Check ↔ Location
- Song ↔ Circle
- Visitor ↔ Kennel

The graph supports search, analytics, and historical exploration.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-002 — Historical Discovery

Users may discover reports by:

- Theme
- Anniversary
- Country
- Kennel
- Hare
- Scribe
- Visitor
- Landmark
- Song
- Tradition
- Run Number

Discovery respects privacy settings.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-003 — AI Historical Insights

Where enabled, AI may highlight observations such as:

- Frequently visited locations.
- Long-running traditions.
- Rare event types.
- Historic milestones.
- Memorable anniversaries.
- Seasonal patterns.

Insights are presented as observations rather than conclusions.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-004 — Tradition Explorer

The platform may identify recurring traditions within a kennel.

Examples include:

- Annual Red Dress Runs.
- Charity events.
- Special songs.
- Costume themes.
- Memorial Runs.
- Holiday traditions.

These traditions become part of the kennel's historical identity.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-005 — Story Connections

Published reports may display related stories.

Connections may be based on:

- Shared participants.
- Similar routes.
- Anniversary dates.
- Matching themes.
- Historic locations.
- Visitor exchanges.

Readers can navigate seamlessly between related reports.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-006 — Scribe Insights

Each Scribe may view private editorial insights including:

- Reports published.
- Average drafting time.
- Media utilization.
- Revision history.
- Preferred writing structure.

These insights are intended for self-reflection and are not publicly ranked.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-007 — Community Story Map

The platform may visualize stories on an interactive world map.

Users can explore reports geographically while respecting visibility rules.

This capability integrates with the Global Hash Atlas.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-008 — Knowledge Preservation

Structured relationships remain preserved even if presentation formats evolve.

Future interfaces should continue to benefit from decades of accumulated knowledge.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-009 — AI Research Assistant

Authorized users may ask questions such as:

- "Show all Red Dress Runs in Africa."
- "Which trails ended at this landmark?"
- "Find reports mentioning the founding Hare."
- "When did this kennel first host visitors from Japan?"

Responses include links back to the original reports.

---

# FR-KNOWLEDGE-010 — Long-Term Stewardship

The Story Knowledge Graph shall be designed for decades of growth.

New data types may be added without breaking existing historical relationships.

---

# Business Principles

- Knowledge grows through participation.
- Every report enriches the archive.
- Analytics should reveal stories, not create competition.
- Cultural preservation is a long-term commitment.
- The community owns its shared history.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- The Story Knowledge Graph exists.
- Historical discovery is supported.
- AI insights remain optional.
- Story relationships are preserved.
- Global historical exploration is possible.
