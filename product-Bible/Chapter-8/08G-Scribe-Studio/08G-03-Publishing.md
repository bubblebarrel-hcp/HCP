# Purpose

This document defines how Trail Reports are reviewed, published, preserved, revised, and integrated into the long-term historical archive of the Hash Community Platform.

Publication is the final editorial step performed by the Hash Scribe.

Once published, the report becomes part of the permanent memory of the Run Session.

---

# Philosophy

Publishing is not simply pressing a button.

It is the official act of recording history.

A published Trail Report represents the collective memory of the Run and becomes a cultural artifact belonging to the community.

---

---

# FR-PUBLISH-001 — Publish Official Report

## Priority

Critical

Only an authorized Hash Scribe or designated officer may publish the official Trail Report.

Publishing shall:

- Assign a permanent publication timestamp.
- Generate an immutable report version.
- Link the report to the associated Run Capsule.
- Trigger configured notifications.
- Update eligible Hash Passport achievements.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-002 — Report Identity

Every published Trail Report shall receive:

- Unique identifier
- Associated Run Session
- Kennel
- Publication date
- Scribe attribution
- Revision number

These identifiers remain permanent.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-003 — Historical Versioning

Published reports shall never be silently overwritten.

Corrections shall create:

- Revision number
- Editor
- Timestamp
- Reason for revision

Earlier published versions remain accessible to authorized users.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-004 — Multiple Reading Modes

Readers may view reports in different formats:

- Classic article
- Timeline
- Story Cards
- Interactive map
- Media gallery
- Hash Time Machine replay

All modes present the same historical record.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-005 — Interactive References

Published reports may contain references to:

- Beer Checks
- Waypoints
- Photos
- Videos
- Circle events
- Awards
- Songs
- Passport milestones

Selecting a reference opens the related historical context.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-006 — Export

Authorized users may export Trail Reports as:

- PDF
- Markdown
- HTML
- Plain Text

Future formats may include EPUB and print-ready book layouts.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-007 — Translation

Where enabled, reports may be translated into supported languages.

The original report remains the authoritative version.

Translations are clearly identified.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-008 — Citation

Each published report shall include a permanent citation format.

Example:

Owerri Hash House Harriers.
Run #412.
Trail Report.
Published 12 July 2026.
Hash Community Platform.

This supports long-term referencing and historical research.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-009 — Search Indexing

Published reports become searchable using:

- Run Number
- Kennel
- Hare
- Scribe
- Date
- Theme
- Country
- Keywords
- Tags

Search respects privacy settings.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-010 — Preservation

The platform shall preserve:

- Original formatting
- Embedded media references
- Interactive links
- Timeline relationships
- Story Graph
- Story Cards

Reports remain usable as technology evolves.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-011 — Community Engagement

Readers may:

- Like reports
- Bookmark reports
- Leave comments (if enabled)
- Share reports
- Recommend reports to other Hashers

Engagement never alters the published report itself.

---

# FR-PUBLISH-012 — Legacy Collections

Kennels may curate collections such as:

- Best Trail Reports
- Anniversary Reports
- Charity Runs
- Red Dress Runs
- Interhash Reports
- Memorial Runs

Collections become part of the kennel archive.

---

# Business Principles

- Publication creates history.
- History should never be silently rewritten.
- Reports belong to the community that created them.
- Every report deserves long-term preservation.
- Future generations should experience reports as intended.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Official publication is supported.
- Version history is immutable.
- Reports are searchable.
- Multiple reading modes exist.
- Long-term preservation is guaranteed.
