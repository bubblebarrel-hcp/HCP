# Purpose

This document defines the Story Editor, the primary workspace where Hash Scribes transform collected events into the official Trail Report.

The Story Editor combines structured event data, rich media, interactive maps, AI-assisted writing, and traditional editing tools into a single storytelling experience.

---

# Vision

The Story Editor should feel less like a word processor and more like a documentary editing suite.

Everything the Scribe needs should already be available:

- Story Timeline
- Photos
- Videos
- Map
- Circle events
- Beer Checks
- Participant comments
- Historical references
- AI suggestions

The Scribe simply shapes the story.

---

# Philosophy

The editor exists to reduce administrative effort while protecting the Scribe's unique writing style.

AI writes drafts.

The Scribe writes history.

---

# Workspace Layout

The default editor includes:

- Story Timeline
- Rich Text Editor
- Media Browser
- Interactive Map
- Story Graph
- AI Assistant
- Version History
- Comments & Review
- Preview Panel
- Publishing Controls

Each panel may be resized, hidden, or rearranged.

---

# FR-EDITOR-001 — Rich Story Editor

## Priority

Critical

The editor shall support:

- Headings
- Paragraphs
- Quotes
- Lists
- Tables
- Hyperlinks
- Embedded media
- Captions
- Callout boxes
- Horizontal dividers

Formatting should remain clean and accessible across web and mobile.

---

# FR-EDITOR-002 — Drag-and-Drop Story Building

The Scribe may drag items from the Story Timeline directly into the report.

Supported assets include:

- Photos
- Videos
- Beer Checks
- Waypoints
- Comments
- Awards
- Circle moments
- Songs

The editor automatically generates contextual placeholders that the Scribe can refine.

---

# FR-EDITOR-003 — Interactive Media Embeds

Media embedded in the report remains interactive.

Readers may:

- Expand photos
- Play videos
- View image galleries
- Open map locations
- Explore related comments

Media retains its original metadata.

---

# FR-EDITOR-004 — Live Map References

The editor may embed interactive maps showing:

- Trail geometry
- Beer Checks
- Scenic locations
- Hazards
- Circle venue

Maps remain synchronized with the associated Run Capsule.

---

# FR-EDITOR-005 — AI Writing Assistant

The AI Assistant may:

- Generate opening paragraphs.
- Summarize sections.
- Suggest transitions.
- Improve grammar.
- Detect duplicated content.
- Highlight missing events.
- Suggest captions.
- Generate concise or expanded versions.

All AI-generated text is clearly identified until accepted by the Scribe.

---

# FR-EDITOR-006 — Writing Style Profiles

Each Scribe may define a preferred writing style.

Examples include:

- Humorous
- Traditional
- Narrative
- Formal
- Conversational
- Minimalist

AI adapts suggestions to the selected style without attempting to imitate another individual's writing.

---

# FR-EDITOR-007 — Historical References

The editor may surface related historical material such as:

- Previous reports
- Similar themes
- Anniversary runs
- Past Beer Checks
- Historic photos

References inspire the Scribe but are never inserted automatically.

---

# FR-EDITOR-008 — Auto Save & Versioning

The editor shall:

- Auto-save continuously.
- Maintain revision history.
- Support named versions.
- Allow rollback to previous drafts.

No work should be lost due to connectivity issues.

---

# FR-EDITOR-009 — Review Mode

Reviewers may:

- Comment on passages.
- Suggest wording.
- Flag factual issues.
- Approve sections.

Suggestions remain separate until accepted by the Scribe.

---

# FR-EDITOR-010 — Publish Report

Publishing performs the following actions:

- Locks the official report.
- Generates a publish timestamp.
- Links the report to the Run Capsule.
- Updates Hash Passport milestones where applicable.
- Makes the report available according to privacy settings.

Subsequent corrections require a documented revision.

---

# Business Principles

- The Scribe remains the author.
- AI accelerates writing without replacing creativity.
- Rich media should enhance, not distract from, the narrative.
- Every report should remain readable decades into the future.
- Editorial control always belongs to the Scribe.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- A full-featured Story Editor exists.
- Media integrates seamlessly.
- AI suggestions remain optional.
- Version history is preserved.
- Reports can be reviewed and published.
