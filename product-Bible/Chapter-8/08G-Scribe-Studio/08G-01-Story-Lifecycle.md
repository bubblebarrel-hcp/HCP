# Purpose

This document defines how HCP collects story assets throughout a Run Session and transforms them into the official Trail Report.

Storytelling in HCP is continuous. The platform begins gathering context before the run starts and continues until the final report is published and archived.

---

# Philosophy

Stories are not written after the run.

They are collected throughout the entire lifecycle of the event.

Every photo, comment, Beer Check, visitor, song, and memorable moment contributes to the final narrative.

---

# Story Assets

The platform continuously collects structured story assets, including:

- Trail events
- GPS timeline
- Beer Checks
- Waypoints
- Participant comments
- Media uploads
- Officer announcements
- Circle activities
- Awards
- Visitor recognitions
- Down-Down records
- Weather conditions
- Attendance statistics

Each asset includes metadata such as timestamp, location, contributor, and source.

---

# FR-STORY-001 — Automatic Story Collection

## Priority

Critical

The platform shall automatically collect eligible events during the Run Session.

Story collection shall require no manual intervention.

Collected events become part of the Story Graph.

---

# FR-STORY-002 — Manual Story Entries

The Scribe may manually create story entries.

Examples include:

- Funny incidents
- Memorable quotes
- Unexpected events
- Local traditions
- Behind-the-scenes moments

Manual entries remain editable until publication.

---

# FR-STORY-003 — Story Timeline

The Story Timeline displays all collected events in chronological order.

The Scribe may:

- Reorder events
- Merge related events
- Hide irrelevant items
- Pin important moments
- Add narrative notes

The original event history remains unchanged.

---

# FR-STORY-004 — Event Categorization

Story entries may be categorized as:

- Trail
- Beer Check
- Circle
- Visitor
- Award
- Song
- Incident
- Humor
- Safety
- Historical
- General

Categories improve organization and searchability.

---

# FR-STORY-005 — Narrative Sections

The report editor shall support predefined sections such as:

- Opening
- Trail Overview
- Memorable Moments
- Beer Checks
- Circle Highlights
- Awards & Recognitions
- Closing Remarks

Kennels may customize or extend these sections.

---

# FR-STORY-006 — Draft Management

Multiple drafts may exist before publication.

Each draft includes:

- Author
- Timestamp
- Revision notes
- Version history

Previous drafts remain recoverable.

---

# FR-STORY-007 — AI Draft Assistance

Where enabled, AI may generate an initial narrative draft using:

- Story Timeline
- Media metadata
- GPS events
- Participant comments
- Officer notes

The draft is clearly marked as AI-assisted.

Publication always requires Scribe approval.

---

# FR-STORY-008 — Supporting Evidence

Every paragraph in the draft may link back to supporting assets.

Examples include:

- Photo galleries
- Videos
- Map locations
- Beer Check records
- Circle announcements

Readers may explore the evidence behind the story.

---

# FR-STORY-009 — Collaborative Review

If enabled by the kennel, designated reviewers may:

- Suggest edits
- Leave comments
- Approve sections

Only the Hash Scribe (or an authorized delegate) may publish the official report.

---

# FR-STORY-010 — Publish Narrative

Publishing:

- Locks the official report.
- Creates the historical version.
- Links the report to the Run Capsule.
- Triggers Hash Passport updates where applicable.
- Makes the report available according to privacy settings.

---

# Business Principles

- Story collection is continuous.
- The Scribe remains the author.
- AI organizes; humans interpret.
- Every report strengthens the historical archive.
- Authenticity is more valuable than automation.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Story assets are collected automatically.
- Narrative drafts are supported.
- AI assistance is optional.
- Revision history is preserved.
- Published reports become permanent historical records.
