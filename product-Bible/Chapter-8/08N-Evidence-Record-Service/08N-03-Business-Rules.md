# Purpose

This document establishes the governance model, business rules, audit requirements, preservation principles, and long-term stewardship expectations for the Evidence Record Service.

The Evidence Record Service underpins trust across every domain of HCP. Its governance therefore requires stronger guarantees than ordinary application data.

---

# Vision

Evidence should outlive software.

Trust should outlive people.

History should outlive generations.

---

# Philosophy

Evidence is a community asset.

No single feature owns it.

Every platform service contributes to it.

Every future generation benefits from it.

# Guiding Principles

- Evidence is immutable.
- Provenance is permanent.
- Verification is transparent.
- Preservation is intentional.
- Privacy is respected.
- AI is explainable.

# Governance Responsibilities

The Evidence Record Service supports:

- Identity
- Passport
- Events
- Governance
- Community Graph
- Trail Studio
- Run Capsules
- Awards
- AI Platform
- Audit Services
- Historical Archives

No individual feature may bypass Evidence governance policies.

---

# FR-EV-021 — Evidence Governance

Platform governance defines:

- Evidence standards
- Verification policies
- Trust models
- Preservation policies
- Retention schedules
- Access controls
- Compliance requirements

Governance changes are version-controlled and auditable.

---

# FR-EV-022 — Immutable Audit

Every action affecting an Evidence Record is recorded, including:

- Creation
- Verification
- Dispute
- Supersession
- Access to sensitive evidence
- Policy changes

Audit logs are append-only and tamper-evident.

---

# FR-EV-023 — Preservation Strategy

Evidence designated as historical or institutional shall support:

- Redundant storage
- Geographic replication
- Integrity verification
- Periodic restoration testing
- Format migration
- Metadata preservation

The goal is preservation across decades, not application versions.

---

# FR-EV-024 — Legal & Privacy Compliance

Evidence handling shall comply with applicable laws and organizational obligations, including:

- Privacy legislation
- Data retention requirements
- Right to access
- Right to correction
- Lawful deletion where required
- Archival exemptions where legally permitted

Compliance actions are themselves auditable.

---

# FR-EV-025 — Cross-Domain Integrity

Every domain referencing Evidence Records shall:

- Store references rather than duplicate evidence.
- Respect access policies.
- Preserve provenance.
- Surface verification status.
- Support explainability.

This ensures consistency across the platform.

---

# FR-EV-026 — Historical Preservation

Evidence identified as heritage material may receive enhanced protection.

Examples include:

- Founding documents
- Historic trail maps
- Legacy photographs
- Oral histories
- Constitutional milestones
- Landmark Run Capsules
- Anniversary publications

These records may be curated as part of the HCP Heritage Archive.

---

# FR-EV-027 — Explainability Obligations

Platform services using AI or automated decision-making shall expose supporting evidence where practical.

If supporting evidence is unavailable, the system must clearly communicate this to the user.

---

# FR-EV-028 — Evidence Stewardship

Evidence stewardship responsibilities include:

- Quality assurance
- Metadata consistency
- Periodic review
- Preservation planning
- Historical curation
- Community collaboration

Stewardship is a shared responsibility between platform administrators and participating organizations.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-EV-001

Evidence Records are immutable once created.

Subsequent changes create new states or linked records rather than overwriting history.

---

## BR-EV-002

Evidence ownership remains with the originating contributor or organization, subject to applicable platform terms and legal requirements.

---

## BR-EV-003

Evidence references shall remain stable throughout the lifetime of the platform.

---

## BR-EV-004

Every verified claim should reference supporting Evidence Records wherever feasible.

---

## BR-EV-005

AI-generated statements referencing platform data should provide supporting citations or explicitly acknowledge uncertainty.

---

## BR-EV-006

Historical preservation takes precedence over convenience where institutional memory would otherwise be lost.

---

## BR-EV-007

Sensitive evidence shall only be accessible to authorized identities within approved contexts.

---

## BR-EV-008

Evidence governance policies are version-controlled and permanently archived.
