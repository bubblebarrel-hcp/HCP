# Purpose

The Evidence Record Service is the canonical source of supporting evidence throughout the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Rather than storing isolated facts, HCP records the evidence that supports those facts, enabling explainability, auditability, historical preservation, governance integrity, AI transparency, and long-term trust.

Every major platform service may reference one or more Evidence Records.

---

# Vision

Every important fact should answer one question:

**"How do we know this?"**

---

# Philosophy

Facts create knowledge.

Evidence creates trust.

Trust creates communities.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Explainability by default.
- Immutable history.
- Reusable references.
- Provenance preservation.
- Privacy-aware evidence.
- AI transparency.
