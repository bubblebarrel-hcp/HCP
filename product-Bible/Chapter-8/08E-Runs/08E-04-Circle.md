# Purpose

This document defines the management of the Circle phase of a Run Session.

The Circle is one of the defining traditions of the Hash House Harriers. It serves as the social and ceremonial conclusion of a run where announcements are made, songs are sung, newcomers are welcomed, visitors are recognized, achievements are celebrated, and light-hearted "justice" is dispensed according to the traditions of each kennel.

HCP exists to document and preserve the Circle—not to dictate how it is conducted.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Respect local kennel traditions.
- Avoid enforcing a single interpretation of Circle customs.
- Preserve memories rather than interfere with participation.
- Allow documentation without disrupting the experience.
- Make Circle records available for future generations of Hashers.

# FR-CIRCLE-001 — Start Circle

## Priority

Critical

### Actors

- Grand Master
- Religious Advisor
- Authorized Officer

### Description

An authorized officer may officially begin the Circle.

### Postconditions

- Run Session enters Circle state.
- Circle timeline begins.
- Circle media tagging becomes available.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-002 — Circle Attendance

The platform shall record participants present during the Circle.

Attendance may differ from trail attendance.

The system shall preserve both records independently.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-003 — Announcements

Authorized officers may publish announcements during or after the Circle.

Examples:

- Upcoming runs
- Charity activities
- AGM notices
- Interhash travel
- Kennel updates
- Special celebrations

Announcements become part of the Run Capsule.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-004 — Visitor Recognition

Visiting Hashers may be formally recognized.

The platform may record:

- Home Kennel
- Country
- Number of previous visits
- First-time visitor status

Recognition contributes to the visitor's Hash Passport.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-005 — Virgins

The platform may record first-time participants ("Virgins") for historical purposes.

Where culturally appropriate, the system may award a "First Hash" Passport stamp.

Participation remains optional and subject to local traditions.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-006 — Down-Down Records

The platform may record Down-Downs where appropriate.

Recorded information may include:

- Recipient
- Reason
- Category
- Optional notes

The platform shall not judge or rank Down-Downs.

Kennels may disable this feature.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-007 — Songs

Circle songs may be documented.

Supported content:

- Song title
- Lyrics reference (where permitted)
- Audio recording (optional)
- Video recording (optional)

Songs become part of the historical Run Capsule.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-008 — Awards & Recognitions

The Circle may record:

- Hare recognition
- Best trail
- Milestones
- Service awards
- Fun awards
- Kennel-specific recognitions

Awards contribute to Passport achievements where applicable.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-009 — Circle Media

Participants may upload:

- Photos
- Videos

Media may be tagged specifically to the Circle phase.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-010 — Circle Notes

The Hash Scribe or authorized officers may capture notes summarizing the Circle.

These notes may later assist AI-generated trail reports.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-011 — AI Circle Summary

Where enabled, AI may generate a draft summary of the Circle using:

- Official notes
- Announcements
- Tagged media
- Recorded recognitions

The Hash Scribe remains responsible for reviewing and publishing the final report.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-012 — Close Circle

Authorized officers may formally close the Circle.

Closing transitions the Run Session into the Reporting phase.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-013 — Historical Preservation

Circle records become part of the permanent Run Capsule.

Historical records remain immutable except through audited administrative revisions.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-014 — Privacy Controls

Kennels may configure whether Circle content is:

- Public
- Members only
- Attendees only
- Officers only

Privacy settings apply independently from the overall Run Session.

---

# FR-CIRCLE-015 — Cultural Flexibility

The platform shall support configurable Circle workflows to accommodate the traditions of different kennels and regions.

HCP shall never require a fixed sequence of Circle activities.

---

# Business Principles

- HCP documents traditions; it does not define them.
- Circle culture belongs to each kennel.
- Documentation should respect the spontaneity and humor of the Hash.
- Historical preservation is prioritized over rigid standardization.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Circle phases are recorded.
- Announcements are preserved.
- Visitors and Virgins are recognized.
- Songs and awards are documented.
- Circle transitions cleanly into Reporting.
- Circle history becomes part of the Run Capsule.
