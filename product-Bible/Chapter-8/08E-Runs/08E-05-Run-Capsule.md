# Purpose

This document defines the Run Capsule, the permanent digital archive created from every completed Run Session.

A Run Capsule preserves the complete history of a run, including planning, participation, trail, media, Circle activities, reports, achievements, and memories.

Unlike traditional event archives, a Run Capsule is designed to remain useful decades after the run took place.

---

# Vision

A Run Capsule answers every important question about a run.

- What happened?
- Who attended?
- Who laid the trail?
- Where did the trail go?
- Where were the Beer Checks?
- What was written in the Trail Report?
- What photos were taken?
- What stories are remembered?

The Capsule is the definitive historical record of the Run Session.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Preserve, never replace, the memories of participants.
- Maintain historical integrity.
- Keep the archive searchable.
- Allow future generations to explore the history of their kennel.
- Respect privacy and local traditions.

---

# Capsule Contents

Each Run Capsule may contain:

- Run metadata
- Kennel information
- Hare(s)
- Co-Hare(s)
- Venue
- Theme
- Attendance
- Visitors
- Trail
- Waypoints
- Beer Stops
- Circle
- Trail Report
- AI Story
- Photos
- Videos
- Comments
- Reactions
- Awards
- Songs
- Weather
- Passport events
- Statistics
- Historical revisions

---

# FR-CAPSULE-001 — Automatic Capsule Creation

## Priority

Critical

The platform shall automatically generate a Run Capsule when a Run Session enters the Archived state.

The creation process shall require no manual intervention.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-002 — Immutable Historical Record

Once created, a Run Capsule shall become read-only.

Corrections shall be performed through audited revision records.

Original historical data shall remain preserved.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-003 — Interactive Timeline

Each Capsule shall include a chronological timeline showing key moments such as:

- Run announced
- Trail released
- First check-in
- Beer Stops reached
- Circle started
- Report published
- AI Story generated

Timeline entries shall be searchable.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-004 — Interactive Map Replay

The Capsule shall provide an interactive map showing:

- Planned trail
- Actual trail (where available)
- Beer Stops
- Waypoints
- Tagged media
- Scenic locations
- Hazards (if shared)

Map controls shall support zoom, replay, and point selection.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-005 — Living Trail Replay

Participants may revisit the trail through the Living Trail experience.

The replay shall display media, comments, reactions, and notes at their original geographic locations.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-006 — Media Archive

The Capsule shall organize media by:

- Trail segment
- Beer Stop
- Circle
- General gallery
- Contributor

Media shall remain linked to their original context.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-007 — Trail Report

The official Trail Report shall be permanently attached to the Capsule.

If AI assisted in generating the report, the final published version shall be clearly identified as reviewed and approved by the Hash Scribe.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-008 — Community Contributions

Participants may contribute after the run by adding:

- Photos
- Videos
- Comments
- Memories
- Corrections (subject to review)

Kennels may configure contribution deadlines.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-009 — Search & Discovery

Users may search Run Capsules using:

- Run number
- Date
- Kennel
- Hare
- Location
- Theme
- Visitor
- Country
- Tag
- Keyword

Search results shall respect privacy settings.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-010 — Historical Analytics

The Capsule shall display analytics such as:

- Attendance
- Visitor count
- Media contributions
- Trail distance
- Duration
- Beer Stops
- Engagement

Analytics provide historical insight without ranking participants.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-011 — Passport Integration

Publishing a Run Capsule shall trigger updates to participants' Hash Passports.

Examples include:

- Run attendance
- First visit to a kennel
- First international run
- Trail laid
- Trail report authored
- Milestones reached

Passport updates shall be automatic.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-012 — Preservation Policy

Run Capsules shall not be permanently deleted once participants have attended the run.

Administrative removal shall be limited to exceptional circumstances and recorded through audited processes.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-013 — Sharing

Capsules may be shared using:

- Public link
- QR Code
- Read-only mode

Sharing shall respect the kennel's privacy settings.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-014 — Historical Collections

The platform shall support curated collections such as:

- Best Trails of the Year
- Anniversary Runs
- Red Dress Runs
- Charity Runs
- Interhash Participation
- Kennel Milestones

Collections help preserve institutional memory.

---

# FR-CAPSULE-015 — Legacy Preservation

Run Capsules shall remain accessible across future platform versions through migration strategies and data preservation policies.

The platform shall treat historical data as a long-term community asset.

---

# Business Principles

- Every Run deserves a permanent place in history.
- Historical accuracy is more valuable than convenience.
- Community memories belong to the community.
- The archive should become richer over time.
- Preservation should outlive technology changes.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Every Run Session automatically becomes a Run Capsule.
- Historical records are immutable.
- Media remains contextually linked.
- Search and replay are available.
- Passport updates are triggered.
- Capsules become the definitive historical record of each run.
