# Purpose

The Security, Privacy & Trust Platform safeguards the confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity, and responsible stewardship of information within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its mission extends beyond cybersecurity to preserving member confidence, institutional trust, and the long-term integrity of community history.

---

# Vision

Protect trust.

Protect people.

Protect history.

---

# Philosophy

Security is not an obstacle.

Security enables confidence.

Privacy demonstrates respect.

Trust is earned continuously.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Zero Trust Architecture.
- Privacy by Design.
- Least Privilege.
- Defense in Depth.
- Secure by Default.
- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Continuous Verification.

# Relationships

# Success Criteria

The platform succeeds when:

- Members trust the platform.
- Security is transparent.
- Privacy is respected.
- Incidents are explainable.
- Access remains appropriate.
- Sensitive information remains protected.
