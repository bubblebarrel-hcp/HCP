# Purpose

This document defines privacy controls, consent management, data governance, member rights, regulatory compliance, and organizational accountability within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its objective is to ensure that personal information is handled transparently, respectfully, and in accordance with applicable legal and organizational requirements while preserving member trust.

---

# Vision

Respect every member.

Protect every choice.

Honor every right.

---

# Philosophy

Privacy is respect in action.

Consent should be informed.

Transparency should be the default.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Privacy by Design.
- Data minimization.
- Explicit consent.
- Member control.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Regulatory adaptability.

---

# FR-SEC-021 — Privacy Preferences

Members may control:

- Profile visibility
- Activity visibility
- Contact preferences
- Search discoverability
- Photo permissions
- AI participation (where applicable)
- Notification preferences

Preferences are respected across all platform modules.

---

# FR-SEC-022 — Consent Management

Consent records include:

- Purpose
- Scope
- Timestamp
- Version of consent text
- Method of collection
- Withdrawal history

Consent may be withdrawn where legally and operationally appropriate.

---

# FR-SEC-023 — Member Data Rights

Members may request:

- Access to personal information
- Correction of inaccurate information
- Export of eligible personal data
- Deletion where permitted
- Restriction of processing
- Consent review history

Requests are processed through auditable workflows.

---

# FR-SEC-024 — Data Retention

Retention policies define:

- Operational records
- Historical archives
- Evidence records
- Governance records
- Audit logs
- Media assets

Retention balances legal obligations with community history preservation.

---

# FR-SEC-025 — Privacy Dashboard

Members have access to a Privacy Dashboard displaying:

- Data collected
- Purpose of collection
- Active consents
- Connected applications
- Active sessions
- Export history
- Deletion requests

The dashboard promotes transparency and self-service.

---

# FR-SEC-026 — Compliance Framework

The platform supports alignment with applicable privacy and security regulations through configurable organizational policies.

Compliance features include:

- Audit reporting
- Consent records
- Data export
- Retention controls
- Access history
- Policy versioning

---

# FR-SEC-027 — Cross-Border Data Governance

Organizations may define policies governing:

- Data residency
- Cross-border transfers
- Regional storage requirements
- Jurisdiction-specific controls

Policy enforcement is configurable.

---

# FR-SEC-028 — Privacy Auditing

Privacy-related events include:

- Consent granted
- Consent withdrawn
- Data export
- Data deletion
- Access requests
- Policy changes

Privacy events integrate with the Audit and Evidence platforms.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-SEC-001

Personal information shall be collected only for defined purposes.

---

## BR-SEC-002

Consent shall be specific, informed, and recorded.

---

## BR-SEC-003

Privacy preferences shall be honored consistently across the platform.

---

## BR-SEC-004

Data retention policies shall be transparent and reviewable.

---

## BR-SEC-005

Privacy-related actions shall be auditable.

---

## BR-SEC-006

Compliance policies shall be configurable by organization where permitted.

---

## BR-SEC-007

Historical preservation requirements shall be balanced with individual privacy rights in accordance with applicable law and governance policy.

---

## BR-SEC-008

Privacy controls shall remain understandable to ordinary members.
