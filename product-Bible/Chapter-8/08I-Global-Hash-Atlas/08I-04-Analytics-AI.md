# Purpose

This document defines the analytics, AI-assisted discovery, and cultural insight capabilities of the Global Hash Atlas.

The Atlas should reveal meaningful patterns that help Hashers discover communities, understand traditions, and preserve the evolution of the worldwide Hash movement.

The purpose is understanding—not surveillance.

---

# Philosophy

Data becomes valuable when it helps tell the community's story.

The Atlas should answer questions that would otherwise take years of research.

AI should help reveal patterns while remaining transparent, explainable, and respectful of privacy.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Community before metrics.
- Transparency before automation.
- Insights before engagement.
- Privacy by design.
- Every insight should be traceable to historical records.

---

# Analytics Categories

The Atlas supports insights across:

- Community Growth
- Travel
- Trails
- Events
- Culture
- History
- Participation
- Preservation

---

# FR-ATLAS-031 — Community Growth Dashboard

## Priority

High

The Atlas visualizes the growth of the Hash community over time.

Examples include:

- New kennels founded by year
- Active vs dormant kennels
- Geographic expansion
- Growth by continent
- Growth by country
- Anniversary milestones

Users may filter by time period.

---

# FR-ATLAS-032 — Cultural Insights

AI identifies recurring traditions such as:

- Annual Red Dress Runs
- Full Moon Runs
- Charity events
- Holiday traditions
- Memorial Runs
- Regional customs

Insights link back to the Run Capsules from which they were derived.

---

# FR-ATLAS-033 — Trail Intelligence

The Atlas provides aggregate trail insights including:

- Average trail length by region
- Common terrain types
- Seasonal trends
- Elevation profiles
- Typical completion times
- Popular trail formats

Individual private trails remain protected.

---

# FR-ATLAS-034 — Visitor Movement

Using anonymized, aggregated data (with user consent), the Atlas may visualize:

- International visitor flows
- Regional exchanges
- Popular travel corridors
- Interhash participation trends

No individual's movements are exposed without explicit permission.

---

# FR-ATLAS-035 — Historical Milestones

The Atlas automatically highlights significant moments, such as:

- Founding anniversaries
- Landmark runs
- First recorded Hash in a country
- Longest continuously active kennel
- Historic attendance records

Milestones are linked to supporting historical evidence.

---

# FR-ATLAS-036 — AI Discovery

AI assists users in finding interesting content by answering questions like:

- Which coastal kennels have the most scenic trails?
- Which cities have the oldest active Hash traditions?
- Which kennels regularly host charity runs?
- Show me jungle trails in Southeast Asia.

Every answer includes references to underlying Run Capsules or kennel records.

---

# FR-ATLAS-037 — Story Constellations

The Atlas may visualize relationships between:

- Shared Hares
- Visiting Hashers
- Twin kennels
- Sister events
- Recurring traditions

These relationships form an interactive graph of community connections.

---

# FR-ATLAS-038 — Historical Trends

Users may explore long-term trends, including:

- Attendance over decades
- Geographic expansion
- Event frequency
- Trail diversity
- Community participation
- Preservation progress

Trend charts are available at global, regional, and kennel levels.

---

# FR-ATLAS-039 — Preservation Index

Each kennel receives a Preservation Index based on:

- Number of preserved Run Capsules
- Trail Reports archived
- Media completeness
- Historical metadata quality
- Timeline coverage

The index is informational and encourages preservation rather than competition.

---

# FR-ATLAS-040 — Cultural Archive Explorer

Users may browse collections such as:

- Oldest surviving Trail Reports
- Historic songs
- Legendary Beer Checks
- Memorial collections
- Founding stories
- Oral histories

These collections support long-term cultural preservation.

---

# Business Principles

- Preserve culture, not just content.
- Encourage stewardship rather than competition.
- Make AI explain its work.
- Celebrate diversity across kennels.
- Respect the uniqueness of local traditions.

---

# Completion Criteria

This document is complete when:

- Community analytics are defined.
- AI-assisted discovery is transparent.
- Historical trends are supported.
- Cultural preservation tools exist.
- Privacy safeguards are documented.
