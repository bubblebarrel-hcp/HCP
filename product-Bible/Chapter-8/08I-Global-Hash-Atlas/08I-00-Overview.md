# Purpose

The Global Hash Atlas is the geographic discovery platform of HCP.

It provides an interactive world map through which users can explore the worldwide Hash House Harriers community.

Rather than acting as a simple directory, the Atlas connects geography with history, people, trails, and stories.

---

# Vision

Imagine opening HCP.

Instead of landing on a dashboard...

You see the world.

Thousands of Hash communities are quietly waiting to be explored.

Zoom into any location.

Discover kennels.

Open historic runs.

Replay legendary trails.

Meet people.

Read stories.

Travel through decades of Hash history.

---

# Philosophy

Every trail happens somewhere.

Every memory belongs somewhere.

Every story has a location.

The Atlas is the geographic memory of the Hash.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Geography should encourage discovery.
- Maps should tell stories.
- Exploration should be effortless.
- Every location has history.
- Local traditions deserve global visibility.
