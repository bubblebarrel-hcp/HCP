# Purpose

The Platform Operations & Observability Service provides the operational foundation required to monitor, manage, troubleshoot, and continuously improve the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The platform observes infrastructure, applications, business processes, and member experience to ensure long-term reliability.

---

# Vision

Healthy systems create healthy communities.

Observe everything.

Understand what matters.

---

# Philosophy

Monitoring detects problems.

Observability explains them.

Operations prevent them from happening again.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Business-first monitoring.
- End-to-end visibility.
- Explainable incidents.
- Continuous improvement.
- Automation where appropriate.
- Human-centered operations.
- Operational resilience.

---

# Scope

The Platform Operations Service supports:

- Infrastructure monitoring
- Service monitoring
- Business monitoring
- Distributed tracing
- Centralized logging
- Metrics
- Alerting
- Incident management
- Configuration management
- Release management
- Capacity planning
- Operational dashboards

---

# Core Objects

The platform manages:

- Service
- Incident
- Alert
- Metric
- Trace
- Log Entry
- Health Check
- Dashboard
- Deployment
- Feature Flag
- Operational Policy

---

# Relationships

Operations integrates with:

- Every Platform Service
- Every Business Module
- Analytics
- Notification Center
- Identity
- AI Platform
- Evidence Record Service

---

# Success Criteria

Operations succeeds when:

- Problems are detected quickly.
- Root causes are explainable.
- Recovery is predictable.
- Business disruption is minimized.
- Operational knowledge improves continuously.

---

# Business Principles

Observe continuously.

Respond intelligently.

Improve relentlessly.

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Operational philosophy is established.
- Platform scope is defined.
- Core operational objects are documented.
- Integration points are complete.
