# Purpose

This document establishes governance, operational policies, analytics, federation capabilities, and business rules for the Search & Discovery Service.

The objective is to ensure that search remains accurate, transparent, permission-aware, performant, and trustworthy throughout the lifetime of the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

---

# Vision

Search should inspire confidence.

Discovery should strengthen the community.

Knowledge should remain accessible across generations.

---

# Philosophy

Trustworthy discovery depends on trustworthy governance.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Privacy before convenience.
- Explainability before automation.
- Relevance before popularity.
- Knowledge before information.
- Long-term preservation.

---

# FR-SRCH-021 — Search Governance

Organizations define policies governing:

- Search visibility
- Searchable object types
- AI discovery permissions
- Historical archive visibility
- Federated search participation
- Knowledge publication

Policies are version-controlled and auditable.

---

# FR-SRCH-022 — Search Analytics

Analytics include:

- Query volume
- Zero-result searches
- Successful discoveries
- Search latency
- Click-through rate
- Saved searches
- AI-assisted discovery usage
- Knowledge reuse

Analytics improve platform usability rather than monitor individuals.

---

# FR-SRCH-023 — Federated Search

Authorized organizations may participate in federated discovery.

Examples include:

- National associations
- Regional councils
- Interhash committees
- Heritage archives
- Research initiatives

Federation respects organizational boundaries and access permissions.

---

# FR-SRCH-024 — Search Auditing

Audit records include:

- Search policy changes
- Index rebuilds
- AI ranking updates
- Permission model changes
- Discovery collection sharing

Audit history integrates with the Evidence Record Service.

---

# FR-SRCH-025 — AI Governance

Organizations define policies for:

- AI summaries
- AI recommendations
- Story generation
- Semantic search
- Discovery assistance

AI-generated content clearly identifies supporting evidence and confidence levels.

---

# FR-SRCH-026 — Search Reliability

The service supports:

- Distributed indexing
- Multi-region replication
- Incremental rebuilding
- Snapshot restoration
- Consistency validation
- Automatic failover

Search remains available during infrastructure disruptions.

---

# FR-SRCH-027 — Search Lifecycle

Indexed objects transition through:

- Created
- Indexed
- Updated
- Archived
- Heritage Indexed
- Retired

Lifecycle changes preserve historical discoverability where permitted.

---

# FR-SRCH-028 — Knowledge Preservation

Search shall prioritize the long-term discoverability of:

- Heritage materials
- Constitutional records
- Historical Run Capsules
- Significant governance decisions
- Landmark events
- Legacy member biographies

Preservation policies balance accessibility with privacy obligations.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-SRCH-001

Every indexed object must have a canonical identity.

---

## BR-SRCH-002

Search shall never expose inaccessible information.

---

## BR-SRCH-003

Every AI-generated answer shall reference supporting evidence.

---

## BR-SRCH-004

Ranking decisions should remain explainable.

---

## BR-SRCH-005

Federated search shall respect local organizational policies.

---

## BR-SRCH-006

Historical records shall remain discoverable according to preservation policies.

---

## BR-SRCH-007

Search indexes shall preserve referential integrity across the Living Community Graph.

---

## BR-SRCH-008

Search quality improvements shall never compromise evidence integrity or member privacy.
