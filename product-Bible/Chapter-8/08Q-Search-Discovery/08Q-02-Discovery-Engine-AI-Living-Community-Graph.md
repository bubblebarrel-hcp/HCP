# Purpose

This document defines the Discovery Engine, AI-assisted search capabilities, relationship exploration, and the Living Community Graph that powers intelligent discovery across the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to transform search from retrieving records into exploring the collective memory, relationships, and history of the community.

---

# Vision

Search should answer questions.

Discovery should inspire new ones.

---

# Philosophy

Every object tells a story.

Every relationship adds meaning.

Every generation enriches the graph.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Relationship-first discovery.
- Explainable AI.
- Context-aware exploration.
- Permission-respecting intelligence.
- Human-centered recommendations.
- Continuous enrichment.

---

# FR-SRCH-011 — Living Community Graph

The Living Community Graph models relationships among:

- Members
- Organizations
- Events
- Trails
- Passport Journeys
- Awards
- Conversations
- Evidence
- Knowledge Articles
- Locations
- Media
- Historical Archives

Relationships evolve continuously as community activity grows.

---

# FR-SRCH-012 — AI Discovery Assistant

Members may ask natural-language questions such as:

- Who have I hashed with the most?
- Which trails have I never completed?
- Show me every Interhash I've attended.
- Which members became Grand Masters after joining my kennel?
- Which events produced the most Run Capsules?

AI translates questions into explainable discovery workflows.

---

# FR-SRCH-013 — Relationship Explorer

Members may navigate relationship paths including:

- Member → Event
- Event → Trail
- Trail → Run Capsule
- Run Capsule → Evidence
- Evidence → Governance
- Governance → Organization

Relationship paths are visual and interactive.

---

# FR-SRCH-014 — Historical Discovery

The platform supports exploration by:

- Time
- Era
- Leadership period
- Anniversary
- Historical milestone
- Geographic expansion

Historical narratives link related community events.

---

# FR-SRCH-015 — AI Story Builder

AI may generate contextual narratives such as:

- The history of a kennel.
- A member's hashing journey.
- The evolution of an annual event.
- How a tradition began.
- The development of trail styles over time.

Narratives include citations to supporting Evidence Records.

---

# FR-SRCH-016 — Serendipitous Discovery

The platform may recommend unexpected but relevant discoveries including:

- Members with similar journeys.
- Forgotten historical photographs.
- Related Run Capsules.
- Parallel governance decisions.
- Anniversary milestones.

Recommendations remain optional.

---

# FR-SRCH-017 — Discovery Collections

Members may save collections including:

- Historic trails.
- Favorite events.
- Heritage stories.
- Member biographies.
- Research projects.

Collections may be shared with others.

---

# FR-SRCH-018 — Community Insights

AI may generate aggregate insights such as:

- Growth trends.
- Event participation.
- Geographic expansion.
- Volunteer engagement.
- Historical patterns.

Insights never expose private information.

---

# FR-SRCH-019 — Explainable AI Discovery

Every AI-generated insight includes:

- Supporting Evidence Records.
- Confidence indicators.
- Related objects.
- Discovery pathway.
- Data freshness.

Members may inspect how conclusions were reached.

---

# FR-SRCH-020 — Continuous Graph Enrichment

Every verified platform activity enrichishes the Living Community Graph including:

- New relationships.
- Verified evidence.
- Community knowledge.
- Historical narratives.
- AI-generated summaries.
- Organizational memory.

The graph continuously grows throughout the lifetime of HCP.
