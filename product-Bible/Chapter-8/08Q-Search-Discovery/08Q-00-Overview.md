# Purpose

The Search & Discovery Service provides the unified discovery infrastructure for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Rather than simply retrieving matching records, the platform enables members to discover people, places, events, knowledge, relationships, evidence, and history through intelligent, context-aware exploration.

The service connects every domain within HCP into a single searchable knowledge network.

---

Every answer should reveal deeper connections.
