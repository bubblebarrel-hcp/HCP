# Purpose

This document defines person-to-person messaging, organizational announcements, broadcast communication, intelligent communication workflows, and AI-assisted messaging within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure that every communication reaches the right audience, through the right channel, with the appropriate level of visibility and permanence.

---

# Vision

Every message has an audience.

Every announcement has a purpose.

Every broadcast has accountability.

---

# Philosophy

Not all communication is conversation.

Some communication informs.

Some coordinates.

Some celebrates.

Some preserves history.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Audience-aware communication.
- Context-sensitive delivery.
- Respect attention.
- Minimize noise.
- Preserve important information.
- AI should assist, not overwhelm.

---

# FR-COM-011 — Direct Messaging

Members may initiate direct conversations with other members, subject to privacy settings.

Supported capabilities include:

- One-to-one messaging
- Group messaging
- Media sharing
- Voice notes
- Location sharing
- Event sharing
- Trail sharing
- Passport references
- Evidence references

Blocked members cannot initiate new conversations.

---

# FR-COM-012 — Announcements

Organizations, committees, and event organizers may publish announcements.

Announcement attributes include:

- Title
- Audience
- Priority
- Effective period
- Attachments
- Related objects
- Expiration date

Announcements may be pinned within relevant workspaces.

---

# FR-COM-013 — Broadcast Channels

Broadcast channels support one-to-many communication.

Examples:

- Global HCP Updates
- National Association News
- Kennel Announcements
- Event Bulletins
- Safety Alerts
- Heritage Publications

Broadcast permissions are role-controlled.

---

# FR-COM-014 — Communication Targeting

Messages and broadcasts may target audiences using filters such as:

- Organization membership
- Event registration
- Volunteer role
- Geographic region
- Language
- Passport achievements
- Committee membership
- Notification preferences

Targeting rules are auditable.

---

# FR-COM-015 — Scheduled Communication

Authorized users may schedule:

- Announcements
- Reminder messages
- Event countdowns
- Governance notices
- Membership renewals
- Anniversary celebrations

Scheduled communications support preview and approval workflows.

---

# FR-COM-016 — AI Communication Assistant

AI may assist with:

- Drafting announcements
- Summarizing discussions
- Translating messages
- Improving clarity
- Suggesting recipients
- Detecting duplicate announcements
- Identifying unanswered questions

Human approval is required before publishing organizational communications.

---

# FR-COM-017 — Communication Analytics

Communication metrics may include:

- Delivery status
- Read rate
- Engagement
- Reaction summaries
- Click-through rate
- Participation trends

Analytics support organizational improvement rather than member surveillance.

---

# FR-COM-018 — Emergency Communication

Authorized organizations may issue emergency broadcasts.

Examples include:

- Trail safety alerts
- Weather advisories
- Venue changes
- Medical incidents
- Event cancellations

Emergency broadcasts may override standard notification preferences where appropriate and lawful.

---

# FR-COM-019 — Communication Retention

Retention policies vary by communication type.

Examples:

- Direct messages
- Governance discussions
- Event announcements
- Historical publications
- Heritage communications

Retention respects legal obligations and organizational policies.

---

# FR-COM-020 — AI Conversation Summaries

AI may generate summaries for:

- Long discussions
- Event planning channels
- Committee meetings
- Governance debates
- Volunteer coordination
- Trail planning

Summaries include links to original conversations and indicate whether they are AI-generated.
