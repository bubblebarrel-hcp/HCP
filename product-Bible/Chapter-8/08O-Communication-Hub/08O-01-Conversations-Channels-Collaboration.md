# Purpose

This document defines the conversation architecture for the Hash Community Platform (HCP), including channels, threads, collaboration spaces, participation models, and contextual communication.

Every conversation exists within a defined context and contributes to the platform's living knowledge base.

---

# Vision

Every conversation belongs to something.

Context gives conversations meaning.

History gives conversations value.

---

# Philosophy

People don't wake up wanting to chat.

They wake up wanting to organize events, plan trails, preserve stories, solve problems, and strengthen friendships.

Communication simply enables those goals.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Context-first communication.
- Persistent collaboration.
- Searchable conversations.
- Threaded discussions.
- Inclusive participation.
- AI-assisted understanding.

---

# FR-COM-001 — Conversation Context

Every conversation must belong to a context.

Supported contexts include:

- Organization
- Event
- Trail
- Run Capsule
- Governance Motion
- Committee
- Passport Journey
- Heritage Project
- Identity
- AI Workspace

Standalone conversations are supported only for direct messaging.

---

# FR-COM-002 — Channel Types

The platform supports reusable channel types such as:

- Announcements
- General Discussion
- Planning
- Logistics
- Documents
- Media
- Volunteers
- Finance
- Governance
- Archive
- Questions & Answers
- Social

Organizations may define custom channel templates.

---

# FR-COM-003 — Automatic Channel Provisioning

When a supported object is created, HCP automatically provisions its default communication channels.

Examples:

**Event**

- Announcements
- General Discussion
- Volunteers
- Transport
- Accommodation
- Organizers (Private)

**Committee**

- General
- Decisions
- Documents
- Meetings
- Archive

**Run Capsule**

- Draft Discussion
- Fact Verification
- Media Review
- Publication

Administrators may customize templates before activation.

---

# FR-COM-004 — Threads

Every message may contain threaded discussions.

Threads preserve conversation flow without disrupting the primary channel.

Thread history remains searchable.

---

# FR-COM-005 — Rich Messages

Messages may include:

- Text
- Images
- Videos
- Voice notes
- Documents
- Maps
- Polls
- Checklists
- Code blocks
- Hyperlinks
- Evidence references
- AI summaries

Attachments are managed through the shared attachment service.

---

# FR-COM-006 — Collaboration Objects

Conversations may embed platform objects directly, including:

- Events
- Trail maps
- Passport entries
- Evidence Records
- Governance motions
- Meeting agendas
- Awards
- Run Capsules

Embedded objects remain live and synchronized.

---

# FR-COM-007 — Mentions & Presence

The platform supports:

- Member mentions
- Role mentions
- Committee mentions
- Organization mentions
- Online presence
- Typing indicators
- Availability status

Presence visibility respects privacy settings.

---

# FR-COM-008 — Reactions & Feedback

Participants may interact using:

- Emoji reactions
- Approval acknowledgements
- Decision markers
- Quick voting
- Read confirmations

These interactions may contribute to engagement analytics where appropriate.

---

# FR-COM-009 — Collaborative Editing

Supported conversations may include collaborative editing of:

- Meeting notes
- Trail reports
- Run Capsules
- Governance drafts
- Historical documents

Version history is preserved through the Evidence Record Service.

---

# FR-COM-010 — Conversation Lifecycle

Conversations may transition through states:

- Active
- Archived
- Locked
- Read-only
- Heritage Archive

Archived conversations remain searchable according to access policies.

---

# Business Principles

Conversations should remain understandable.

Knowledge should remain discoverable.

History should remain preserved.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Conversation model is defined.
- Channel architecture is documented.
- Collaboration workflows are specified.
- Context-first communication is established.
