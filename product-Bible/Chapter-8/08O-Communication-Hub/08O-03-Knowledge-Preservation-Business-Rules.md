# Purpose

This document defines how communication is preserved as organizational knowledge, federated across trusted communities, governed through business rules, and protected for future generations.

The Communication Hub is not merely a messaging platform—it is a long-term knowledge infrastructure for the Hash Community.

---

# Vision

Today's conversations become tomorrow's guidance.

Today's decisions become tomorrow's history.

Today's friendships become tomorrow's stories.

---

# Philosophy

Messages are temporary.

Knowledge is enduring.

History is priceless.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Preserve knowledge, not noise.
- Respect privacy.
- Archive intentionally.
- Share responsibly.
- Learn continuously.
- Protect community memory.

---

# FR-COM-021 — Knowledge Promotion

Authorized users may promote conversations into structured knowledge assets.

Examples include:

- Event playbooks
- Trail planning guides
- Volunteer manuals
- Governance FAQs
- Committee procedures
- Safety guidance
- Heritage stories

Promoted knowledge links back to the originating conversations and Evidence Records.

---

# FR-COM-022 — Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge assets progress through lifecycle stages:

- Draft
- Under Review
- Approved
- Published
- Archived
- Heritage Collection

Version history is permanently preserved.

---

# FR-COM-023 — Communication Federation

Trusted organizations may federate selected communication spaces.

Examples include:

- National announcements
- Interhash coordination
- Regional committees
- Heritage collaborations
- Joint event planning

Federation respects access controls and organizational boundaries.

---

# FR-COM-024 — Heritage Conversations

Organizations may designate conversations as heritage material.

Examples include:

- Founding discussions
- Constitutional debates
- Landmark event planning
- Anniversary reflections
- Oral history interviews
- Legacy member conversations

Heritage conversations receive enhanced preservation and curation.

---

# FR-COM-025 — AI Knowledge Extraction

AI may assist by identifying:

- Frequently asked questions
- Repeated solutions
- Best practices
- Lessons learned
- Common workflows
- Emerging themes

Extracted knowledge requires human approval before publication.

---

# FR-COM-026 — Conversation Governance

Organizations define policies for:

- Channel creation
- Retention periods
- Moderation
- Archiving
- Knowledge promotion
- Federation
- AI participation

Policies are version-controlled and auditable.

---

# FR-COM-027 — Communication Audit

Audit records include:

- Channel creation
- Membership changes
- Moderation actions
- Announcement publication
- Knowledge promotion
- Policy updates

Audit records integrate with the Evidence Record Service.

---

# FR-COM-028 — Business Continuity

The Communication Hub shall support:

- Backup and restoration
- Geographic replication
- Disaster recovery
- Long-term archival
- Search index rebuilding
- Integrity verification

Critical communications remain recoverable across decades.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-COM-001

Every communication belongs to a defined context.

---

## BR-COM-002

Knowledge assets reference their originating conversations.

---

## BR-COM-003

AI-generated knowledge must be clearly identified and human-approved before publication.

---

## BR-COM-004

Heritage conversations receive enhanced preservation.

---

## BR-COM-005

Communication policies are organization-specific but follow platform governance standards.

---

## BR-COM-006

Federated communication never bypasses local authorization policies.

---

## BR-COM-007

Important conversations remain searchable according to access permissions.

---

## BR-COM-008

Institutional knowledge should remain accessible across leadership transitions.
