# Purpose

This document establishes the framework for identifying, responding to, communicating about, resolving, and learning from operational incidents affecting the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The goal is to minimize community impact while continuously improving operational resilience.

---

# Vision

Fast detection.

Coordinated response.

Continuous learning.

---

# Philosophy

Incidents are managed through preparation, communication, disciplined execution, and post-incident learning.

The objective is not to eliminate all failures but to respond consistently and responsibly.

---

# Incident Lifecycle

The standard lifecycle includes:

1. Detection
2. Assessment
3. Classification
4. Response
5. Communication
6. Resolution
7. Recovery
8. Post-Incident Review
9. Improvement Actions

Every incident follows a documented process.

---

# Severity Levels

Examples include:

**SEV-1**

Critical platform outage.

Major community impact.

Immediate executive response.

---

**SEV-2**

Major feature unavailable.

Significant operational disruption.

Rapid response required.

---

**SEV-3**

Limited service degradation.

Workarounds available.

Normal operational response.

---

**SEV-4**

Minor defects.

Minimal community impact.

Scheduled resolution.

---

# Response Principles

Response teams shall:

- Prioritize safety.
- Protect community data.
- Restore critical services first.
- Communicate regularly.
- Escalate appropriately.
- Record decisions.
- Coordinate transparently.

---

# Communication

During significant incidents the platform provides:

- Status updates.
- Estimated impact.
- Current mitigation efforts.
- Recovery progress.
- Resolution confirmation.
- Post-incident summary.

Communication remains factual and timely.

---

# Post-Incident Reviews

Every significant incident shall include:

- Timeline.
- Root cause analysis.
- Contributing factors.
- Successful actions.
- Improvement opportunities.
- Assigned follow-up actions.
- Verification of completion.

Learning is mandatory.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-006

Incident severity shall determine response procedures.

---

## BR-OE-007

Community communication shall remain timely and accurate.

---

## BR-OE-008

Major incidents require documented post-incident reviews.

---

## BR-OE-009

Improvement actions shall be tracked to completion.

---

## BR-OE-010

Operational learning shall be shared across relevant teams.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Incident Management succeeds when:

- Detection improves.
- Recovery becomes faster.
- Communication remains trusted.
- Recurring incidents decrease.
- Communities maintain confidence.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Incident framework is documented.
- Severity model is established.
- Post-incident review process is defined.
