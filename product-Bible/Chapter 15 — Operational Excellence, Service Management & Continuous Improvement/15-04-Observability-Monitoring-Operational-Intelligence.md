# Purpose

This document defines the observability strategy, monitoring framework, telemetry standards, and operational intelligence practices for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to enable proactive operations through comprehensive visibility into platform health, performance, reliability, and community experience.

---

# Vision

Observe everything that matters.

Detect problems early.

Understand community impact.

---

# Philosophy

Monitoring answers whether systems are healthy.

Observability explains why they are not.

Operational intelligence transforms data into actionable decisions.

---

# Observability Pillars

The platform shall collect and correlate:

- Metrics
- Logs
- Traces
- Events
- Audit records
- Community experience indicators

These signals provide a holistic operational view.

---

# Monitoring Categories

The platform monitors:

- Infrastructure health
- Application performance
- Database performance
- API availability
- Authentication services
- AI services
- Background jobs
- Notification systems
- Storage health
- Security events

Monitoring remains continuous.

---

# Community Experience Monitoring

Operational intelligence shall include:

- Login success rates
- Event check-in completion
- Governance workflow completion
- Archive search performance
- Volunteer onboarding success
- AI response latency
- Accessibility interaction metrics

Communities remain the primary operational focus.

---

# Alerting Principles

Alerts shall be:

- Actionable
- Prioritized
- Context-rich
- Routed appropriately
- Deduplicated
- Continuously refined

Alert fatigue shall be minimized.

---

# Operational Dashboards

Dashboards provide visibility into:

- Platform health
- Community health
- Reliability trends
- Service dependencies
- Incident timelines
- Deployment health
- Capacity forecasts

Dashboards support informed decisions.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-021

Critical services shall maintain continuous monitoring.

---

## BR-OE-022

Monitoring shall include community-facing workflows.

---

## BR-OE-023

Alerts shall provide sufficient context for rapid response.

---

## BR-OE-024

Observability data shall support incident investigation.

---

## BR-OE-025

Operational dashboards shall remain continuously available.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Observability succeeds when:

- Issues are detected rapidly.
- Root causes are easier to identify.
- Community impact is measurable.
- Alert quality improves.
- Operational decisions become data-driven.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Observability strategy is documented.
- Monitoring framework is established.
- Operational intelligence model is defined.
