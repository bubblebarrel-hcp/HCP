# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework, operational performance metrics, review cadence, and continuous improvement processes for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure operational excellence remains measurable, transparent, and continuously evolving as the platform grows.

---

# Vision

Govern with clarity.

Measure what matters.

Improve continuously.

---

# Philosophy

Operational governance aligns people, processes, technology, and data around shared objectives.

Performance metrics should drive learning and improvement rather than compliance alone.

---

# Governance Principles

Operational governance shall:

- Define accountability.
- Establish review cadences.
- Promote transparency.
- Encourage evidence-based decisions.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Align operational objectives with community outcomes.

Governance enables consistency.

---

# Operational Metrics

Representative metrics include:

## Reliability

- Availability
- Error budget consumption
- SLO compliance
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Restore (MTTR)

---

## Quality

- Defect escape rate
- Regression success
- Release confidence
- Test effectiveness

---

## Community Success

- Community satisfaction
- Support response time
- Support resolution time
- Community health score
- Adoption metrics

---

## Operational Efficiency

- Deployment frequency
- Change success rate
- Automation coverage
- Toil reduction
- Knowledge base utilization

---

## Security

- Security incident rate
- Vulnerability remediation time
- Compliance status
- Audit completion

---

# Review Cadence

Operational reviews occur:

- Daily operational review
- Weekly engineering review
- Monthly reliability review
- Quarterly governance review
- Annual operational strategy review

Each review has defined objectives and outputs.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Improvement initiatives may originate from:

- Operational metrics
- Community feedback
- Incident reviews
- Quality reviews
- Security assessments
- Accessibility reviews
- Engineering retrospectives

Every improvement shall be measurable.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-046

Operational metrics shall remain transparent to appropriate stakeholders.

---

## BR-OE-047

Review cadences shall be consistently maintained.

---

## BR-OE-048

Improvement initiatives shall define measurable outcomes.

---

## BR-OE-049

Governance decisions shall be evidence-based.

---

## BR-OE-050

Operational governance shall evolve with organizational maturity.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Operational Governance succeeds when:

- Operational performance improves.
- Decisions become data-informed.
- Accountability remains clear.
- Community outcomes improve.
- Continuous improvement becomes organizational culture.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Governance framework is documented.
- Operational metrics are defined.
- Review cadence is established.
