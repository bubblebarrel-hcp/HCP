# Purpose

This document defines the quality engineering philosophy, testing strategy, verification practices, and continuous quality framework for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure quality is designed, validated, and continuously improved throughout the software lifecycle.

---

# Vision

Quality by design.

Verification through evidence.

Continuous confidence.

---

# Philosophy

Quality is the shared responsibility of every discipline involved in delivering HCP.

Testing provides confidence that quality objectives have been achieved.

---

# Quality Principles

Quality engineering shall:

- Begin during design.
- Continue through development.
- Include accessibility.
- Include security.
- Include performance.
- Include usability.
- Include maintainability.

Quality spans the entire lifecycle.

---

# Testing Strategy

The testing strategy includes:

- Unit testing
- Integration testing
- End-to-end testing
- API testing
- Performance testing
- Security testing
- Accessibility testing
- Regression testing
- Exploratory testing
- User acceptance testing

Testing remains risk-based.

---

# Continuous Verification

Verification occurs through:

- Automated pipelines
- Static analysis
- Dependency scanning
- Code review
- Deployment validation
- Production monitoring
- Community feedback

Verification continues after release.

---

# Test Data Management

Testing environments shall provide:

- Representative datasets
- Privacy-safe data
- Repeatable scenarios
- Automated provisioning
- Version-controlled fixtures

Reliable tests require reliable data.

---

# Defect Management

Defects shall be:

- Prioritized by impact
- Categorized consistently
- Tracked transparently
- Verified after resolution
- Analyzed for trends

Defect learning improves quality.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-036

---

---

---

## BR-OE-040

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Quality Engineering succeeds when:

- Defect escape rates decline.
- Test confidence improves.
- Deployment quality increases.
- Accessibility compliance improves.
- Community satisfaction increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Quality strategy is documented.
- Testing framework is established.
- Continuous verification model is defined.
