# Purpose

This document defines the customer success strategy, support operations, communication principles, and community care framework for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure that every interaction with Bubble Barrel reinforces trust, enables success, and strengthens long-term community relationships.

---

# Vision

Responsive support.

Successful communities.

Trusted partnerships.

---

# Philosophy

Support is more than problem resolution.

Customer success proactively helps communities achieve their goals while support provides timely assistance when challenges arise.

Together they create lasting trust.

---

# Community Care Principles

Community Care shall:

- Listen actively.
- Respond respectfully.
- Communicate clearly.
- Resolve efficiently.
- Educate continuously.
- Follow through consistently.
- Learn from every interaction.

Every conversation matters.

---

# Support Channels

Communities may receive assistance through:

- Knowledge base
- AI Assistant
- Community forums
- Email support
- Live chat
- Scheduled consultation
- Enterprise success managers
- Emergency support

Support remains accessible.

---

# Support Priorities

Support teams prioritize:

- Community impact
- Issue severity
- Time sensitivity
- Accessibility needs
- Security implications
- Service commitments

Prioritization remains transparent.

---

# Customer Success

Customer Success focuses on:

- Community onboarding
- Adoption guidance
- Best practices
- Training
- Health reviews
- Strategic planning
- Success metrics
- Long-term partnerships

Success extends beyond support tickets.

---

# Knowledge Management

Every resolved issue should contribute to:

- Documentation improvements
- FAQ updates
- AI knowledge
- Training materials
- Product improvements
- Community education

Knowledge compounds over time.

# Acceptance Criteria

Community Care succeeds when:

- Communities receive timely assistance.
- Support satisfaction improves.
- Community adoption increases.
- Documentation quality improves.
- Long-term trust grows.
