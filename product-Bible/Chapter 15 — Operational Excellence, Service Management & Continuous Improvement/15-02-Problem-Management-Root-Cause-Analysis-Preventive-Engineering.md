# Purpose

This document defines the framework for identifying recurring operational problems, performing root cause analysis, implementing preventive engineering measures, and continuously reducing operational risk across the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to transform operational experience into long-term platform resilience.

---

# Vision

Fewer recurring incidents.

Better engineering.

Continuous learning.

---

# Philosophy

Problems differ from incidents.

Incidents interrupt service.

Problems explain why incidents occur.

The goal is to eliminate root causes rather than repeatedly treating symptoms.

---

# Problem Lifecycle

Problems follow a structured lifecycle:

1. Identification
2. Investigation
3. Root Cause Analysis
4. Risk Assessment
5. Corrective Actions
6. Preventive Actions
7. Verification
8. Knowledge Capture
9. Closure

Each stage produces actionable outcomes.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Analysis should consider:

- Technical factors
- Human factors
- Process gaps
- Documentation quality
- Monitoring effectiveness
- Training needs
- Environmental conditions

Root cause analysis seeks understanding, not blame.

---

# Preventive Engineering

Preventive improvements may include:

- Automation
- Improved monitoring
- Better testing
- Stronger validation
- Architecture improvements
- Documentation updates
- Operational training
- Simplified workflows

Engineering reduces future risk.

---

# Knowledge Management

Lessons learned shall be documented through:

- Knowledge base articles
- Engineering standards
- Runbook updates
- Design guidelines
- Training materials
- Architecture reviews

Learning becomes organizational knowledge.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-011

Significant recurring incidents shall generate formal problem records.

---

## BR-OE-012

Root cause analysis shall avoid assigning personal blame.

---

## BR-OE-013

Corrective and preventive actions shall be tracked to completion.

---

## BR-OE-014

Operational learning shall update documentation.

---

## BR-OE-015

Preventive engineering shall be prioritized according to risk.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Problem Management succeeds when:

- Recurring incidents decline.
- Root causes are understood.
- Preventive actions are implemented.
- Documentation improves.
- Engineering maturity increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Problem management framework is documented.
- Root cause methodology is established.
- Preventive engineering strategy is defined.
