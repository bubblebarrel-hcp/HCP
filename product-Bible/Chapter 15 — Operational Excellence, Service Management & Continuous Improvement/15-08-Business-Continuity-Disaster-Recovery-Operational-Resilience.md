# Purpose

This document defines the business continuity, disaster recovery (DR), and operational resilience framework for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure that critical services can continue operating, recover quickly from disruptive events, and preserve community trust under adverse conditions.

---

# Vision

Prepared organizations.

Resilient systems.

Confident communities.

---

# Philosophy

Operational resilience is achieved through preparation, redundancy, regular testing, and continuous improvement.

Recovery capabilities shall be verified rather than assumed.

---

# Business Continuity Objectives

The platform shall:

- Maintain critical services.
- Protect community data.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Support alternative operating procedures.
- Enable coordinated recovery.
- Communicate clearly during disruptions.

Continuity planning extends beyond technology.

---

# Disaster Recovery Strategy

Recovery planning shall address:

- Infrastructure failures
- Database failures
- Cloud provider outages
- Network disruptions
- Cybersecurity incidents
- Human error
- Third-party service failures
- Regional disasters

Recovery plans remain documented and tested.

---

# Recovery Objectives

Critical services shall define:

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Recovery priority
- Recovery dependencies
- Recovery owners
- Verification procedures

Objectives guide recovery planning.

---

# Backup Strategy

Backups shall be:

- Automated
- Encrypted
- Versioned
- Geographically separated
- Regularly verified
- Periodically restored for testing

Successful restoration matters more than successful backup creation.

---

# Resilience Engineering

Operational resilience includes:

- Redundancy
- Failover
- Graceful degradation
- Capacity reserves
- Chaos testing
- Recovery automation
- Cross-training
- Runbooks

Resilience is continuously strengthened.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OE-041

Critical services shall define recovery objectives.

---

## BR-OE-042

Backups shall be tested regularly through restoration exercises.

---

## BR-OE-043

Disaster recovery plans shall be reviewed periodically.

---

## BR-OE-044

Recovery exercises shall produce documented lessons learned.

---

## BR-OE-045

Community communication shall remain active during recovery events.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Operational Resilience succeeds when:

- Recovery objectives are consistently met.
- Backup restoration succeeds.
- Communities remain informed.
- Recovery exercises improve preparedness.
- Operational confidence increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Business continuity framework is documented.
- Disaster recovery strategy is established.
- Recovery objectives are defined.
