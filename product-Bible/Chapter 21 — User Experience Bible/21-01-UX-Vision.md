# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision for the user experience of the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

It establishes the principles that guide every interaction, journey, workflow, and service provided by the platform.

The UX Vision ensures that all product decisions contribute toward a coherent, community-first experience.

---

# Vision

HCP should feel less like using software and more like being part of a living community.

Technology should quietly support relationships, participation, and shared experiences without becoming the center of attention.

---

# Experience Mission

Enable every community member to:

- Discover communities.
- Join confidently.
- Participate easily.
- Contribute meaningfully.
- Preserve memories.
- Build lasting relationships.

Every feature should support at least one of these outcomes.

---

# Experience Goals

The platform should enable users to:

- Feel welcomed within minutes.
- Understand their next step without guidance.
- Complete common tasks efficiently.
- Recover easily from mistakes.
- Trust the platform with their information.
- Return because of the community—not because of notifications.

---

# Experience Characteristics

The ideal HCP experience is:

- Simple
- Predictable
- Inclusive
- Reliable
- Human
- Calm
- Fast
- Helpful
- Community-centered

---

# Guiding Questions

Every UX decision should answer:

- Does this reduce effort?
- Does this strengthen relationships?
- Does this remove uncertainty?
- Does this encourage participation?
- Does this respect users' time?
- Does this improve trust?
- Does this remain accessible?

If the answer to several of these questions is "no," the solution should be reconsidered.

---

# User Success Definition

A successful experience is not measured by time spent in the application.

Success is measured by:

- Communities becoming more active.
- Members participating more frequently.
- Volunteers contributing more easily.
- Organizers spending less time on administration.
- Users completing tasks with confidence.

The platform succeeds when people accomplish meaningful goals.

---

# Experience Across Platforms

Whether using:

- Mobile
- Web
- Tablet
- Future desktop clients

Users should recognize the same interaction model, terminology, and design language.

Platform conventions may differ, but the underlying experience remains consistent.

---

# Experience Boundaries

HCP should:

- Help communities organize.
- Preserve community history.
- Facilitate communication.
- Encourage participation.
- Reduce administrative effort.

HCP should not:

- Replace face-to-face interaction.
- Encourage addictive usage patterns.
- Prioritize engagement metrics over community value.
- Overcomplicate simple community activities.

---

# Experience Metrics

Representative UX metrics include:

- Time to first successful action.
- Onboarding completion rate.
- Event participation rate.
- Volunteer participation.
- User retention.
- Task completion rate.
- User satisfaction.
- Accessibility satisfaction.
- Community health indicators.

These metrics should be reviewed regularly to guide product improvements.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-051

Every new feature shall support at least one defined user outcome.

---

## BR-UX-052

User success shall be measured primarily by successful task completion and community participation.

---

## BR-UX-053

Platform engagement metrics shall not outweigh community well-being.

---

## BR-UX-054

User experience decisions shall prioritize simplicity before feature expansion.

---

## BR-UX-055

Cross-platform experiences shall remain functionally consistent.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The UX Vision succeeds when:

- Teams share a common understanding of the desired experience.
- Product decisions consistently reinforce community goals.
- UX metrics improve over successive releases.
- Users complete common tasks with confidence.
- Community participation increases.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- UX vision is documented.
- Experience goals are defined.
- User success metrics are established.
