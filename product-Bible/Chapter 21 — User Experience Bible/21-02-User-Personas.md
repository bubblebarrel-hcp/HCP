# Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Rather than focusing on demographic profiles, HCP models users by the roles they perform, the goals they pursue, and the responsibilities they hold within their communities.

A single person may occupy multiple personas over time.

---

# Vision

Design for what people are trying to accomplish, not who they are.

---

# Persona Philosophy

Personas represent patterns of behavior and responsibility.

Users may transition between personas throughout their journey in HCP.

The platform should support those transitions naturally.

---

# Persona 1 — Visitor

## Goal

Understand what HCP is and decide whether to join.

## Typical Tasks

- Explore communities.
- Learn about hashing.
- Browse public events.
- View public content.

## Success

The visitor confidently creates an account or decides to learn more.

---

# Persona 2 — Community Member

## Goal

Participate in community activities.

## Typical Tasks

- Join events.
- View trails.
- Upload photos.
- Comment.
- React.
- Connect with other members.

## Success

The member participates regularly with minimal administrative effort.

---

# Persona 3 — Hare

## Goal

Create engaging trail experiences.

## Typical Tasks

- Plan trails.
- Publish trail information.
- Upload maps.
- Coordinate logistics.
- Share updates.

## Success

Participants can easily find and enjoy the planned experience.

---

# Persona 4 — Scribe

## Goal

Preserve community history.

## Typical Tasks

- Write trail reports.
- Edit AI-assisted drafts.
- Publish event summaries.
- Attach media.
- Archive memorable moments.

## Success

Community memories remain accurate, engaging, and searchable.

---

# Persona 5 — Volunteer

## Goal

Support community operations.

## Typical Tasks

- Assist organizers.
- Check in participants.
- Coordinate activities.
- Help during events.

## Success

Operational tasks become easier and more reliable.

---

# Persona 6 — Committee Member

## Goal

Manage community administration.

## Typical Tasks

- Review requests.
- Manage roles.
- Approve events.
- Moderate content.
- Communicate with members.

## Success

Administrative effort decreases while transparency increases.

---

# Persona 7 — Community Administrator

## Goal

Operate the community effectively.

## Typical Tasks

- Configure settings.
- Manage permissions.
- Oversee governance.
- Review analytics.
- Maintain community information.

## Success

The community remains healthy, active, and well-managed.

---

# Persona 8 — Platform Administrator

## Goal

Maintain the overall HCP ecosystem.

## Typical Tasks

- Monitor system health.
- Review reports.
- Manage platform policies.
- Support communities.
- Resolve escalated issues.

## Success

The platform remains reliable, secure, and trusted.

---

# Persona Transitions

Users may progress naturally.

Example progression:

Visitor

↓

Member

↓

Volunteer

↓

Hare

↓

Scribe

↓

Committee Member

↓

Community Administrator

Transitions should require minimal friction.

---

# Shared Needs

Every persona values:

- Simplicity.
- Reliability.
- Trust.
- Community.
- Accessibility.
- Fast interactions.
- Clear communication.

---

# Experience Expectations

Regardless of role, users should:

- Understand their next step.
- Know their responsibilities.
- Recover from mistakes easily.
- Receive meaningful feedback.
- Feel welcomed.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-UX-056

The platform shall support multiple personas per account.

---

## BR-UX-057

Role transitions shall preserve user identity and history.

---

## BR-UX-058

Interfaces shall adapt to responsibilities without overwhelming users.

---

## BR-UX-059

Permissions shall be determined by assigned roles rather than separate accounts.

---

## BR-UX-060

Common tasks shall remain consistent across personas where practical.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The persona framework succeeds when:

- Product decisions align with user goals.
- Role transitions remain intuitive.
- Common workflows remain efficient.
- Permissions remain understandable.
- Users recognize their responsibilities.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Personas are documented.
- Goals are defined.
- Role transitions are established.
