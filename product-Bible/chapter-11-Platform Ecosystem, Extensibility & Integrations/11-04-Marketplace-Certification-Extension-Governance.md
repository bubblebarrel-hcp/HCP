# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for the HCP Extension Marketplace, including certification, review processes, trust levels, lifecycle management, and ongoing compliance for community extensions.

Its purpose is to foster innovation while protecting community security, privacy, and trust.

---

# Vision

Open innovation.

Responsible governance.

Trusted extensions.

---

# Philosophy

Marketplace participation is a privilege earned through transparency, quality, and ongoing accountability.

---

# Marketplace Objectives

Provide:

- Discoverability
- Certification
- Trust indicators
- Compatibility information
- Security reviews
- Governance oversight
- Continuous monitoring

---

# Extension Categories

Marketplace extensions may include:

- Official HCP extensions
- Bubble Barrel extensions
- Community-developed extensions
- Commercial partner extensions
- Academic and research tools
- Regional organization packages

Each category follows appropriate governance requirements.

---

# Certification Levels

Extensions may achieve:

### Community Verified

Basic quality and compatibility checks.

---

### Security Reviewed

Passed technical security review.

---

### Governance Approved

Complies with organizational governance standards.

---

### Heritage Certified

Approved for historical preservation workflows.

---

### Enterprise Ready

Supports large-scale organizational deployment.

Certification status remains visible.

---

# Marketplace Information

Each extension displays:

- Publisher
- Version
- Permissions
- Compatibility
- Certification level
- Changelog
- Security review date
- Privacy declaration
- Community feedback
- Installation count

Transparency supports informed decisions.

---

# Continuous Compliance

The platform regularly evaluates:

- Security vulnerabilities
- Compatibility
- Policy compliance
- User feedback
- Performance
- Stability

Certification may change over time.

---

# Lifecycle Management

Extensions may be:

- Published
- Updated
- Deprecated
- Suspended
- Archived
- Retired

Lifecycle events remain auditable.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-PE-016

Marketplace listings shall disclose requested permissions.

---

## BR-PE-017

Certification status shall remain publicly visible.

---

## BR-PE-018

Extensions may be suspended for significant security or governance concerns.

---

## BR-PE-019

Marketplace information shall remain transparent.

---

## BR-PE-020

Continuous compliance reviews shall be supported.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Marketplace succeeds when:

- Administrators understand extension trustworthiness.
- Developers understand certification expectations.
- Communities install extensions confidently.
- Governance remains effective.
- Innovation continues responsibly.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Marketplace governance is documented.
- Certification framework is established.
- Continuous compliance model is defined.
