# Purpose

This document establishes the long-term evolution strategy for HCP, ensuring the platform remains adaptable, trustworthy, and sustainable while preserving compatibility, community identity, and institutional knowledge.

---

# Vision

Build for decades.

Evolve with purpose.

Preserve what matters.

---

# Philosophy

Every platform changes.

The challenge is changing without losing identity.

Evolution should strengthen the platform while respecting community trust and historical continuity.

---

# Evolution Principles

- Community-first decisions.
- Backward compatibility where practical.
- Incremental modernization.
- Stable foundations.
- Transparent communication.
- Evidence-based improvements.
- Long-term stewardship.
