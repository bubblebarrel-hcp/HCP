# Purpose

This document defines the operational principles, observability standards, reliability objectives, and continuous improvement practices that ensure HCP remains dependable, resilient, and maintainable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Vision

Reliable communities.

Observable systems.

Continuous excellence.

---

# Philosophy

Operational excellence is not an emergency response.

It is a daily practice.

The platform continuously measures, learns, and improves.

---

# Experience Principles

- Reliability first.
- Observe everything meaningful.
- Detect issues early.
- Recover gracefully.
- Learn continuously.
- Automate responsibly.
- Measure outcomes.

---

# Observability

The platform continuously monitors:

- System health
- API performance
- Plugin execution
- AI services
- Event processing
- Search performance
- Background jobs
- Security events
- Data synchronization
- Infrastructure resources

Monitoring supports proactive operations.

---

# Reliability Objectives

The platform defines objectives for:

- Availability
- Response times
- Error rates
- Recovery time
- Recovery point
- Data durability
- Service continuity

Objectives are reviewed periodically.

---

# Incident Management

Operational capabilities include:

- Incident detection
- Alerting
- Classification
- Escalation
- Communication
- Resolution tracking
- Root cause analysis
- Post-incident reviews

Learning follows every incident.

---

# Operational Metrics

The platform tracks:

- Service health
- User experience
- Automation reliability
- AI quality
- Plugin stability
- Infrastructure utilization
- Deployment success
- Backup integrity

Metrics support continuous improvement.

---

# Disaster Recovery

The platform maintains:

- Verified backups
- Recovery procedures
- Failover strategies
- Restoration testing
- Data validation
- Continuity planning

Preparedness is continuously verified.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-PE-031

## BR-PE-032

---

## BR-PE-033

---

## BR-PE-034

---

## BR-PE-035

# Acceptance Criteria

Operational Excellence succeeds when:

- Issues are detected early.
- Recovery is reliable.
- Platform health remains measurable.
- Learning follows incidents.
- Community trust is maintained.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Operational philosophy is documented.
- Reliability framework is defined.
- Observability model is established.
