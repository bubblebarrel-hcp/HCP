# Purpose

The Identity Platform provides secure authentication, identity federation, and cross-community access while preserving a member's lifelong community identity, governance roles, and participation history.

---

# Vision

One identity.

Many communities.

One continuous journey.

---

# Philosophy

A member's identity belongs to the individual.

Organizations steward roles and permissions.

The platform connects both without conflating them.

---

# Experience Principles

- Identity portability.
- Secure authentication.
- Federation by design.
- Privacy first.
- Governance-aware authorization.
- Minimal friction.
- Lifelong continuity.

---

# Identity Model

Each member possesses:

- Global Member Identity
- Community Passport
- Membership history
- Leadership history
- Recognition history
- Verified participation record

Identity persists across organizational changes.

---

# Authentication

Supported authentication methods may include:

- Email & password
- Passkeys (WebAuthn)
- OAuth providers
- Enterprise identity providers
- Multi-factor authentication
- Future authentication standards

Authentication mechanisms may evolve independently of identity.

---

# Federation

The platform supports identity federation with:

- Approved community organizations
- Enterprise identity providers
- Educational institutions
- Future federation partners

Federation respects governance boundaries.

---

# Authorization

Access decisions consider:

- Member role
- Organizational role
- Chapter membership
- Regional permissions
- Committee assignments
- Temporary delegations
- Historical governance rules

Permissions remain contextual.

---

# Cross-Community Participation

Members may:

- Join multiple organizations.
- Participate in international events.
- Hold roles in multiple chapters where permitted.
- Preserve one continuous participation history.

Identity remains unified.

---

# Privacy

Members control:

- Public profile visibility.
- Discoverability.
- Shared identity attributes.
- Federation preferences.
- Linked accounts.

Privacy controls remain transparent.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-PE-021

Identity remains independent of organizational membership.

---

## BR-PE-022

Authentication providers shall remain replaceable.

---

## BR-PE-023

Authorization decisions shall respect governance structures.

---

## BR-PE-024

Cross-community participation shall preserve a unified identity.

---

## BR-PE-025

Identity history shall remain portable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Identity Platform succeeds when members can:

- Authenticate securely.
- Move between communities seamlessly.
- Retain lifelong participation history.
- Control identity sharing.
- Trust access controls.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Identity architecture is documented.
- Federation model is defined.
- Authorization philosophy is established.
