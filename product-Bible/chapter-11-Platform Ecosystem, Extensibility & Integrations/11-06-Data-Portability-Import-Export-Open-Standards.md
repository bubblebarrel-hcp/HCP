# Purpose

The Data Portability Platform ensures that communities can import, export, preserve, and migrate their data using transparent, documented, and interoperable standards.

Its purpose is to protect long-term community ownership while supporting platform evolution, disaster recovery, historical preservation, and interoperability.

---

# Vision

Community-owned data.

Portable knowledge.

Open futures.

---

# Philosophy

The platform earns loyalty through value, not through data lock-in.

Communities retain ownership of their records.

---

# Experience Principles

- Open standards.
- Transparent exports.
- Reliable imports.
- Preservation-first.
- Metadata retention.
- Explainable migrations.
- Future compatibility.

---

# Supported Data Domains

Import and export may include:

- Members
- Organizations
- Events
- Community Passports
- Run Capsules
- Historical archives
- Media metadata
- Recognition records
- Governance records
- Knowledge graphs
- AI-generated content (clearly identified)
- Audit logs (where appropriate)

---

# Export Formats

Supported formats may include:

- JSON
- CSV
- PDF reports
- Markdown
- XML
- Media archives
- Future standardized community packages

Formats remain documented.

---

# Import Services

The platform supports importing from:

- Legacy HCP versions
- Spreadsheets
- Approved third-party systems
- Community archives
- Digitized historical collections
- Standardized interchange packages

Imports preserve provenance where possible.

---

# Metadata Preservation

Exports retain:

- Timestamps
- Attribution
- Version history
- Evidence links
- Relationships
- Verification status
- Governance context

Context is preserved alongside content.

---

# Open Standards

The platform promotes:

- Documented schemas
- Versioned formats
- Published specifications
- Backward compatibility where practical
- Community review of standards

Interoperability is encouraged.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-PE-026

Communities shall be able to export their authorized data.

---

## BR-PE-027

Exports shall preserve metadata where possible.

---

## BR-PE-028

AI-generated content shall remain distinguishable.

---

## BR-PE-029

Import processes shall preserve provenance when available.

---

## BR-PE-030

Published data formats shall remain documented.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Data Portability Platform succeeds when communities can:

- Move their data confidently.
- Preserve historical context.
- Restore archived information.
- Integrate with external systems.
- Trust long-term ownership.

# Completion Criteria

- Data portability philosophy is documented.
- Import/export strategy is defined.
- Open standards approach is established.
