# Purpose

This document defines the extension architecture, plugin framework, marketplace governance, security principles, and lifecycle management for third-party capabilities within the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Extensions expand platform functionality while preserving stability, trust, and long-term compatibility.

---

# Vision

A secure, thriving ecosystem where communities can confidently discover, install, manage, and build trusted extensions.

---

# Philosophy

The core platform remains intentionally focused.

Extensions enable specialization without increasing unnecessary complexity for every community.

Every extension should uphold the ethical and technical standards of HCP.

---

# Extension Principles

Extensions shall:

- Be isolated from the core platform.
- Use documented APIs.
- Request only necessary permissions.
- Respect community privacy.
- Remain version-aware.
- Support graceful failure.
- Be removable without damaging community data.

---

# Plugin Categories

Examples include:

- Community Operations
- Historical Preservation
- Education & Learning
- Accessibility
- Communications
- Regional Integrations
- Analytics
- AI Assistants
- Finance
- Volunteer Management
- Cultural Programs
- Research Tools

Categories evolve responsibly.

---

# Marketplace Principles

The marketplace shall:

- Encourage quality.
- Promote transparency.
- Support discoverability.
- Clearly display permissions.
- Explain data access.
- Surface compatibility.
- Enable community reviews.

Trust remains central.

---

# Extension Lifecycle

Lifecycle stages include:

1. Development
2. Validation
3. Security Review
4. Marketplace Publication
5. Updates
6. Monitoring
7. Deprecation
8. Retirement

Lifecycle management remains transparent.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-PE-006

Extensions shall operate within documented permission boundaries.

---

## BR-PE-007

Marketplace listings shall disclose requested capabilities.

## BR-PE-008

Communities retain control over installed extensions.

---

## BR-PE-009

---

## BR-PE-010
