# Purpose

The Recognition & Appreciation Platform celebrates meaningful contributions made by members while preserving authenticity, fairness, and the values of the Hash community.

Recognition exists to strengthen belonging, encourage participation, and preserve the stories behind community service.

---

# Vision

Celebrate people.

Honor service.

Preserve legacy.

---

# Philosophy

Recognition is gratitude made visible.

Achievements represent contribution—not status.

Every member should have the opportunity to be appreciated.

---

# Experience Principles

- Celebrate contribution.
- Avoid unhealthy competition.
- Preserve authenticity.
- Encourage gratitude.
- Recognize service at every level.
- Value consistency over popularity.
- Tell the story behind recognition.

---

# Recognition Types

The platform supports:

- Thank You messages
- Volunteer recognition
- Event appreciation
- Leadership recognition
- Milestone celebrations
- Community service awards
- Historical preservation awards
- Mentorship recognition
- International participation
- Lifetime achievements

---

# Achievements

Achievements may recognize:

- First Run
- Volunteer milestones
- Organizer milestones
- Trail Master milestones
- International events
- Years of membership
- Historical contributions
- Community education
- Evidence verification
- Mentorship

Achievements emphasize contribution rather than accumulation.

---

# Appreciation

Members may:

- Thank another member
- Recognize volunteers
- Celebrate organizers
- Highlight mentors
- Share stories of kindness
- Recommend awards

Appreciation remains personal and sincere.

---

# Award Narratives

Every major award includes:

- Why it was awarded
- Supporting evidence
- Community impact
- Related events
- Photos
- Stories
- Historical context

Awards become lasting historical records.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- Suggesting overlooked contributors
- Identifying volunteer impact
- Drafting appreciation messages
- Summarizing years of service
- Recommending award candidates

AI never grants awards autonomously.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-016

Recognition shall never be based solely on activity volume.

---

## BR-CXP-017

Major awards require human governance.

---

## BR-CXP-018

Recognition shall remain explainable and evidence-backed.

---

## BR-CXP-019

Members may decline public recognition where appropriate.

---

## BR-CXP-020

Recognition should encourage future contribution.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Recognition succeeds when members feel:

- Appreciated.
- Encouraged.
- Connected.
- Inspired.
- Respected.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Recognition framework is documented.
- Achievement philosophy is defined.
- Appreciation experience is established.
