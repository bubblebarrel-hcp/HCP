# Purpose

The Community Feed provides members with timely, relevant, and meaningful updates from across their organizations and the wider Hash community.

Unlike traditional social feeds, the Community Feed prioritizes participation, recognition, discovery, and institutional memory over passive engagement.

---

# Vision

Keep members connected.

Celebrate participation.

Encourage contribution.

---

# Philosophy

Attention is valuable.

Trust is more valuable.

The platform should never manipulate attention for engagement.

---

# Experience Principles

- Relevance over recency.
- Community before popularity.
- Encourage participation.
- Reduce information overload.
- Celebrate achievements.
- Explain recommendations.
- Respect member preferences.

---

# Feed Content

The feed may include:

- New Run Capsules
- Upcoming events
- Volunteer opportunities
- Leadership announcements
- Recognition
- Historical memories
- Community milestones
- New members
- Governance updates
- Safety notices
- Chapter anniversaries

---

# Discovery

Members discover:

- Nearby chapters
- Upcoming events
- International events
- Historic Run Capsules
- Volunteer opportunities
- New organizations
- Interest groups

Discovery adapts to member preferences.

---

# Social Interaction

Members may:

- React
- Comment
- Thank volunteers
- Welcome new members
- Share memories
- Recommend events
- Save posts
- Follow organizations

Interactions emphasize encouragement rather than competition.

---

# Intelligent Ranking

Ranking considers:

- Relevance
- Community relationship
- Upcoming commitments
- Organization membership
- Participation history
- Freshness
- Member preferences

Popularity alone shall never determine visibility.

---

# Historical Discovery

The platform may surface:

- "On this day"
- Historic anniversaries
- Classic Run Capsules
- Legacy members
- Organization milestones

Historical content strengthens institutional memory.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may:

- Summarize busy periods
- Highlight important updates
- Recommend events
- Identify community trends
- Suggest members to reconnect with

AI recommendations remain explainable.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-011

Emergency and safety information supersedes routine feed content.

---

## BR-CXP-012

Algorithms shall prioritize meaningful participation over engagement metrics.

---

## BR-CXP-013

Recommendations shall remain explainable.

---

## BR-CXP-014

Members retain meaningful control over feed preferences.

---

## BR-CXP-015

Historical preservation shall remain a core feed objective.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Community Feed succeeds when members can:

- Stay informed.
- Feel connected.
- Discover opportunities.
- Celebrate others.
- Reconnect with community history.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Feed philosophy is documented.
- Discovery model is defined.
- Social interaction principles are established.
