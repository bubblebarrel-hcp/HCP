# Purpose

This document establishes accessibility, internationalization, localization, cultural adaptability, and inclusive design principles for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

Its objective is to ensure that every member can participate confidently regardless of language, ability, location, device, or cultural background.

---

# Vision

Every member belongs.

Every culture is respected.

Every interaction is inclusive.

---

# Philosophy

Accessibility is a requirement.

Localization is respect.

Inclusion is intentional.

---

# Experience Principles

- Design for everyone.
- Accessibility by default.
- Mobile-first experience.
- Progressive enhancement.
- Cultural neutrality where appropriate.
- Local relevance where valuable.
- Human dignity above convenience.

---

# Accessibility

The platform supports:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- High-contrast themes
- Adjustable text sizes
- Reduced motion
- Accessible media alternatives
- Focus indicators
- Semantic navigation

Accessibility is integrated into every feature rather than added later.

---

# Internationalization

The platform supports:

- Multiple languages
- Multiple scripts
- Locale-aware formatting
- Right-to-left languages
- Time zone awareness
- Regional calendars (where applicable)
- Currency formatting (where relevant)

Platform text remains translatable.

---

# Localization

Organizations may customize:

- Terminology
- Event naming
- Local traditions
- Date formats
- Regional preferences
- Volunteer terminology
- Recognition wording

Localization preserves local identity.

---

# Inclusive Design

Design considers:

- New members
- Long-term members
- Elder members
- Members with disabilities
- Volunteers
- Leaders
- International visitors

Interfaces remain understandable across experience levels.

---

# Offline Experience

Where feasible, members may:

- View downloaded Run Capsules
- Access Passport records
- Review event details
- Capture evidence for later synchronization
- Read organizational history

Connectivity should not unnecessarily prevent participation.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-026

Accessibility shall be considered during initial feature design.

---

## BR-CXP-027

Localization shall preserve organizational traditions.

---

## BR-CXP-028

Language preferences shall remain member-controlled.

---

## BR-CXP-029

Offline functionality shall prioritize high-value community interactions.

---

## BR-CXP-030

Inclusive design shall remain measurable throughout product development.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Inclusive Experience succeeds when members can participate confidently regardless of:

- Ability
- Language
- Culture
- Geography
- Device
- Connectivity

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Accessibility standards are documented.
- Internationalization strategy is defined.
- Inclusive design principles are established.
