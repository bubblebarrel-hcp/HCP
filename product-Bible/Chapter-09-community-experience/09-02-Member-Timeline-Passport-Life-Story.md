# Purpose

The Member Timeline and Passport experience preserves a member's journey through the Hash Community Platform (HCP), combining verified achievements with meaningful moments to create a rich, trustworthy, and inspiring record of participation.

The Timeline emphasizes personal narrative, while the Passport provides official community records.

---

# Vision

Every contribution matters.

Every memory belongs somewhere.

Every journey deserves to be remembered.

---

# Philosophy

The platform should help members see not only what they have done, but how they have grown within the community.

---

# Experience Principles

- Celebrate milestones.
- Preserve memories.
- Separate official records from personal reflections.
- Encourage storytelling.
- Highlight contribution.
- Respect privacy.
- Inspire future participation.

---

# Timeline

The Timeline includes:

- Joined community
- First run
- Volunteer activities
- Leadership appointments
- Awards
- Photos
- Videos
- Run Capsules
- Governance participation
- Historical memories
- AI-generated highlights
- Personal reflections (optional)

Timeline entries may combine verified and personal content with clear visual distinction.

---

# Passport

The Passport contains official records including:

- Membership history
- Verified runs
- Official stamps
- Awards
- Offices held
- Certifications
- Verified volunteer service
- International participation

Passport entries are evidence-backed and immutable except through authorized correction workflows.

---

# Memory Collections

Members may organize memories into collections such as:

- First Experiences
- Favorite Trails
- International Events
- Volunteer Highlights
- Leadership Journey
- Historic Moments
- Personal Albums

Collections support photos, videos, documents, and narrative text.

---

# AI Storytelling

AI may generate:

- Annual summaries
- Milestone retrospectives
- Leadership highlights
- Volunteer impact summaries
- Travel history
- Community contribution reports

Members control whether AI-generated stories appear publicly.

---

# Reflection

Members may privately record:

- Lessons learned
- Personal goals
- Community reflections
- Event notes

Reflections remain private unless intentionally shared.

---

# Privacy

Members control visibility of:

- Timeline entries
- Photos
- Albums
- Reflections
- Awards
- Passport elements

Visibility integrates with platform privacy settings.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-006

Official Passport records remain distinct from personal storytelling.

---

## BR-CXP-007

Verified activities display supporting evidence where appropriate.

---

## BR-CXP-008

AI-generated narratives clearly indicate automated authorship.

---

## BR-CXP-009

Historical memories shall preserve original context.

---

## BR-CXP-010

Members remain owners of their personal narrative.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The experience succeeds when members can:

- Revisit meaningful moments.
- Verify official achievements.
- Share selected memories.
- Reflect on personal growth.
- Preserve community history.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Timeline model is documented.
- Passport relationship is defined.
- Storytelling experience is established.
