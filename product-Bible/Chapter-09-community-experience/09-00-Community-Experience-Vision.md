# Purpose

The Community Experience Platform defines how members, leaders, volunteers, and organizations experience the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

It establishes the interaction philosophy, experience principles, navigation model, engagement patterns, and human-centered design approach that guide every user-facing feature.

---

# Vision

Every interaction should strengthen belonging.

Every screen should encourage contribution.

Every journey should preserve community.

---

# Philosophy

Communities are built by people.

Software should help people connect, contribute, celebrate, and remember.

The platform should disappear behind the experience.

- Trusted

# Primary Personas

The platform serves:

- New Members
- Returning Members
- Volunteers
- Event Organizers
- Committee Members
- Executives
- Administrators
- Guests

---

# Platform Promise

HCP helps every member answer:

- What is happening?
- Where can I help?
- What have I achieved?
- Who can I connect with?
- What matters to my community today?

---

# Success Criteria

The experience succeeds when:

- Members quickly understand the platform.
- Important actions require minimal effort.
- Contributions are visible.
- Community participation increases.
- Institutional memory becomes discoverable.
