# Purpose

This document defines the mobile-first interaction model, offline capabilities, synchronization strategy, and future interaction paradigms for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure that members can participate naturally wherever community life happens.

---

# Vision

Carry the community everywhere.

Participate anywhere.

Reconnect automatically.

---

# Philosophy

The platform should adapt to the realities of community life.

Connectivity is beneficial, but participation should not depend on it.

---

# Experience Principles

- Mobile-first.
- Offline-capable.
- Fast by default.
- Battery-conscious.
- Context-aware.
- Seamless synchronization.
- Future-ready interaction models.

---

# Mobile Experience

The platform prioritizes:

- Thumb-friendly navigation
- One-handed interaction
- Minimal typing
- Camera-first workflows
- Fast startup
- Responsive layouts
- Native device capabilities

---

# Offline Capabilities

Members may:

- View downloaded Run Capsules
- Access Passport summaries
- View event information
- Capture photos
- Record GPS trails
- Draft reflections
- Prepare volunteer checklists
- Queue evidence for upload

Changes synchronize automatically when connectivity returns.

---

# Synchronization

Synchronization shall provide:

- Background uploads
- Conflict detection
- Conflict resolution guidance
- Incremental synchronization
- Retry mechanisms
- Synchronization history

Members remain informed of synchronization status.

---

# Device Integration

The platform may integrate with:

- Camera
- GPS
- Calendar
- Contacts (with permission)
- Biometric authentication
- Health platforms (future)
- Wearable devices (future)

Integrations remain optional and permission-based.

---

# Future Interfaces

The architecture should support future interaction methods including:

- Voice assistance
- Wearables
- Smart glasses
- Ambient notifications
- AI companions
- Spatial computing
- Emerging interaction technologies

Core business logic remains independent of interface technology.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-031

Critical community workflows shall remain usable offline where feasible.

---

## BR-CXP-032

Synchronization shall preserve data integrity.

---

## BR-CXP-033

Device permissions shall remain transparent and revocable.

---

## BR-CXP-034

Future interfaces shall reuse core platform capabilities rather than duplicate business logic.

---

## BR-CXP-035

Mobile interactions shall prioritize speed and simplicity.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Mobile Experience succeeds when members can:

- Participate during events.
- Continue working offline.
- Synchronize confidently.
- Trust device integrations.
- Transition smoothly across devices.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Mobile-first philosophy is established.
- Offline behavior is documented.
- Synchronization strategy is defined.
- Future interaction model is articulated.
