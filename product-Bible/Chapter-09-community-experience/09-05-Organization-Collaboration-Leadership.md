# Purpose

The Organization Experience provides clubs, chapters, kennels, regional bodies, committees, and international organizations with collaborative workspaces that support leadership, coordination, institutional memory, and community growth.

Organizations are treated as living communities with evolving histories rather than static administrative entities.

---

# Vision

Empower leaders.

Connect members.

Preserve organizations.

---

# Philosophy

Healthy organizations create healthy communities.

Leadership should be supported, not burdened.

Institutional memory should survive leadership transitions.

---

# Experience Principles

- Shared ownership.
- Transparent leadership.
- Collaborative planning.
- Knowledge continuity.
- Respect for tradition.
- Inclusive participation.
- Context-aware experiences.

---

# Organization Home

Each organization includes:

- Welcome banner
- Upcoming events
- Community Pulse
- Announcements
- Leadership team
- Recent Run Capsules
- Volunteer opportunities
- Historical highlights
- Current initiatives

---

# Leadership Workspace

Leaders access:

- Operational dashboard
- Pending approvals
- Event planning
- Volunteer coordination
- Membership insights
- Governance actions
- Organization health
- AI assistance

Leadership tools adapt to assigned responsibilities.

---

# Collaboration

Organizations support:

- Working groups
- Committees
- Shared documents
- Planning boards
- Task coordination
- Meeting records
- Decision tracking
- Evidence collection

Collaboration preserves organizational knowledge.

---

# Community Health

Organizations view indicators including:

- Member participation
- Volunteer engagement
- Event cadence
- Leadership continuity
- Membership growth
- Historical preservation
- Recognition activity

Health indicators guide improvement rather than competition.

---

# Leadership Continuity

The platform preserves:

- Handover notes
- Strategic plans
- Historical decisions
- Lessons learned
- Committee archives
- Annual reports

Leadership transitions retain institutional memory.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- Summarizing meetings
- Drafting announcements
- Identifying volunteer gaps
- Suggesting recognition
- Highlighting governance deadlines
- Recommending historical references

Human leaders remain responsible for decisions.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-021

Organizations retain ownership of their institutional history.

---

## BR-CXP-022

Leadership transitions shall preserve operational continuity.

---

## BR-CXP-023

Community health indicators shall encourage improvement rather than ranking.

---

## BR-CXP-024

Collaboration records become part of organizational memory where appropriate.

---

## BR-CXP-025

Leadership tools shall remain understandable for volunteers, not just technical administrators.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Organization Experience succeeds when leaders can:

- Coordinate effectively.
- Preserve knowledge.
- Welcome new volunteers.
- Plan confidently.
- Sustain community growth.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Organization workspace is defined.
- Collaboration model is documented.
- Leadership experience is established.
