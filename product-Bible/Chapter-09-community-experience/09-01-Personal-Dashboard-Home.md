# Purpose

The Personal Dashboard (Community Home) provides every member with a personalized, contextual, and action-oriented view of their community life.

Rather than presenting raw information, it helps members understand where they are, what is happening, what deserves attention, and how they can contribute.

---

# Vision

One screen.

One community.

Everything that matters.

---

# Philosophy

Show less.

Mean more.

Help members act with confidence.

---

# Experience Principles

- Context over clutter.
- Personal relevance.
- Community awareness.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Recognition of contribution.
- Action before information.
- Delight through simplicity.

# Personalization

The Home adapts based on:

- Role
- Organization
- Participation history
- Upcoming commitments
- Interests
- Time zone
- Device
- Accessibility preferences

---

# Business Rules

## BR-CXP-001

Important community events take precedence over promotional content.

---

## BR-CXP-002

Community contribution should be celebrated naturally without excessive gamification.

---

## BR-CXP-003

Urgent tasks appear before informational content.

---

## BR-CXP-004

Historical memories should strengthen community identity.

---

## BR-CXP-005

The Home should adapt continuously without becoming unpredictable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Community Home succeeds when members can immediately answer:

- What is happening?
- What needs my attention?
- What have I accomplished?
- How can I help?
- What should I do next?

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Home experience is defined.
- Personalization rules are documented.
- Navigation priorities are established.
