# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) maturity model, operational review process, and future evolution strategy for the Hash Community Platform (HCP).

The objective is to ensure operational excellence scales alongside the platform through structured governance, measurable reliability objectives, and continuous organizational improvement.

---

# Vision

Reliable platforms.

Mature operations.

Continuous evolution.

---

# Philosophy

Operational excellence is not a destination but a continuous improvement process.

Processes, tooling, and organizational structures should evolve as the platform grows.

---

# Strategic Objectives

Operations Governance shall:

- Standardize operational practices.
- Measure reliability.
- Improve engineering efficiency.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Promote cross-team collaboration.
- Enable organizational learning.
- Support long-term scalability.

Governance provides consistency without unnecessary bureaucracy.

---

# Governance Model

Operational governance includes:

- Reliability standards.
- Service ownership.
- Operational reviews.
- Change governance.
- Architecture reviews.
- Capacity reviews.
- Security reviews.
- Incident reviews.

Responsibilities remain clearly defined.

---

# SRE Principles

Representative SRE practices include:

- Service Level Indicators (SLIs).
- Service Level Objectives (SLOs).
- Error budgets.
- Automation.
- Blameless postmortems.
- Reliability engineering.
- Operational metrics.

Reliability should be measurable.

---

# Operational Reviews

Recurring reviews should include:

- Weekly operational review.
- Monthly reliability review.
- Quarterly architecture review.
- Capacity planning review.
- Disaster recovery review.
- Security posture review.
- Cost optimization review.

Regular reviews promote continuous improvement.

---

# Service Ownership

Every critical service shall define:

- Service owner.
- Technical owner.
- Operational owner.
- Documentation owner.
- Escalation path.
- Review cadence.

Ownership drives accountability.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Improvement activities include:

- Automation initiatives.
- Technical debt reduction.
- Runbook refinement.
- Tooling enhancements.
- Reliability improvements.
- Process optimization.
- Knowledge sharing.

Operational maturity grows incrementally.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-OPS-046

Every critical service shall define measurable reliability objectives.

---

## BR-OPS-047

Operational governance reviews shall occur regularly.

---

## BR-OPS-048

Blameless postmortems shall support organizational learning.

---

## BR-OPS-049

Service ownership shall remain documented.

---

## BR-OPS-050

Operational maturity shall be periodically assessed.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Operations Governance succeeds when:

- Reliability improves.
- Operational consistency increases.
- Engineering efficiency improves.
- Organizational learning accelerates.
- Customer trust strengthens.

---

# Completion Criteria

Complete when:

- Governance framework is documented.
- SRE maturity model is established.
- Operational review cadence is defined.
